165 features

Everything in Airline Manager Pro

The full list of what's in the app today, on Android and Windows. New features are added here as they arrive.

Platform2

  • Android app, landscape only (phones and tablets)
  • Windows version: the whole app in its own window from the PC app, connected straight to the sim; window, borderless, full screen; any screen size including ultrawide; graphics card and sound output choice

Linking with the simulator9

  • PC app (Windows) that reads the simulator through SimConnect, no SDK needed
  • Link over the same Wi-Fi, discovery, 6-digit pairing, auto-reconnect
  • Link over mobile data: the PC joins the phone's hotspot (or USB tethering)
  • Pairing a PC with a code
  • PC app log file; errors keep the window open with the details
  • Sim check: reads everything the app uses from the running simulator and the loaded aircraft and saves a report of anything missing or refused
  • Flight recordings: every flight saved as the simulator sent it, played back to the phone or through the tests
  • Detect which aircraft is loaded in the sim and match it to the aircraft you're flying
  • Passenger numbers from GSX Pro (and the sim's payload stations without GSX)

Companies and pilots14

  • Your own pilot profile, synced with your simulator flights
  • Several companies under one pilot profile, or a separate pilot profile per company (your choice)
  • Divisions inside a company with their own fleets, sharing money and reputation
  • Airlines, regional/cargo and bush outfits (VFR, IFR, small props) running in parallel
  • Quick animated company and division switcher
  • Hire pilots, assign routes and rules (duty time, landing limit, IFR only, return to base)
  • Watch hired pilots' flights live on the world map
  • Hired pilots fly in the background, even with the app closed: landings, turnarounds, next legs on their route, rest after the duty limit, revenue, fuel and fees booked; "While you were away" summary on opening
  • Real-world flight times per aircraft and route: airway distance, taxi times (longer at big hubs), climb, cruise and descent speeds at each altitude, cruise level by distance and direction, jet stream
  • Live countdown on flight cards: phase, altitude, miles to go along the route, time to the gate
  • Virtual pilot speed: real time (default), 2×, 4×, 10× or 30× Setting
  • Phone notification when a hired pilot reaches the gate, also with the app closed Setting
  • You can't fly hired pilots' aircraft
  • You fly one aircraft at a time, chosen from your fleet

Overview widgets5

  • Fleet size (aircraft owned)
  • Active flights
  • Fleet availability in percent
  • Profit: daily, weekly, monthly, yearly (all years flown)
  • Fleet status per aircraft: in flight (green), available (blue), maintenance (red)

Fleet and market9

  • Detailed fleet cards with an original illustration per aircraft type in company colours
  • Buy aircraft from brands installed in the sim (Airbus, Boeing, Bombardier CRJ, ATR, de Havilland Canada, Cessna, Daher, Pilatus …)
  • Add-on aircraft (PMDG, Fenix, iniBuilds, Aerosoft, SimWorks Studios …) Found by the pc app in the simulator's package folders
  • Buy several aircraft, including several identical ones
  • Purchase sound, vibration and delivery animation
  • Sell aircraft to a broker at market value (age, condition) less a 6% fee
  • Used-aircraft market, new every Monday: age, hours, cycles, condition, ferry flight
  • Cabin layouts (first, business, economy) and freighter conversions
  • Only the owner buys, sells and leases aircraft; hired pilots never do

Routes and schedule9

  • Routes to any country, departure and destination
  • Multi-stop routes (3, 4, 5 or more stops a day)
  • Your day's legs and a timeline of every aircraft
  • Your own fares per route for economy, business and first class
  • Demand by airport size, distance, season, fares, popularity and marketing
  • Competing airlines on routes, with their own fares and quality
  • Fuel prices per airport and day; tankering
  • Job board: charters and cargo jobs, flown by you or a hired pilot
  • Tours and a weekly challenge

Flight logs13

  • Flight hours, landings, landing rate and G per flight
  • Crashes: choose to register or disregard
  • Flight replay: track on a map, altitude and speed chart, take-off, top of climb, top of descent and touchdown, playback
  • Flight score out of 100 with the airline's flying rules (250 kt below 10,000 ft, taxi speed, flap and gear limits, stable approach, landing, centre line, touchdown zone, fuel reserve); lights and stall checks once the PC app reads them
  • Fuel check: planned against used and left at the gate
  • Screenshots added to flights, shown on the flight and your profile
  • Recency: 3 landings on a type in 90 days (recency flight or simulator session otherwise; hired pilots go to the simulator)
  • NOTAMs in the briefings (closed runways and ILS are respected)
  • Airline league against the other airlines, Airline of the Month and of the Year with badges and newspaper stories
  • Monthly report against the month before
  • Pilot of the Month (best hired pilot, or the co-op's vote) and staff awards
  • Co-op crew room: shared cockpit flights, trainers signing off check rides, photo contest, base transfer requests
  • Other airlines fly their own daily schedules from their own hubs, with delays

Maintenance and damage5

  • Tail strike, hard landing, flap/gear overspeed, engine failures, overstress
  • Severe turbulence damage (extreme only, realistic results: inspection, overstress, cabin damage)
  • Mechanic reports in the inbox and an animated mechanic pop-up
  • Fix now or fix later; serious damage grounds the aircraft
  • Voices for the mechanic, gate agent and head of operations, word for word: natural voices run on the device (downloaded on the start-up screen), women's and men's voices, angry, apologetic and drunk moods; only-voices and voices-off settings

ATC2

  • Built-in ATC (default) or BeyondATC, chosen in Settings Setting
  • Fines for taking off, landing or taxiing without clearance, with transcript and contest option

Finance12

  • Balance, revenue, costs and profit per day, week, month and year
  • Tap the balance in the top bar to open Finance
  • Real-world fares (distance, domestic or international, aircraft type, cabin mix), on-board sales, bags, cargo
  • Real-world costs per leg: fuel, landing, handling, navigation, maintenance by part, sales fees, catering, crew hotels, overheads
  • Landing messages open the flight's income and expenses
  • GSX Pro ground services charged at real-world rates as GSX completes them (setting); standard charges without GSX
  • Real-world years or simulated years at a chosen speed Setting
  • Transactions list
  • Loans with monthly instalments and early payoff
  • Fleet insurance (basic or full) paying back accident repairs
  • Marketing campaigns for the airline or a route
  • Landing, parking and gate fees by airport; monthly hangar rent at bases

Interface47

  • Search everything (Ctrl+K or the magnifier): aircraft, pilots, staff, airports, routes, flights, logs, news, settings
  • "While you were away" summary after 3 hours or more
  • Reopens on the same screen, tabs, filters and scroll position, also after an update
  • Sort and search the fleet, pilots, crew, flight logs and routes (remembered)
  • Undo for closing a route, dismissing staff, buying stock and clearing messages
  • Notifications per type (show, inbox only, off), quiet hours, repeats grouped
  • Swipe from the edge to go back, swipe a message away, pull down to refresh
  • Android home screen widget: balance, next flight, aircraft in the air
  • Battery saver (automatic below 20%), text size, reduced motion
  • Keyboard shortcuts (? shows them)
  • First-run tour and screen tips (phone and PC)
  • Export flight logs and finances as CSV or PDF
  • Main navigation: Overview, Fleet, Routes, Schedule, Maintenance, Finance, Flight logs, Pilots, Settings
  • Notification bell with unread count and grouped inbox
  • Modular settings with toggles, easy to extend
  • Animated screens, pop-ups and transitions; reduce-motion option
  • Hold-to-exit power button with farewell animation
  • Ranking screen: your own record and the badges you have earned, newest first
  • Registration: a key tied to the first device, reactivation codes for a new phone or PC
  • About 49,000 world airports, plus every airport in your simulator; search by name, city or code
  • Flight menu on the map: follow, favourite and details
  • Co-op flight reports (PIREPs) reviewed by the owner
  • Airport view: runways, taxiways and stands with aircraft taxiing, taking off and landing
  • Real airport layouts from OpenStreetMap: taxiways, aprons, terminals and numbered stands
  • Repair time: instant, or 5 to 90 minutes depending on the job
  • Lease aircraft for 1, 3 or 6 years: monthly rent, extend, buy out or return early
  • Picks up where you left off after being minimised or closed; runs in split screen
  • World map that zooms from the whole world down to airport level, with real coastlines and borders
  • Bases and stands: aircraft per base limited to the airport's real stands
  • Battery indicator with charging and wireless charging
  • Departures, arrivals and ILS/RNP approaches for hired and co-op pilots, shown on the map
  • Real take-off, landing and taxi speeds per aircraft type, by weight and wind
  • Rare delays for hired pilots, for real reasons: de-icing, slots, queues, holding patterns, go-arounds
  • Fly for a real-world airline (names and codes only, no logos)
  • Backup folder outside the app, backup files, restore on a fresh install Android
  • Full-screen mode, edge-swipe protection, back gesture never quits by accident Android
  • Animated start-up screen: detailed compass-star mark, loading steps and a progress bar with sparks; opens on the Overview after five seconds
  • Version number in the bottom-left corner
  • What's new after every update: the newest version's changes open, the two before it folded; again from Settings → General
  • Airline saved on the device and kept through updates; new versions install over the old one
  • Settings saved the moment they change; a second copy of the airline kept in the app's own files and restored if the web storage is cleared
  • Delete a company or everything, from Profiles and companies, with a confirmation prompt
  • Purchase and payment sounds; mute all sounds; the sound button always stays in the top bar
  • Error log: pop-up with the error, Copy and Share; saved list in Settings; start-up failures and Android crashes reported
  • Updates from inside the app, with Android's exact reason when an install is refused
  • World map layers from the layer button on the map: Classic (default, offline), Street (OpenStreetMap, light colours), Satellite (Sentinel-2 cloudless); tap again for Classic
  • Pilot messages open the map on that aircraft and follow it

Crew21

  • Type ratings: hired pilots fly only rated types; courses with a check ride; your first flight on a new type is your check ride
  • Landing leaderboard: you, hired pilots and co-op pilots, softest and best average
  • Hired pilots and crew gain levels and a better rating from their work
  • Hired pilots fly only the routes you assign; test flights only by you
  • 3D full-body avatars (breathing, sway, blinking, look-around, turn with a finger) for all pilots, co-pilots, flight attendants and mechanics, male and female; drawn avatars where 3D isn't available
  • Human figures with real body proportions, skin with pores and colour variation, hair grown strand by strand, beards and moustaches, detailed fabrics
  • Crew figure proportions: Natural, In-between or Stylised Setting
  • Beards (stubble, short, full, goatee, Van Dyke) and moustaches (pencil, chevron, walrus, handlebar, horseshoe); pilot sunglasses
  • Finished crew pictures saved on the device, so they appear at once next time
  • Pilot's cap for captain (gold braid) and co-pilot
  • Create your pilot: name, date of birth, nationality with flag, skin tone, face, eyes, nose, lips, facial hair, glasses, hair, uniform
  • Avatars always fully dressed; clothing only swaps complete garments
  • Hire flight attendants and mechanics, change flight attendants' looks
  • Co-pilot on two-pilot aircraft: choose male or female, look generated
  • Weekly salaries for all staff, paid from the company balance, shown in Finance
  • Conduct reports: discipline or dismiss; adjustable frequency
  • Warnings recorded as light, moderate or severe; most staff improve after a warning; ratings lower the chance of misconduct
  • Rare criminal cases for hired crew and passengers: police, aviation authority, court over 2-3 real days, verdict and sentence
  • Drunk passengers at the gate on your flights: deny or allow boarding, with consequences
  • Real-world crew rules (attendants per seats, co-pilot, mechanics per base and fleet) block flights
  • Birthday greeting in the app and as a phone notification

Flight data5

  • Route legs limited by the aircraft's published range with fuel reserves
  • Fuel on board and weights (empty, passengers, bags, cargo, mail, fuel, total vs. MTOW) from Flight Simulator
  • GSX detection; fallback to the sim's own payload data
  • Connection panel: Wi-Fi network, phone hotspot, PC app scan, manual address
  • Finance transaction details and salary list

Your flights from the simulator12

  • Live position, heading, phase, altitude, remaining distance and arrival time on the map and flight card
  • Leg logged at blocks on: landing rate, G, block time, passengers, hours, landings, aircraft hours and cycles
  • Ticket revenue, fuel and fees booked per leg
  • Mechanic reports damage found on the leg after landing
  • Installed aircraft list from the sim (aircraft packages in Community and the bought add-ons)
  • Plan on SimBrief with the leg filled in; flight plans (.PLN) saved for the simulator
  • SimBrief flight plan import (route, fuel, weights)
  • Live METAR and TAF; the real wind sets the runway in use
  • VATSIM and IVAO: controllers, your airline online, traffic on the map
  • Hoppie ACARS: load sheet and arrival information to your aircraft; messages back
  • Discord posts through a webhook
  • Public airline page to save and share
