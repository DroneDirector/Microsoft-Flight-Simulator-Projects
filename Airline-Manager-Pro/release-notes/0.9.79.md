**Airline Manager Pro 0.9.79: talk to people on your phone**

- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-Setup-0.9.79.exe**: the installer for Windows (recommended). It installs over 0.9.78 and keeps your airline.
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.9.79.zip**: the PC app to unzip anywhere.
- **AirlineManagerPro-0.9.79.apk**: the phone app. It installs over 0.9.78 and keeps your airline.

**Messages that answer what you write**
- Write anything to your family or your staff. They answer what you said: a greeting, asking how they are, thanks, praise, an apology, work (flights, shifts, covering a flight), pay, time off, being sick, things at home, love, a joke, goodbye.
- Be kind or be mean, it's up to you. Kindness warms them up; insults and threats to fire them hurt, and they remember it. A hurt person answers coldly, gets angry, may talk about HR or the union, and needs an apology before things are good again. Each conversation shows how things stand between you (from very close to upset with you).
- They type, sometimes answer in more than one message, and sometimes call you back (ask them to call you, and they will).

**Calls**
- Call anyone in your contacts: it rings, and they answer (unless things are very bad between you). They speak in their own voice, and your lines are spoken in yours.
- Choose what to say from the suggestions, or type your own words; they answer what you said.
- They call you too: the phone rings with Answer and Decline. Calls you miss show in Recents, and they leave you a message.
