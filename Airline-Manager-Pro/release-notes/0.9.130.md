**Airline Manager Pro 0.9.130: window seats zoomed in**

- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-Setup-0.9.130.exe**: the installer for Windows (recommended). It installs over your version and keeps your airline.
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.9.130.zip**: the PC app to unzip anywhere.
- **AirlineManagerPro-0.9.130.apk**: the phone app. It installs over your version and keeps your airline.

**3D view**
- Window L and Window R now start zoomed in towards the window, so the wing and the view outside fill it. The Zoom slider, the mouse wheel and pinching still change it, and the app remembers your choice.
