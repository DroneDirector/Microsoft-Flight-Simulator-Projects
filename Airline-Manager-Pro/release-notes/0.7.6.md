**Airline Manager Pro 0.7.6: smooth when zoomed in on airports**

- **AirlineManagerPro-0.7.6.apk**, the phone app. It installs over 0.7.5 and keeps your airline.
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.7.6.zip**, the PC app with the Windows app inside.

**Performance**
- Zoomed in on an airport, the map is now many times faster: route lines are only drawn where they are on screen (zoomed in, a route is millions of pixels long), the airports in view and the background are looked up from a quick index, and the map draws at a resolution that looks as sharp on a phone with far fewer pixels to fill.
- The same look as before.
