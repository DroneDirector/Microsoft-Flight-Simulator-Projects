**Airline Manager Pro 0.9.11: other airlines, difficulty and markets**

- **AirlineManagerPro-0.9.11.apk**, the phone app. It installs over 0.9.10 and keeps your airline.
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.9.11.zip**, the PC app with the Windows app inside.

- Other airlines fly too: a fixed fleet of aircraft from made-up airlines flies its own routes on the map, many at once, and in the 3D view (only those in front of the camera are drawn). Switch them off and on with the aircraft button on the map or "Other airlines" in the 3D view. Not in co-op mode.
- Difficulty: Cruising along, Easy, Normal, Hard or Extreme, chosen when you found your first airline (Settings → Economy to change it). It sets aircraft prices, sale prices, what flights earn and cost, loan rates, the starting money and how much the markets swing. Your own airline and the co-op airline each have their own.
- Jet fuel and spare parts markets (Finance → Markets): prices move like real markets, with the reason for every rise and fall, and warnings when analysts see a change coming. Buy stock while it's cheap: flights from your bases use the stored fuel first, and maintenance uses the stored parts first.
- Big price moves make the newspaper.
- In the 3D view, the "Other airlines" switch is readable when on.
