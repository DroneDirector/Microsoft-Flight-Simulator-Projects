**Your hand on the phone**
- Hands now turn at the wrist, and the fingers and the thumb bend at their joints.
- Picking up the phone, your hand comes down over it from above and takes it. As you lift it, your hand turns until the phone lies in your palm, with your fingers round its left edge and your thumb resting on the glass, as you would hold a real phone. Your fingers stay outside the phone instead of going through it.
- Putting it down, your hand turns back and lets go.
