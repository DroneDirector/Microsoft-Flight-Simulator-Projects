**Airline Manager Pro 0.9.62: the view out of every cabin window, service with cups and meals, the schedule in your office**

- **AirlineManagerPro-0.9.62.apk**, the phone app. It installs over 0.9.61 and keeps your airline.
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.9.62.zip**, the PC app with the Windows app inside.

**Cabin**
- Every window shows the world outside as it is: the sky, the ground, the wing, the clouds. All shades are up.
- During the service the flight attendants hand each passenger what they asked for, a cup with their drink or a meal tray, onto their tray table, and wait 9 seconds before moving on to the next row.

**Your office**
- "Schedule" in the menu at the bottom: the whole schedule over the room, to see, change and add flights as on the Schedule screen. "Back to the office" or Esc returns.
- A visitor now waits 3 seconds after the door opens, then walks in on a clear path past the open door and the furniture, and out again the same way. No more walking through the door or the walls.
- The conversation box has an X to close it, and stays above the row of places instead of covering it.
- The family photo is on the desk again.
- A new laptop: aluminium, a full keyboard with real keys, a trackpad, the hinge and a thin black bezel round the screen.
