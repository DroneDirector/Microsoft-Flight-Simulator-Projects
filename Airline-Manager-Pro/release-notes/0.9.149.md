## The lounge bar (Business and Flagship lounges)
- A bartender is always behind the bar, in a black waistcoat, tie and white apron, swaying a little. Come within
  about 2.5 m and he turns to you and waves.
- Click him for the drinks: red and white wine, champagne, single malt whisky, gin, vodka, aquavit, dark rum, cognac,
  port and amaretto. Each shows what it is, its strength (ABV), the serving, and how much alcohol is in it (grams and
  standard drinks).
- He takes one of the bottles of that drink from the shelf, a glass from the rack hanging over the counter, sets the
  glass on the counter and pours: you see the drink rise until he stops. He puts the bottle back where it was, and
  the glass is yours. The rack fills up again.
- New bottles on the shelf: wine, champagne, whisky, gin, vodka, rum, cognac and liqueur bottles with labels and the
  drink inside, drawn so they cost little.
- The drink looks like itself (red wine red, white wine pale gold, clear spirits clear), moves in the glass as you
  move, and spills if you swing it hard.

## Drinking
- Each sip is alcohol, by the drink's strength and how much you drink, worked out with the Widmark formula (your
  pilot's sex). Drunk: your walk wobbles and drifts, your knees give a little now and then, the view sways, blurs and
  doubles (on any screen, ultra-wide too). It wears off in about 10 to 25 seconds, the more you drank the longer.
- Every bottle, glass and the glass rack have their own model file names (lounge-props/).
