**Airline Manager Pro 0.9.94: one desktop on every computer**

- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-Setup-0.9.94.exe**: the installer for Windows (recommended). It installs over 0.9.93 and keeps your airline.
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.9.94.zip**: the PC app to unzip anywhere.
- **AirlineManagerPro-0.9.94.apk**: the phone app. It installs over 0.9.93 and keeps your airline.

**The laptop and the Operations computer fill the screen**
- Both take the whole screen on a phone, a tablet, a PC or a TV.
- To go back to your office or the Operations room, press the power button on the taskbar.

**The same desktop in Operations**
- The Operations computer now runs the same desktop as your office laptop: Mail, Calendar, Bank, Screenshots, Ranking, My career, Markets, Aircraft market, the web browser and the rest.
- It also has the operations screens as apps of their own, on the desktop and in the taskbar: Operations, Schedule, Dispatch and Flight planner.
- Clicking the monitor opens the operations screen you chose, with the taskbar still at the bottom.
- The apps on your phone (Messages, Phone, Store) are on both desktops too. Anything you install later shows up on both.
