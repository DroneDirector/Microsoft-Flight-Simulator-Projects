**Airline Manager Pro 0.9.86: your pet, and screens that fit**

- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-Setup-0.9.86.exe**: the installer for Windows (recommended). It installs over 0.9.85 and keeps your airline.
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.9.86.zip**: the PC app to unzip anywhere.
- **AirlineManagerPro-0.9.86.apk**: the phone app. It installs over 0.9.85 and keeps your airline.

**Your pet**
- A dog asleep in its bed on the floor of your office, by the bookshelf: it breathes slowly, an ear twitches now and then, and its paws twitch in a dream. Tap it and it stirs, then sleeps on.
- Or a cat, curled up in its own round bed, its tail flicking now and then.
- Choose and make it yours with your pilot (Your pilot, the Pet tab): a dog, a cat or no pet; its name, coat colour, white chest and paws or one colour, a dog's size and ears, and the colour of its bed.

**The phone and the laptop fit your screen**
- On a phone, the phone in the game is shrunk as a whole to fit, so nothing in it is too big any more; the laptop in your office is shrunk to fit too. On tablets and PCs they look as before.
- Fixed: an empty window frame could cover the laptop's desktop.

**Switching between the phone and the screens**
- With the phone in your hand (or its screen up), click the laptop or the Operations monitor: the phone is put down first, then the screen comes up.
- The laptop and the Operations monitor now open with the room showing round them: click anywhere beside them and they go back down (click the phone, and you pick it up after).
