**Your dog or cat, in far more detail**
- Your pet is now sculpted as one smooth animal instead of separate round pieces: the body, the neck and head, the muzzle, ears, legs, paws and tail flow into one another.
- It has fur: fine strands over the coat with lighter tips, a soft sheen, and a furry edge round its outline.
- Its coat is shaded like a real one: a white (or lighter) chest, muzzle and paws if you chose them, and a slightly darker back.
- It still breathes slowly, twitches an ear, flicks its tail (a cat) or twitches its paws in a dream (a dog), and lifts its head a little when you tap it.
