**Airline Manager Pro 0.9.92: a quiet start screen**

- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-Setup-0.9.92.exe**: the installer for Windows (recommended). It installs over 0.9.91 and keeps your airline.
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.9.92.zip**: the PC app to unzip anywhere.
- **AirlineManagerPro-0.9.92.apk**: the phone app. It installs over 0.9.91 and keeps your airline.

**Only the soundtrack and the film on the start screen**
- Your airline keeps running underneath, but nothing from the game shows or sounds while the start screen is up.
- That means no messages ("on final" and the like), no pop-ups, no badges, no voices and no game sounds.
- On the phone, the system notifications still arrive in the notification shade.
- Pop-ups the game wanted to show wait until you leave the start screen.
- What you open yourself (Options, Quit, New game) still works as normal.

**The menu**
- The menu always reads Continue, New game, Save game, Load game, Options and Quit.
- Explore the demo is gone: Continue takes you into the game.
