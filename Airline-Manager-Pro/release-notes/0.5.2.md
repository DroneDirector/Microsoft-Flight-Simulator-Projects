**Airline Manager Pro 0.5.2: the app on Windows**

- **AirlineManagerPro-0.5.2.apk**, the phone app. It installs over 0.5.1 and keeps your airline.
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.5.2.zip**, the PC app, now with the whole app for Windows inside.

**The app on Windows**
- Start AirlineManagerPro-PC.exe and the full app opens in its own window, connected straight to Flight Simulator. No phone needed, but you can still link one at the same time.
- Same screens as on the phone. Everything scales with the window and keeps its shape, from a small window to wide, ultrawide and super-ultrawide screens.
- Settings → Display: window, borderless or full screen (F11 switches), the size of everything, which graphics card to use, and the sound output (speakers, headphones, Bluetooth).
- Your airline is saved on the PC and backed up to Documents\Airline Manager Pro.

**Staff and money**
- Weekly payroll: everyone you employ is paid each week, taken from the balance and listed in Finance.
- Hiring costs a recruitment and training fee.
- The hiring list changes over time: new co-pilots, flight attendants, mechanics and captains come looking, others find other jobs.
- Conduct reports can be switched off in Settings → Staff. Nothing that already happened is removed; court cases pause and carry on when you switch back on.
- All spending shows straight away in the balance and in Finance with the right date.

**Asking first**
- Changing the look of your pilot, your co-pilot or your staff asks "Are you sure?" first. No keeps the look they have.
- Edit your airline's name, codes, home base and colour in Settings → Profiles and companies, also with a question first.
