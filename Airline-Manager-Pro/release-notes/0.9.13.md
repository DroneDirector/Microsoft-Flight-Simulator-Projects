**Airline Manager Pro 0.9.13: your career, and a fuller co-op**

- **AirlineManagerPro-0.9.13.apk**, the phone app. It installs over 0.9.12 and keeps your airline.
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.9.13.zip**, the PC app with the Windows app inside.

**My career (new in the menu, in your own airline and in co-op)**
- Profile: your flights, hours, average landing, favourite aircraft, a map of every route you've flown, your licence (type ratings: your first flight on a type is the check ride) and where you are now.
- Pay and wallet: you earn flight pay by the hour, more as you level up.
- Pilot location and jumpseat (Settings → Economy, always on in co-op): your next flight starts where you landed; to start elsewhere, ride the jumpseat, paid from your wallet.
- Flight board: book your next flights ahead. In co-op everyone's bookings are on the board, and an aircraft can't be booked twice.
- Pilot challenges every week, paid into your wallet, and events: fly the leg in the time window for a bonus.
- Airport briefings for tricky airports, shown at dispatch. Write your own.

**Co-op**
- New tabs: flight board, chat, events, staff and members, rules and codeshares.
- Ranks unlock aircraft: cadets fly turboprops, airliners from senior first officer, widebodies from captain.
- Flight reports are checked automatically (landing rate, g-force, a block time too short for the distance, rank); those that pass are accepted at once, the rest wait for a reviewer with the reason.
- Staff roles: chief pilot, report reviewer, trainer and events manager.
- Group flights and events with sign-ups and a countdown.
- New pilots apply and the owner accepts them; leave of absence; inactive pilots are warned, then removed.
- Chat with announcements, and an optional Discord post for landings, bookings, events and announcements.
- Codeshares with other co-op airlines: more passengers on every co-op flight.
