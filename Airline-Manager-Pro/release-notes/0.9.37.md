**Airline Manager Pro 0.9.37: almost 3,900 well-known airports built in**

- **AirlineManagerPro-0.9.37.apk**, the phone app. It installs over 0.9.36 and keeps your airline.
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.9.37.zip**, the PC app with the Windows app inside.

- Every large airport with scheduled flights, and every other airport with scheduled flights and an airline code, is now built into the app: 3,886 airports instead of 62 (Stockholm Skavsta, Sogndal, Florø, Sumburgh, Honolulu and many more). They are there from the moment the app starts, offered for jobs, new routes, the used-aircraft market and your home base. The full list of about 49,000 airports is still searchable as before.
- The new-route window lists the 60 nearest airports; search finds every other one.
- Seaplane bases are no longer counted as major airports, and are not used as ETOPS alternates.
