**Airline Manager Pro 0.9.51: your office, the courtroom any time, conduct reports in full, alerts with the time**

- **AirlineManagerPro-0.9.51.apk**, the phone app. It installs over 0.9.50 and keeps your airline.
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.9.51.zip**, the PC app with the Windows app inside.

**Your office**
- A corner office in 3D: a walnut desk with a leather top, your laptop showing the airline, a banker's lamp, the phone, coffee, a model of one of your aircraft in the company colours, your nameplate and a photo of you with your family. A big window over the airport and the mountains, bookshelves, aviation posters, your route map, a propeller on the wall, a leather sofa, a globe and your trophies.
- Go there any time: Office in the menu, or My office on the Overview. Sit in your chair, look at the paintings, the window, the route map or the family photo, or switch to free mode and walk around (W A S D, or the stick). Click your chair to sit; click the door to leave.
- Over a conduct report, choose **Invite to my office**. There is a knock, they come in and sit down across the desk, facing you. Ask what happened, whether the report is true, why, how you'll know it won't happen again, and why you should keep them.
- They answer honestly, defensively, dismissively or with a lie, in different words each time, and you can see it in how they sit and look at you. Then keep them, discipline them or dismiss them, and they react before they leave.
- Their answers can be played aloud. Settings → Conduct → Play answers aloud in the office plays them by themselves.

**The courtroom, any time**
- Court in the menu and The courtroom on the Overview take you in. When a hearing or a verdict is waiting, you go straight to it with everyone there. Otherwise the courtroom is empty and you can walk around it, or jump to the bench, the witness box, the defence table, the public benches or the doors.
- Crew → Court lists every court case.
- The courtroom and the office open behind a loading screen: the scales of justice weighing, in walnut and gold, with a progress bar until everything is ready.

**Conduct reports**
- Each report now says what was said, word for word, or what was done, who reported it, any witnesses, and the employee's side.

**Alerts**
- Conduct reports, the court, the aviation authority, serious accidents and staff reporting a safety problem now show a notification on the phone or in Windows, also when the app is in the background, following your notification settings and quiet hours.
- Every message shows when it came: "Today 17:09 · 5 min ago" in Notifications, and the date and time on reports and court cases.
