**Airline Manager Pro 0.9.64: the Operations monitor comes to life**

- **AirlineManagerPro-0.9.64.apk**, the phone app. It installs over 0.9.63 and keeps your airline.
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.9.64.zip**, the PC app with the Windows app inside.

**Operations office**
- Click or tap the monitor: it lights up, and the screens grow out of it while you lean in, until they fill the view. Back to the desk (or Esc) shrinks them back into the monitor as you sit back, and the whole office comes into view again. With "reduce motion" on, the screens open and close at once.
- The monitor sits closer to you, about 30 cm behind the keyboard, and the archive folders on the desk no longer show through it.
- Switching between Operations, Schedule, Dispatch and the Flight planner fades the new screen in.
