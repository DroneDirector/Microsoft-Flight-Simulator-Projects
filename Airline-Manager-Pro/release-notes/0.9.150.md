# 0.9.150

## Flight boards
- Departures and Arrivals screens side by side, 30 cm apart: on the lounge wall, and hanging over the concourse
  just before the lounge (not in your office, Operations or the Training room).
- Your own flights and the other airlines' traffic at that airport. Departures: scheduled time, expected time when
  it changes, flight, destination, aircraft and status (On time, Boarding, Delayed, Departed, Cancelled). Arrivals:
  scheduled and expected time of arrival, flight, origin, aircraft and status (On time, Expected, Delayed, Landed).
- Changed text turns over like the yellow split-flap text on the flight cards, character by character, and the glass
  over the screens catches the light like the monitors and the phone.
