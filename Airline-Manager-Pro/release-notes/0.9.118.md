**Airline Manager Pro 0.9.118: Hangars in the menu**

- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-Setup-0.9.118.exe**: the installer for Windows (recommended). It installs over your version and keeps your airline.
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.9.118.zip**: the PC app to unzip anywhere.
- **AirlineManagerPro-0.9.118.apk**: the phone app. It installs over your version and keeps your airline.

- **Hangars in the menu:** the left-hand menu has Hangars, between Operations and Court. It opens the Hangars page, with every hangar at every base.
- **What the page shows:** which aircraft is in each hangar, what is being fixed, how far the work has come (with a progress bar), who is working on it and when it will be ready.
