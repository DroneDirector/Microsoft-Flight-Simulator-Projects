**Airline Manager Pro 0.7.2: published procedures from the simulator**

- **AirlineManagerPro-0.7.2.apk**, the phone app. It installs over 0.7.1 and keeps your airline.
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.7.2.zip**, the PC app with the Windows app inside. Update it too: it reads the procedures from the simulator.

**Real SIDs, STARs and approaches**
- Linked to the simulator, your hired and co-op pilots fly the published departures (SIDs), arrivals (STARs) and approaches from Microsoft Flight Simulator's own navigation data, through their real fixes.
- The departure for the runway in use that heads towards the destination, the arrival that suits where the flight comes from, and the ILS (or RNAV/RNP) approach for the landing runway.
- The map names them as the simulator does. They are kept on the phone, so they also work later without the link.
- Where the simulator has none, the generated departures and approaches are used as before. A flight in the air keeps its path.
