# 0.9.153

- Frame generation: what you hold (a cup, a glass, a snack from the vending machine, the photo from your desk, an
  item in the hangar) now moves with your view in the frames in between, so it no longer flickers or jumps when you
  move or turn quickly.
- Spills on the floor fade away as they dry up instead of vanishing at once (every kind of drink).
