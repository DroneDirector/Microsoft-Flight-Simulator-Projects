**Airline Manager Pro 0.9.60: the Flight planner, PlayStation 5 controllers, smoother turns**

- **AirlineManagerPro-0.9.60.apk**, the phone app. It installs over 0.9.59 and keeps your airline.
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.9.60.zip**, the PC app with the Windows app inside.

**Flight planner** (in the menu, below Dispatch)
- Your route, the weather and the NOTAMs together on one map. Pick one of your routes (or type two airports) and the aircraft.
- Waypoints: drag them on the map, tap "Add waypoints" and then the map, or type an airport or a position (6200N00700W). Each has the level to fly from it on (step climbs and descents happen there) and, if you want, a speed (M.82 or 280 kt); with the time and the wind there. Climb and descent speeds and an alternate for the whole flight.
- The vertical profile under the map: the climb, the step climbs, the top of climb and of descent, and the SIGMETs at the levels the route passes through them.
- "Flights fly this plan": every flight between the two airports then follows it, on the map, in 3D and in its times. A level above the aircraft's ceiling is flown at the ceiling (shown in amber).
- Weather: the live reports and forecasts at the departure, the arrival and the alternate, the SIGMETs on the route (also drawn on the map), and the winds along it.
- NOTAMs: real ones with your own free FAA NOTAM API key (Settings → Online services; kept on your device), and the airline's own for the day.

**Smoother turns**
- Aircraft turn at waypoints in round, gentle arcs as real airliners do: about 7 nm across at cruise speed, 3.5 to 4 nm joining and leaving the route, about 1.5 nm in departures and arrivals. The same path on the map and in the 3D view.

**PlayStation 5 controllers** (Settings → Controller)
- The DualSense and DualSense Edge (and other controllers) work everywhere: the left stick moves a pointer, Cross or the touchpad press clicks (hold to drag), Circle goes back, the right stick scrolls or zooms, L1 and R1 change page, the d-pad jumps between buttons, Options opens Settings.
- In 3D, the cabin, the office and the courtroom: the sticks walk and look; L2 and R2 zoom, L1 and R1 change aircraft, Square changes camera, Triangle brings the pointer.
- On the PC the touchpad works as a mouse (slide to move, tap to click, two fingers to scroll) once you allow it in Settings → Controller. A light rumble on each click.
