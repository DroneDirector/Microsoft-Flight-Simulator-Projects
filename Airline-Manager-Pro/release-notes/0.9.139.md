**Airline Manager Pro 0.9.139: the lounge's machines, a knock at your door, your family**

- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-Setup-0.9.139.exe**: the installer for Windows (recommended). It installs over your version and keeps your airline.
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.9.139.zip**: the PC app to unzip anywhere.
- **AirlineManagerPro-0.9.139.apk**: the phone app. It installs over your version and keeps your airline.

**Walking between your rooms**
- Doors are easier to walk through. Heading at an open door, you are eased through the middle of the doorway instead of catching on the frame, from the corridor and from inside the rooms. A door counts as open a little sooner. A locked door still never lets you through.

**The lounge**
- The vending machine from the hangars now stands where the fridge was. Choose a snack or a drink, the coil turns and it drops; open the flap and take it, then eat or drink it a bite or a sip at a time.
- The coffee machine: your hand reaches out to its touchscreen and stays there while you choose espresso, cappuccino, latte, americano, hot chocolate or tea; then your arm comes back and the drink runs from the spouts into the cup, which fills as you watch. Hot drinks steam.
- Each sip lowers the drink. Carry the cup and the drink sways with how you move; swing it about wildly and it spills over the rim, leaving less in the cup and drops on the floor.

**Your office**
- A knock at the door: you answer with Come in (in your own voice), the door opens and two seconds later they walk in and sit down. If you don't answer, they knock again.

**Your family**
- In Create your pilot and Your pilot: married or not, your partner's name, and none to three children.
- The photo on your desk follows it: your family; or, not married, your pet; or, with no family and no pet, you on your first day in the job, dated in the corner.

**Android**
- Lock to the game (Settings → Display): swipes from the edges can't open the notification panel or the system buttons while you play. Android asks once to confirm; to leave, hold Back and Recents, or swipe up and hold.
- The system bars hide again more quickly after a swipe from the edge.
