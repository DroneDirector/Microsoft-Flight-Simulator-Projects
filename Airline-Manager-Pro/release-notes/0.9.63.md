**Airline Manager Pro 0.9.63: the Operations office, talking faces, loading screens on the way out**

- **AirlineManagerPro-0.9.63.apk**, the phone app. It installs over 0.9.62 and keeps your airline.
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.9.63.zip**, the PC app with the Windows app inside.

**The Operations office**
- A new room for everything scheduling: "Operations" in the menu, on the Overview and on the Schedule screen. It has its own loading screen.
- You sit in a high-backed leather chair at a long oak desk with a curved ultra-wide monitor, a keyboard and a mouse. The monitor shows your schedule live, as on the Schedule screen.
- Click the monitor (or the buttons below) for Operations, the Schedule, Dispatch and the Flight planner, working exactly as in the app. It is the same data: every change shows at once on the Schedule screen, in your office and on the monitor.
- Back or Esc returns to the desk. With nothing open, Back or Esc leaves the room.
- Archive folders stacked on the desk, and a tall archive rack full of binders and boxes.

**Leaving a 3D place**
- Your office, the Operations office, the courtroom and the 3D view each show their own loading screen when you leave, for at least 6 seconds, until the app is ready again.
- Meanwhile your airline is saved and the room's 3D graphics are cleared from memory: models, textures, people and the drawing surface. None of your data is touched. This keeps phones from running short of graphics memory after visiting several places.
- The Android back button and the gamepad's Circle work the same way.

**Faces**
- Crew, passengers, the people in your office and the courtroom move their mouths while they speak in a conversation, from the words they say. The jaw turns on its hinge, the lips round on "o" and spread on "e", and the teeth and tongue show as the mouth opens. Mouths stay still otherwise.

**Settings → Graphics → Animation**
- Walking, Speaking and Body language, each Low, Medium, High or Ultra (Automatic follows the cabin quality).
