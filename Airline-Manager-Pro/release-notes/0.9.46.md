**Airline Manager Pro 0.9.46: your airline on all your devices, cabin crew, and the PC display**

- **AirlineManagerPro-0.9.46.apk**, the phone app. It installs over 0.9.45 and keeps your airline.
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.9.46.zip**, the PC app with the Windows app inside.

**Sync: one airline on your phone and your PC, anywhere**
- Settings → Sync: switch it on, and your airline (the demo airline too) is on all your devices, live, wherever they have internet.
- Link another device with a short code (valid ten minutes) from Settings → Sync on the first device.
- The device you use runs the airline; the others show it live and take over the moment you use them. During a flight in Flight Simulator, the device linked to the simulator keeps running it.
- Locked on your devices before it goes online: only your own devices can read it.
- No internet? Keep playing; it catches up later. If two devices were played apart, you choose which copy to keep, and the other is kept.
- Display, sound and graphics settings stay with each device.

**The cabin**
- The cabin crew: each aircraft has its own team of your hired flight attendants (shown on their Crew card as "Flies on"), with the flight's captain. They walk the aisle, sit on the jump seats for every take-off, landing and spell of severe turbulence (a different seat each time), and serve drinks and snacks from a trolley in the cruise, never before it and always seated well before landing.
- They talk with the passengers, who answer in their own mood; a catering shortfall, a rude attendant from the conduct reports or a drunk passenger you let on all show. Tap a speech mark, a crew member or a passenger to read what they said, with a play button to hear it.
- Every passenger on the passenger list is in a seat, spread through the cabin as booked; business and first class in their real seats (A C D F). Nobody shows through the seat backs any more.
- Overhead bins at head height with the windows visible below them.
- At night the cabin is dimmed, with soft mood lighting and a few reading lights on.

**At the stand**
- Parked aircraft keep the nose wheel on the stop mark with the main wheels on the lead-in line, also where the line curves.

**PC**
- Settings → Display: Windowed, Borderless or Full screen; a resolution setting (from 4:3 to 32:9 super ultrawide, "Match my screen"); and anti-aliasing from Off to Ultra.
- Ultrawide and super-ultrawide screens are filled edge to edge, and narrower screens (4:3, 5:4, 16:10) now fit too.
