**Airline Manager Pro 0.5.9: real airport layouts and repair times**

- **AirlineManagerPro-0.5.9.apk**, the phone app. It installs over 0.5.8 and keeps your airline.
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.5.9.zip**, the PC app with the Windows app inside.

**Airport view**
- Airports now show their real layout: runways, taxiways, taxilanes, aprons, terminal buildings and numbered stands, from OpenStreetMap.
- Aircraft taxi along the real taxiways between their stand and the runway.
- Runways that are mapped in several pieces are joined, and the official runway length is used.
- Each airport is downloaded once (usually 5-110 KB) and kept on the device. When the app is linked to the simulator, the simulator's own layout is still used.

**Maintenance**
- New in Settings → Damage and realism: Repair time. Instant, or decided by the app: 5, 10, 15, 20, 25, 30, 40, 50, 60 or 90 minutes in the hangar, depending on the job and your mechanics.
- One payment repairs every open report on the aircraft together, so it isn't asked for again.
- Aircraft stuck in the hangar after an earlier payment get their repair time and come out.

**Live operations**
- Details in a flight's menu now opens for your own flight too, with a close button.
