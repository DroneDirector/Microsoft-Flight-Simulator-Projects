# 0.9.148

## Flight trace
- No more false "no touchdown" reports. A flight now only counts as watched during its landing when the app looked
  at it just before and just after the touchdown. With the screen off or the app in the background across the
  landing, the first look afterwards finding it taxiing in is no longer reported as a problem.
- A landing shorter than the time between two looks (at a faster virtual pilot speed) counts as landed, also when
  the look before it was still on the approach.
