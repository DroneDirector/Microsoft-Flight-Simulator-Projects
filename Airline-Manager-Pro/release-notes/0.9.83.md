**Airline Manager Pro 0.9.83: saved as things happen, and when you say so**

- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-Setup-0.9.83.exe**: the installer for Windows (recommended). It installs over 0.9.82 and keeps your airline.
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.9.83.zip**: the PC app to unzip anywhere.
- **AirlineManagerPro-0.9.83.apk**: the phone app. It installs over 0.9.82 and keeps your airline.

**Saved as things happen**
- What changes by itself is saved too: the markets and prices moving, money coming in and going out, concessions, flights, messages and conversations, the court and the authority, all the airline's data. The app checks every couple of seconds and saves whenever anything has changed (while aircraft are moving, at most every 8 seconds, so phones stay smooth).

**Save when you want**
- Settings > Backup and restore has a Save now button: it saves at once, writes the phone's own copy and a backup save, and copies to your backup folder if you have one. On the PC, Ctrl+S does the same.
