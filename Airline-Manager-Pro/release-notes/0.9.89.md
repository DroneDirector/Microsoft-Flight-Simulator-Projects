**Airline Manager Pro 0.9.89: your pet's colours and its bowls**

- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-Setup-0.9.89.exe**: the installer for Windows (recommended). It installs over 0.9.88 and keeps your airline.
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.9.89.zip**: the PC app to unzip anywhere.
- **AirlineManagerPro-0.9.89.apk**: the phone app. It installs over 0.9.88 and keeps your airline.

**Colour your dog or cat** (Your pilot → Pet)
- Choose the main coat colour from the coats, or pick any colour of your own.
- Choose the colour of the markings (chest, muzzle and paws) from white, cream, tan, silver, grey, brown, black and gold, or pick your own.
- The colours blend into the fur just like the default ones: the same shading, darker back, lighter strand tips and soft sheen.
- A picture of your pet in its bed shows each change as you make it.

**Water and food bowls**
- Two glazed bowls stand side by side next to the bed: one of water, and one of food (kibble).
- The food bowl matches the bed and has your pet's name painted on it, with a paw print either side.
- Rename your pet and the bowl changes with it.
