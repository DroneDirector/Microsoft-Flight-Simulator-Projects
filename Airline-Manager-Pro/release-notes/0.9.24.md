**Airline Manager Pro 0.9.24: boarding scan fix**

- **AirlineManagerPro-0.9.24.apk**, the phone app. It installs over 0.9.23 and keeps your airline.
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.9.24.zip**, the PC app with the Windows app inside.

- Boarding scan: tapping the notice about a passenger on a no-fly list now opens the decision (deny boarding or let them on). Before, nothing happened.
- If the flight has already left or the gate decided for you, the window says so instead of staying empty.
