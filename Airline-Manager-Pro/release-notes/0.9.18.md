**Airline Manager Pro 0.9.18: airports that look right in 3D**

- **AirlineManagerPro-0.9.18.apk**, the phone app. It installs over 0.9.17 and keeps your airline.
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.9.18.zip**, the PC app with the Windows app inside.

- Taxiways and aprons now show in the 3D view, laid over the ground in real colours: asphalt taxiways with shoulders and yellow centre lines, concrete aprons, rounded corners where taxiways meet. Runways and hold short lines no longer disappear into the terrain.
- Sharper satellite imagery right at the airport: the 5 km around it get their own picture at the imagery's full detail, fading into the ground around.
- Settings → 3D view: use your own high-resolution imagery service (for example MapTiler Satellite with your own key) for up to about 1 m a pixel at airports.
