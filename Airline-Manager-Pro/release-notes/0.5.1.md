**Airline Manager Pro 0.5.1: careers, courts and real airlines**

- **AirlineManagerPro-0.5.1.apk**, the phone app. It installs over 0.5.0 and keeps your airline (or use Settings → Updates and errors → Check for updates).
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.5.1.zip**, the PC app. Update it too: it now sends the weather from Flight Simulator to the phone.

**Your career and your airline**
- New Ranking screen: ranks for you and for the airline, your pilot rating, the airline's popularity and over 1,100 badges in six tiers, from your first flight to every aircraft type, airport and country.
- Fly for a real-world airline: Atlantic Airways, Icelandair, SAS, Widerøe, British Airways, Lufthansa, Emirates, Delta, Air Greenland, Harbour Air and many more. You start at its home base with part of its fleet.
- Real-world money: fares by distance, domestic or international and aircraft type; food, drinks and bags; fuel, landing, handling and navigation charges, maintenance per engine, airframe and parts, catering, crew hotels and overheads. Tap a landing message to see the income and expenses.

**Hired pilots and flights**
- Each aircraft model starts its descent, approach and final at real-world distances (an A320 about 120 nm out, a 777 about 125-135 nm, an ATR 72 about 80 nm, a Cessna about 25 nm), with messages at top of descent and on final.
- The schedule follows what really happens: tap any aircraft to see its day; your own legs follow your flights in the sim.
- Weather and turbulence on your flight come from Flight Simulator (demo weather in demo mode).

**Aircraft and maintenance**
- Condition now depends on age, use and your mechanics, in one bar: green, yellow, orange, red. Worn aircraft have emergencies, and in the worst state, accidents. If poor maintenance caused one, the airline goes to court.
- Repairs take real time, 5 minutes to 2 hours, and carry on while the app is closed. The hangar is drawn with the aircraft inside. No more red backgrounds on Maintenance.
- New Aircraft types view in Fleet.

**Crew and passengers**
- Conduct reports are light, moderate or severe. Warnings are recorded, and most people learn from them. Well-rated staff misbehave less.
- Very rarely, a crew member's misconduct is a crime: police, the aviation authority and a court case over two to three days, with a verdict and sentence that fit the offence. This only happens to your hired crew, never to you.
- Drunk passengers at the gate: deny boarding, or let them on and live with the consequences.

**Looks**
- Natural skin tones. Outfits match men and women. Skirts fit properly.
- The look editor zooms to what you're editing (face, glasses and hats, shoes) and moves smoothly.

**Saving and backups**
- Settings save the moment you change them, and the app reopens where you left it.
- Choose a backup folder (Documents, an SD card or Google Drive) for automatic backups that survive clearing the app's data, reinstalling or a new phone. Backup files can also be saved and restored by hand.
