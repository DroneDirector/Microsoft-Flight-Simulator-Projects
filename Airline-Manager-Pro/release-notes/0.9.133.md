**Airline Manager Pro 0.9.133: recommended graphics, a stress test, photos onto your device**

- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-Setup-0.9.133.exe**: the installer for Windows (recommended). It installs over your version and keeps your airline.
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.9.133.zip**: the PC app to unzip anywhere.
- **AirlineManagerPro-0.9.133.apk**: the phone app. It installs over your version and keeps your airline.

**Recommended graphics settings (Settings, Graphics)**
- A new card at the top shows what this device is (its graphics chip, memory, processor cores, screen and refresh rate) and the graphics settings recommended for it.
- It never changes anything by itself: "Change to recommended settings" applies them, and Undo puts yours back.

**Stress test**
- "Run a stress test" walks through your office, the Operations office, the concourse and your lounge for about a minute, asking more and more of the device: a sharper picture, shadows, busier rooms.
- It stops close to the device's limit, never past it, and then reports how well it coped, stage by stage, with the settings it recommends and a button to use them.
- The result is kept, and the recommendation follows it from then on.

**Photos**
- Photos taken in cinema mode (with all of its looks, wide frame and depth of field) and screenshots in the 3D view are now also saved onto your device: in your phone's gallery (Pictures, Airline Manager Pro) and on the PC in your Pictures folder (Airline Manager Pro). They are still kept on your laptop in the game too.
- The Cinema button now has the same colours as the other buttons.

**Title screen**
- Testing is gone from the title screen: the joined rooms move into the game itself (coming next). The stress test uses them.
