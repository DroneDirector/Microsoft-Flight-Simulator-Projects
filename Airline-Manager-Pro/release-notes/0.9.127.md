**Airline Manager Pro 0.9.127: no more pop-ins, a smooth 3D view, coffee in the lounge, and a showroom for aircraft**

- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-Setup-0.9.127.exe**: the installer for Windows (recommended). It installs over your version and keeps your airline.
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.9.127.zip**: the PC app to unzip anywhere.
- **AirlineManagerPro-0.9.127.apk**: the phone app. It installs over your version and keeps your airline.

**3D view**
- **No more pop-ins.** The ground under you no longer disappears and reloads as you fly on:
  - The detailed ground is moved along with you long before you reach its edge.
  - The ground you see stays until the new ground, with its satellite pictures, has fully loaded. Then it changes over in one frame. Only the far edges of the detail rings move, out in the haze.
- **Clouds** stay where they are in the world as you fly on, instead of being rebuilt. Other cloud changes fade in and out.
- **Rain and snow** fade in and out on their own, so rain turning to snow fades across.
- **No more flicker.** When the app adjusted the 3D view's sharpness to keep the frame rate smooth, the screen could flash for a moment. The change now happens just before a frame is drawn, and only after two readings agree, so it no longer swings back and forth. This applies in the 3D view, the office, the operations room, the courtroom, the hangars and the lounges.
- **Sunrise and sunset are more vibrant:**
  - deeper oranges and reds near the sun
  - a soft pink band opposite it, and a richer violet-blue overhead
  - warmer golden light on everything that lasts a little longer
  - clouds that take on the sunset's colours
  - a sea that reflects them

**Lounges**
- **Coffee and tea, one cup at a time.** Order at the bar, then pick your cup up from the counter. Click to take a sip (three sips and it's finished), or aim at a table, the counter or the bar to put it down. You can order the next cup once the first is drunk or put down.
- **Heat rising from hot drinks:** a small shimmer like the heat behind the engines, fading as the drink cools. You can switch it off in Settings → Graphics → Lounges.

**Aircraft market**
- **View:** next to Buy and Lease, for new and used aircraft. You see the aircraft on its own in a new hangar of the right size, and can walk round it. Your own hangars aren't affected. "Back to the market" takes you back.
