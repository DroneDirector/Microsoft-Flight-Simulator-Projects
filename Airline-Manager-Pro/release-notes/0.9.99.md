**Airline Manager Pro 0.9.99: pick the runways, with real procedures at US airports**

- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-Setup-0.9.99.exe**: the installer for Windows (recommended). It installs over 0.9.98 and keeps your airline.
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.9.99.zip**: the PC app to unzip anywhere.
- **AirlineManagerPro-0.9.99.apk**: the phone app. It installs over 0.9.98 and keeps your airline.

**Flight planner: Runways**
- A new Runways tab. For each route, choose the take-off runway and the landing runway, or leave them on "By the wind".
- Each runway shows its length, the wind along it right now, and whether it has an ILS.
- The choice applies to every flight on the route: your own flights and the ones your pilots fly.
- A runway with more than 10 kt of tailwind isn't used until the wind turns, just as the tower would decide. You'll see a warning when this happens, and also when the crosswind is more than your aircraft is allowed.
- The tab shows the departure, arrival and approach that flights will fly, and where they come from.

**Real procedures without Flight Simulator**
- US airports now use the FAA's published departures (SIDs), arrivals (STARs) and approaches, with their real waypoints. This data comes with the app, so Flight Simulator doesn't need to be running.
- Elsewhere, flights still use the procedures read from Flight Simulator when it has been linked. Otherwise the procedures are built from the runway.
- Next: choose the SID, STAR and approach yourself, with their waypoints on the map.

**Schedule**
- The Refresh button now always shows the word "Refresh", so it's easy to find on phones and tablets.
