**Airline Manager Pro 0.7.5: delays only for hired pilots**

- **AirlineManagerPro-0.7.5.apk**, the phone app. It installs over 0.7.4 and keeps your airline.
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.7.5.zip**, the PC app with the Windows app inside.

**Delays**
- The rare delays from 0.7.4 (de-icing, slots, queues, holding patterns, go-arounds) now only happen to your hired pilots' flights. Co-op flights are flown by real players and your own flights by you, so they are never delayed by the app.
