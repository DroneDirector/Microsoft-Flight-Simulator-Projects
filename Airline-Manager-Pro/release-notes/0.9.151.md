# 0.9.151

## Your lounge guests at the bar
- Passengers waiting in your Business and Flagship lounges go to the bar now and then: the bartender serves them just
  as he serves you, and they drink sip by sip. Every drink they buy is paid into your balance.
- Most stop after a drink or two. Now and then (about one guest in twenty) someone drinks far too much; while you
  are not in the lounge, about one departure in twenty from a lounge with a bar has a passenger that drunk.
- A passenger that drunk is reported to you: let them board or deny boarding. Letting a drunk passenger fly costs a
  little popularity; they are marked Drunk on the passenger list and sit slumped and swaying in the cabin. If you
  don't decide before boarding, the cabin crew deny them boarding and tell you why.

## Spirits in storage
- Finance → Storage has a Spirits section: every drink the bartenders serve, bought by the bottle, with what is
  left, what is used a day and when to order. A drink that runs out can't be served; with nothing left the bars
  serve nothing. Your own drinks at your bar come from this stock and are free.
