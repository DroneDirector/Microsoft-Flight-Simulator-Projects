**Airline Manager Pro 0.9.61: real airways from Flight Simulator, the Flight planner in your office**

- **AirlineManagerPro-0.9.61.apk**, the phone app. It installs over 0.9.60 and keeps your airline.
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.9.61.zip**, the PC app with the Windows app inside.

**Real airways**
- While the PC app is linked to Flight Simulator, airliners and cargo jets fly the high airways and turboprops and light aircraft the low ones, from where their departures end to where their arrivals begin, from the simulator's own navigation data. The same path on the map and in 3D, with the times worked out along it.
- Each route is worked out once per city pair and kept on your phone or PC, so it is flown afterwards also without the simulator. The PC app keeps the parts of the network it has read.
- In the Flight planner, "Real airways (Flight Simulator)" fills in the waypoints, each with the airway it is on.
- Settings → Simulator link → "Real airways from Flight Simulator" switches it off.

**Flight planner**
- In your office: "Flight planner" in the menu at the bottom opens the whole planner over the room; "Back to the office" or Esc returns.
- Ready for SimBrief: your SimBrief flight plan's waypoints, airways, levels, cruise speeds and alternate in the planner and from Dispatch, as soon as the SimBrief link is switched on.
