**Water salute**
- Your airline might land at an airport it has never been to before, on your own flight or one your pilots fly. When it does, the airport's fire service welcomes it with a water salute.
- Two fire trucks stand on either side of the taxiway. They spray water from their roof cannons, and the two jets cross high above in an arch, like a rainbow. The aircraft taxis underneath.
- The papers write about it. Each article has its own photo, taken from a different spot with the aircraft right under the arch.
- The scene is set at the real airport. You'll see its runways where they really are and its name on the terminal. The sun and the sky match the time of day and the weather there. The land around matches the place: mountains on the Faroes or in the Alps, snow in Iceland in winter, sand in Dubai.
- Tap **Watch it in 3D** in the article to see the whole salute as a short film, with the sound of the water.
- Airports your airline has already flown to don't get a salute.
