# 0.9.144

## Frame generation that actually makes frames
- The performance monitor could show "0 made" and the same number of frames as real ones. Each frame in between
  still worked out every aircraft, the sky, the clouds, the weather and the cabin's outside view, so it took as long
  as a real frame and was never worth making.
- Frames in between now only turn the picture to where the camera is looking. Everything else is worked out on the
  real frames, so the frames in between cost next to nothing.
- The number of frames in between follows the screen's own pace (60 Hz or faster): one when real frames come at
  about 30 a second, two when they are slower.
- The cabin now gets frames in between too, up to 60 a second (it was held at 30).
- In tests: real frames at 30 a second now show about 40; at 20 a second, about 36; at 13 a second, about 26.
