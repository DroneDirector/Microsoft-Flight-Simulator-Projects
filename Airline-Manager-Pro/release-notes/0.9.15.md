**Airline Manager Pro 0.9.15: free camera, runway markings and a steadier 3D view**

- **AirlineManagerPro-0.9.15.apk**, the phone app. It installs over 0.9.14 and keeps your airline.
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.9.15.zip**, the PC app with the Windows app inside.

- Free camera in the 3D view: fly around like a drone. Look with one finger, move with the stick, climb and sink with the height buttons, set the speed with the slider; "?" shows all controls (W A S D, Q/E, arrows and Shift on a PC). Closing the 3D view from the free camera takes you to the Overview.
- Following an aircraft: tilt down to ground level and look ahead, and slide the view with two fingers.
- Runway markings in 3D: runway numbers (with L, C, R) at both ends on every runway, threshold stripes by width, centre line, aiming point, touchdown zone and side stripes where the runway is big enough; grass runways get numbers and edge markers. Hold short lines where taxiways meet a runway.
- Fixed the app closing when the 3D view opened with high satellite detail: imagery now fits the device's graphics memory and loads one piece at a time. The loading screen waits for the satellite imagery (longer at higher detail). If the system ever closes the app in the 3D view, it's reported on the next start and the detail steps down.
