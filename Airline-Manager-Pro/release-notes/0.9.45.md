**Airline Manager Pro 0.9.45: a more realistic cabin, window zoom and cabin sound**

- **AirlineManagerPro-0.9.45.apk**, the phone app. It installs over 0.9.44 and keeps your airline.
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.9.45.zip**, the PC app with the Windows app inside.

**Cabin**
- New overhead bins: large rounded white doors in one smooth curve, a grey service panel underneath with reading lights and air vents over every row, and light lines along the ceiling and above the windows.
- New seats: dark leather with smooth outlines, slimmer backs and padded winged headrests, each with a cloth cover in your airline's colour. Light-grey armrests with dark pads, a dark carpet and a pleated curtain at the front galley.
- Smooth, bright ceiling and walls with deep rounded window frames.
- Window seats: you sit level with the wing tip and look straight out, with the window square in front of you and the tip in the middle of it. The cabin around the seat is closed all round, so no sky shows through gaps.
- Zoom in the window views: a slider on the left (or pinch, or the mouse wheel) brings you closer to the window. It stops just short of the glass, so you can't go through. The zoom is remembered.
- Settings → Graphics → Cabin quality: Low, Medium, High or Extreme. Higher makes the curves of the bins, walls, seats, headrests and passengers smoother. Extreme is the very best: the smoothest curves, the cabin drawn at your screen's full sharpness and the sharpest textures.
- Cabins are only made while you look at them and are cleared from memory when you leave, so they cost nothing in the other views.

**Sound**
- Inside the cabin your own engines sound as they do to passengers: a deep rumble and a steady drone through the hull, the air rushing past louder as you go faster, and at a window seat the engine on that side nearer and louder. Propeller aircraft throb through the cabin, and other aircraft are muffled.
- Settings → Sound and feel → 3D view: a Cabin sound slider of its own; all the way down mutes it.
