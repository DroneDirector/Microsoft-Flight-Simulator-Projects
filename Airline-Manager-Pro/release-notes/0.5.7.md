**Airline Manager Pro 0.5.7: airport view**

- **AirlineManagerPro-0.5.7.apk**, the phone app. It installs over 0.5.6 and keeps your airline.
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.5.7.zip**, the PC app with the Windows app inside. Update it too: it reads the airport layouts from the simulator.

**Airport view**
- A map of the airport in the same style as Live operations: runways with their numbers, taxiways and stands.
- Linked to the simulator, the layout is the simulator's own, add-on scenery included. Without the link, the airport's real runways are shown with the taxiways and apron drawn in simplified form.
- Your hired and co-op pilots' aircraft push back, taxi to the runway, take off and climb out; arrivals come in on the approach, land and taxi to a stand. Your parked aircraft stand at their gates, and your own flight is shown where the simulator has it.
- The runway in use follows the wind, from the simulator's weather when linked.
- Open it from a flight's menu on the map (the airport button keeps following the same aircraft), or from an airport's card. When the aircraft flies out of the area, the world map takes over and keeps following it.
- Pinch to zoom, drag to move, and tap an aircraft to follow it.
