**Airline Manager Pro 0.9.91: a real new game**

- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-Setup-0.9.91.exe**: the installer for Windows (recommended). It installs over 0.9.90 and keeps your airline.
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.9.91.zip**: the PC app to unzip anywhere.
- **AirlineManagerPro-0.9.91.apk**: the phone app. It installs over 0.9.90 and keeps your airline.

**New game starts from nothing**
- New game always asks first, also over the demo airlines. No takes you back to the menu.
- Next you create your pilot and choose the difficulty there, then found your airline (name, codes, home base).
- When you start, everything is erased: companies, pilots, aircraft, staff, routes, flights, money, loans, logbook, news, markets and history. Only your settings and registration are kept.
- Cancel at any step and you are back on the start screen, with nothing changed.

**Continue and Load game**
- Continue takes you straight back into the game, where you were.
- Load game asks "Are you sure you want to load this save game?" first, then takes you into the game at that moment.

**Back to the title screen**
- From the game: tap the logo above Overview.
- Or tap your company name at the top and choose "Back to the title screen". The game is saved first.

**The film**
- It now opens above the clouds at sunset. That shot comes back again later in the film.
