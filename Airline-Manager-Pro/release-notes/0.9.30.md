**Airline Manager Pro 0.9.30: tyre smoke at touchdown**

- **AirlineManagerPro-0.9.30.apk**, the phone app. It installs over 0.9.29 and keeps your airline.
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.9.30.zip**, the PC app with the Windows app inside.

- In the 3D view, a puff of tyre smoke comes off the main wheels when an aircraft touches down, left behind on the runway as it rolls on and fading over a few seconds. Firmer landings make a little more smoke. For every aircraft: yours, your pilots', co-op pilots' and the other airlines'.
