**Airline Manager Pro 0.9.117: better contrails, and doors that ask where you're going**

- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-Setup-0.9.117.exe**: the installer for Windows (recommended). It installs over your version and keeps your airline.
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.9.117.zip**: the PC app to unzip anywhere.
- **AirlineManagerPro-0.9.117.apk**: the phone app. It installs over your version and keeps your airline.

**Contrails**
- **They stay behind the engines:** a trail no longer jumps in front of the aircraft after a long flight.
- **Seen from any angle:** a trail no longer disappears when you look along it from behind or in front.
- **Thinner:** they start thin and spread slowly as they age.
- **A gentle fade:** each trail fades in a short way behind the engines and fades out gradually towards its far end.
- **Long enough to see:** in dry air a trail now lasts 20–30 seconds instead of a few, so it is always long enough to see.
- **No flicker:** while it's cold enough up there, the trails stay on. A short change in speed or height no longer switches them off and on.

**Doors**
- **Where to?** The doors of your office, the Operations office and the hangars ask where you are going: the Overview, the hangars, your office, Operations or the courtroom in the city. The room you are leaving isn't offered.
- **Stay here** (or Esc) keeps you where you are.
- **Your office** also has "The hangars" in its menu.
