**Airline Manager Pro 0.9.41: landing lights from the wings, approach lights, PAPI and VASI**

- **AirlineManagerPro-0.9.41.apk**, the phone app. It installs over 0.9.40 and keeps your airline.
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.9.41.zip**, the PC app with the Windows app inside.

- Landing lights sit in the leading edge of each wing, and their light and beam start right there.
- Approach lights: on long runways 900 m of white bars with cross bars, red side bars in the last 300 m and flashing lights running towards the runway; on medium runways a shorter 420 m system.
- PAPI (four lights) beside the touchdown zone of runways from 1,800 m, VASI (two bars) on runways from 1,200 m: white above the glide path, red below, as seen from your position.
- The cabin windows glow a little less brightly at night.
