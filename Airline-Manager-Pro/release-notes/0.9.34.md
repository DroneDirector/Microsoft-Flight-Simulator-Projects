**Airline Manager Pro 0.9.34: back to the international standard for laid-out taxiways**

- **AirlineManagerPro-0.9.34.apk**, the phone app. It installs over 0.9.33 and keeps your airline.
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.9.34.zip**, the PC app with the Windows app inside.

- Laid-out taxiways (only where neither your simulator nor OpenStreetMap has real ones) use the international (ICAO) dimensions everywhere again, as in 0.9.32. The US-specific values from 0.9.33 are taken out until they can be taken directly from the FAA's own tables.
