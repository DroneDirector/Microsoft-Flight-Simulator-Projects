**Airline Manager Pro 0.9.135: a stress test you can watch, and fairer advice**

- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-Setup-0.9.135.exe**: the installer for Windows (recommended). It installs over your version and keeps your airline.
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.9.135.zip**: the PC app to unzip anywhere.
- **AirlineManagerPro-0.9.135.apk**: the phone app. It installs over your version and keeps your airline.

**Stress test**
- A live graph while it runs: frames a second, and how long the processor and the graphics chip take for each frame (measured on the graphics chip where the device allows it, otherwise worked out from the frame).
- The report shows the processor and graphics times for every stage.

**Fairer recommendations**
- On phones and tablets 30 frames a second counts as smooth (as in most games), and a few slower moments in the busiest places no longer bring the advice down to Low.
- The result never falls more than one step below what the device itself suggests, and a phone or tablet with 6 GB of memory or more is recommended at least Medium.
