**Airline Manager Pro 0.9.131: real furniture, doors that open, and the test rooms**

- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-Setup-0.9.131.exe**: the installer for Windows (recommended). It installs over your version and keeps your airline.
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.9.131.zip**: the PC app to unzip anywhere.
- **AirlineManagerPro-0.9.131.apk**: the phone app. It installs over your version and keeps your airline.

**Test rooms (title screen → Testing)**
- Your office, the Operations office and your lounge joined into one building you walk through: a staff corridor between the office and Operations, a door onto a long concourse with a glass wall onto the apron, and the lounge's sliding doors at its far end.
- Everything in the rooms is there as in the game: the desk, the chairs, the monitors, the phone, the family photo, your pet with its bed, ball and bowls. Every door opens and shuts with a click.
- A visitor walks down the corridor, knocks, comes into your office and sits down facing your desk ("A visitor" plays it again).
- The computers show their screens; you can look round the desktop and the operations screens, but nothing there changes your game.
- Buttons take you straight to each room. Exit testing goes back to the title screen.

**Furniture**
- New, rounder sofas and armchairs: soft fabric ones in the Lounge, buttoned leather in the Business lounge and your office, velvet Chesterfields with rolled arms in the Flagship lounge.
- Real-looking plants: a fiddle-leaf fig in a glazed pot, a kentia palm in a tall planter and a snake plant in a concrete bowl, in the lounges, your office and the Operations office.
- A proper coffee machine (bean-to-cup, with a touchscreen, bean hoppers and a drip tray) in the lounges and the hangar break room.
- The detail of all of these follows the graphics settings.

**Doors and signs**
- The lounge has automatic sliding glass doors with your airline's name on them; they open when you leave. They are no longer behind the coffee counter.
- The doors of the hangar's maintenance office and break room open and shut with a click, and you walk through them.
- Airport-style signs beside the doors say which room it is.

**Hangar**
- No more flickering on the roof, the trusses and the window frames.

**Your own models**
- Every new piece has its own file name in the models folder (`props/`), numbered where there are several kinds: plant-1 to plant-3, sofa-1 to sofa-3, armchair-1 to armchair-3, coffee-machine-1, door-1, door-2 and room-sign.
