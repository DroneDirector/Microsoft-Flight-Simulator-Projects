**Airline Manager Pro 0.5.5: a new start-up screen**

- **AirlineManagerPro-0.5.5.apk**, the phone app. It installs over 0.5.4 and keeps your airline.
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.5.5.zip**, the PC app with the Windows app inside. Update it too, so the Windows app gets the new start-up screen as well.

**Start-up screen**
- The app now opens with an animated start-up screen: the compass-star mark in full detail, with a turning compass bezel, a ring that draws itself and a small aircraft circling it.
- What is being loaded is shown step by step, with a progress bar drawn as a route line: a glowing tip with sparks, a moving sheen, and an aircraft that takes off at 100%.
- After five seconds the app opens on the Overview.
- Birthday greetings and "What's new" now wait until the start-up screen has finished.
