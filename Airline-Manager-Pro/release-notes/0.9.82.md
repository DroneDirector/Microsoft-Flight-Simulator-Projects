**Airline Manager Pro 0.9.82: saved at every step, five backups**

- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-Setup-0.9.82.exe**: the installer for Windows (recommended). It installs over 0.9.81 and keeps your airline.
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.9.82.zip**: the PC app to unzip anywhere.
- **AirlineManagerPro-0.9.82.apk**: the phone app. It installs over 0.9.81 and keeps your airline.

**Saved at every step**
- Everything you do is saved the moment you do it: every tap and choice, every conversation and interview, every setting, every aircraft bought, leased, rented or sold, every message, everything. The main save is written over each time, on the phone straight into the app's own file as well.
- Besides it, your device keeps five backup saves, taken in turn: one a minute while you play and one when you leave the app, each new one replacing the oldest. On the phone they are in the app's own storage; on the PC in Documents\Airline Manager Pro\backups.
- Settings > Backup and restore lists the five backup saves with their date and time; Restore takes you back to that moment.

**Your hand on the phone**
- No part of your hand goes through the phone any more, including the thumb's root at its right edge: the grip is fitted to keep a little room round the phone, and anything still touching moves the phone a few millimetres clear.
