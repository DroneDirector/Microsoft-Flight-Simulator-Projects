# 0.9.145

## Frame generation without the glitches
- Fixed a crash in the 3D view ("Cannot read properties of null") when the view moved its centre while flying on.
- The aircraft you follow no longer jumps or breaks up: the frames in between carry it along with it.
- Inside the cabin and at the window seats, everything you see moves with the aircraft, so the cabin no longer jumps
  or breaks into blocks.
- Clouds and every light's glow (aircraft, airports, vehicles, rooms) keep their own distance in the frames in between,
  so they no longer smear or slide.
- The picture behind frame generation now has edge smoothing, so it is as sharp as with frame generation off.
- More precise maths for the frames in between: no more blocky breaks far from the centre or close to the eye.
