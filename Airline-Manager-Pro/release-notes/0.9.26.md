**Airline Manager Pro 0.9.26: comfort and convenience**

- **AirlineManagerPro-0.9.26.apk**, the phone app. It installs over 0.9.25 and keeps your airline.
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.9.26.zip**, the PC app with the Windows app inside.

- Search everything: the magnifier in the top bar (or Ctrl+K, or /) finds aircraft, pilots, crew, airports, routes, flights, flight logs, news and settings, and jumps straight there.
- "While you were away": after 3 hours or more, one card with the flights flown, the money in and out, what needs you and any awards.
- The app reopens where you left it: the same screen, tabs, filters and scroll position, also after an update.
- Sort and search the fleet, pilots, crew, flight logs and routes; your choice is remembered.
- Undo for a few seconds after closing a route, dismissing someone, buying fuel or parts stock and clearing messages.
- Notifications by type: show, inbox only or off. Quiet hours with no sounds or phone notifications. Repeats are grouped ("6 flights landed").
- On the phone: swipe from the left edge to go back, swipe a message left to remove it, pull down to refresh.
- Android home screen widget: balance, next flight and aircraft in the air. Long-press your home screen → Widgets → Airline Manager Pro.
- Battery saver (automatically below 20% when not charging), text size and reduced motion in Settings → Comfort and notifications.
- Keyboard shortcuts on the PC (and on phones with a keyboard): press ? to see them.
- A short tour for new players and a tip the first time each screen opens, on the phone and the PC. Take the tour again from Settings.
- Export your flight logs and finances as a spreadsheet (CSV) or a PDF.
