**Airline Manager Pro 0.9.48: no more black squares on the ground**

- **AirlineManagerPro-0.9.48.apk**, the phone app. It installs over 0.9.47 and keeps your airline.
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.9.48.zip**, the PC app with the Windows app inside.

**3D view**
- Black squares on the ground are gone. They were pieces of satellite imagery that didn't arrive (the imagery server was busy, or had a gap). Now each piece is asked for again a few times, and if it still doesn't come, the same spot is shown from the less detailed imagery instead; the ground is never left black.
