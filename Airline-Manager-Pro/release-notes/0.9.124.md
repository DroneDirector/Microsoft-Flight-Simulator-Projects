**Airline Manager Pro 0.9.124: sit down in your lounge**

- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-Setup-0.9.124.exe**: the installer for Windows (recommended). It installs over your version and keeps your airline.
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.9.124.zip**: the PC app to unzip anywhere.
- **AirlineManagerPro-0.9.124.apk**: the phone app. It installs over your version and keeps your airline.

**Lounges**
- Every armchair, every place on the sofas and every bar stool can be clicked. It glows when you look at it ("An armchair: click to sit down"), and a click sits you down in it.
- Seated, you look around freely from the seat. You can see the room, the window and the other guests.
- To get up, walk (W A S D or the stick) or click anywhere. You stand up beside the seat.
- Seats where a guest is already sitting say "Someone is sitting there".
- Coffee from the bar still works: you get up and fetch a cup.
