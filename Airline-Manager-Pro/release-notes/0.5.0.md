**Airline Manager Pro 0.5.0: real crews, real flight times**

- **AirlineManagerPro-0.5.0.apk**, the phone app. It installs over 0.4.0 and keeps your airline (or use Settings → Updates and errors → Check for updates).
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.5.0.zip**, the PC app. It now also works when the PC is connected to your phone's hotspot.

**Crew**
- New human figures with real body proportions, natural skin, hair grown strand by strand, proper beards and moustaches, and detailed uniforms.
- Choose the proportions in Settings → General → Crew figures: Natural, In-between or Stylised.
- New beard and moustache styles (stubble, short, full, goatee, Van Dyke, pencil, chevron, walrus, handlebar, horseshoe) and pilot sunglasses.
- Figures build faster, and pictures are kept on the phone so they appear at once next time.

**Hired pilots fly in real time**
- Your hired pilots keep flying while the app is closed. When you open it, you see what happened while you were away: landings, earnings, who is resting.
- Each flight takes as long as it would in the real world for that aircraft and route: airway distance, taxi times, climb, cruise and descent speeds at each altitude, cruise level and the jet stream (westbound takes longer).
- Flight cards count down live: altitude, miles to go and time to the gate, with the aircraft moving along the pink line.
- Settings → Virtual pilots: run their flights faster (2× to 30×), and get a phone notification when one reaches the gate.

**Linking**
- Link over mobile data: turn on your phone's hotspot (or USB tethering) and connect the PC to it. The connection panel shows how you're linked.

**Smaller changes**
- Tap the balance in the top bar to open Finance.
- The sound button always stays in the top bar, muted or not.
- Settings are saved the moment you change them, and a second copy of your airline is kept on the phone.
