**Airline Manager Pro 0.9.20: sharp aerial photos and a flat sea**

- **AirlineManagerPro-0.9.20.apk**, the phone app. It installs over 0.9.19 and keeps your airline.
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.9.20.zip**, the PC app with the Windows app inside.

- 3D view: the countries' own open aerial photos near the ground, far sharper than satellite: Faroe Islands, Netherlands, Switzerland, France, Spain, Austria, Japan and the United States; Finland and Denmark with a free key (Settings → 3D view). The sharp patch follows you and gets sharper the lower you fly. Elsewhere the satellite imagery at its full detail. Every source is credited in the 3D view and in Settings → About.
- The sea is flat everywhere: spikes from errors in the height data are gone; mountains and islands are untouched.
