**Airline Manager Pro 0.9.114: contrails, a real sea, gate vehicles, your first crew free and your own models**

- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-Setup-0.9.114.exe**: the installer for Windows (recommended). It installs over your version and keeps your airline.
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.9.114.zip**: the PC app to unzip anywhere.
- **AirlineManagerPro-0.9.114.apk**: the phone app. It installs over your version and keeps your airline.

**In the 3D view**
- **Contrails:** aircraft cruising in air colder than about −40 °C leave white trails from their engines.
  - In dry air they are gone in seconds; in damp air they linger and spread for a minute or more.
  - They are lit like the sky around them, and are longer and finer on the PC.
- **Wet runways:** after rain or snow the runways turn darker, and at night their lights shine back from the wet surface. They dry out over about half an hour.
- **Glow around lights:** a soft halo round runway, taxiway, airport and aircraft lights at night. On by default on the PC.
- **Ground vehicles and jet bridges at the gate:**
  - A jet bridge at big airports, or a stairs truck at smaller ones.
  - Ground power, a belt loader with a baggage tractor and carts, a catering truck lifted to the rear door and a fuel truck under the wing.
  - De-icing in the cold, and a tug at push-back.
  - They drive in and out.
- **Wings that bend:** they droop a little on the ground and bend up with the lift in the air, more in turns and at rotation.
- **The sea and the coastlines:**
  - The sea is now a level surface of its own, and the ground runs on below it.
  - The coastline is where the two meet, along the map's own coast, smooth at every terrain detail instead of zig-zagging along the ground's grid.
  - Steep cliffs are shaded as rock instead of streaked, and the detail rings of the ground blend into each other where they meet.
  - Only the airport itself is levelled now, so no terrace is cut into the hills around it.

**Photo mode**
- **Depth of field:** soft or strong. The aircraft stays sharp; what is nearer or further away blurs by its real distance.

**Rooms on the PC**
- The ceiling lights in your office and the chandeliers in the courtroom cast real shadows.

**Crew**
- **Your first crew is on us:** a new career offers, once, exactly the crew your first aircraft needs to fly (cabin crew, mechanics and a ramp team at your home base; your co-pilot comes with the aircraft).
  - One click hires them, without recruitment or training fees; you pay their salaries as usual.
  - After that, hiring costs as before.
- **A full hiring list to start:** a new career opens with 20 of every kind looking for work: captains, first officers, cabin crew, mechanics and ground crew. Once you hire them, new people come looking over time as before.
- **Crew to fly every aircraft** (Crew): pilots, your co-pilot, cabin crew, mechanics and the ground crew at each base.
  - Each shows how many you have, how many you need and how many more to hire, with a Hire button for that kind.
  - Also listed: which aircraft can't fly now for want of people, and why.

**Your own models**
- Every aircraft type, ground vehicle, the jet bridge and the people (by gender and group of looks) has a clear file name, such as `airbus-a320neo.glb` or `vehicles/fuel-truck.glb`.
- Put a `.glb` model with that name in `www/models/` and it is used everywhere instead of the app's own. `www/models/README.md` lists every name and how the files should be made.

**Settings → Graphics → Sky and water** has a switch for each of these.
