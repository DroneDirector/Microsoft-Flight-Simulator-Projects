**Airline Manager Pro 0.9.56: boarding and deboarding in the cabin, a modern cabin, one PC app at a time**

- **AirlineManagerPro-0.9.56.apk**, the phone app. It installs over 0.9.55 and keeps your airline.
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.9.56.zip**, the PC app with the Windows app inside.

**Boarding and deboarding, seen in the cabin**
- At the gate after landing, passengers rise from their seats row by row, mostly from the front: the aisle seat first, then the middle, then the window, a few seconds apart. They step into the aisle when there is room, walk forward and fade out at the front door.
- Before departure they come in through the front door and walk to the seat on their boarding pass. They stop to stow a bag, step into the row and sit down. The back rows board first, window seats first, so nobody has to get up again.
- Nobody walks through anybody: in the aisle and the galley people keep their distance and wait until the way is clear.
- The pace is the real one: about 22 people a minute leave, and about 10 a minute board. The turnaround on the aircraft cards uses the same times, worked out from the number of passengers.
- Every row has its placard under the bins, as on board: the row number, the aisle, the seat letters from the aisle out to the window, and the window.

**A modern cabin**
- Light grey seat backs with the screen, tray table and pocket, dark headrests trimmed in your airline's colour, and blue mood lighting under the bins and along the windows. White light lines run along the ceiling.
- Some window shades are pulled down here and there. Those over the wing always stay open, so you still see it.
- The back of the cabin is closed with a wall, the rear galley curtain and an exit sign.
- Looking around from the cabin seat follows your finger or mouse, no longer inverted.

**Lights**
- The nose gear's taxi light lights the ground as the landing lights do, and reaches further.
- Taxi and landing lights go off when the aircraft stands still on the ground and at the gate.

**PC**
- An update closes the app's window and any other copy of the PC app before the new version starts, so only one is running. Windows left open from earlier sessions close when the app starts.
