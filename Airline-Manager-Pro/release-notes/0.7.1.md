**Airline Manager Pro 0.7.1: departures, arrivals and approaches**

- **AirlineManagerPro-0.7.1.apk**, the phone app. It installs over 0.7.0 and keeps your airline.
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.7.1.zip**, the PC app with the Windows app inside.

**Departures, arrivals and approaches**
- Your hired and co-op pilots now fly a departure from the runway in use (straight ahead, then a turn onto the route), an arrival (straight in, or via downwind and base) and an ILS or RNP approach down the final to the runway.
- The runway in use follows the wind. Longer paved runways at larger airports get an ILS, others an RNP approach.
- The map shows each flight's departure, route, arrival and approach, with their names when you zoom in.

**Following aircraft**
- A followed aircraft now stays in view at every zoom, however far and fast you zoom: the map and the airport use the same position for it.

**Faster at airports**
- Zoomed in on big airports the map is much faster, with the same look.
