**Airline Manager Pro 0.9.42: a steady flight card, full warning reports, crashes in the papers**

- **AirlineManagerPro-0.9.42.apk**, the phone app. It installs over 0.9.41 and keeps your airline.
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.9.42.zip**, the PC app with the Windows app inside.

- The flight card (in the 3D view and on the map) keeps its size while its numbers change, so it no longer jumps: every figure on one line, room for long phases and for the vertical speed, and the passengers, fuel and catering each in three fixed lines.
- Warning outcomes ("… didn't learn from the warning", "… took the warning to heart") open as a full report from the head of operations: who, what the warning was for, what happened, how they took it, their record, and what you can do, with their record and the original report a tap away.
- Crashes are in the newspaper: hired pilots' accidents, your own crash once you register it, and crashes from a court case. The picture shows the aircraft broken, with fire, smoke and debris.
