**Airline Manager Pro 0.9.44: true 3D clouds, real weather and storms, cabin views, catering and emergencies**

- **AirlineManagerPro-0.9.44.apk**, the phone app. It installs over 0.9.43 and keeps your airline.
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.9.44.zip**, the PC app with the Windows app inside.

**3D view: sky and weather**
- Clouds are built in three dimensions: they keep their shape and their sunny side as you circle around them, stay fixed in the world, and form or dissolve gradually when the weather changes.
- Rain and snow from the real weather reports (and from Flight Simulator's weather when linked): falling around you under a raining cloud, and in grey curtains under the clouds in the distance.
- Thunderstorms: the storm clouds flash from inside, with bolts to the ground now and then; at night the whole area lights up for an instant. Thunder follows as late as real thunder does (about 3 seconds a kilometre), heard out to about 16 km.
- Rainbows where it rains with a low sun behind you (a full bow or part of one, with the fainter second bow), and the northern and southern lights from NOAA's real aurora forecast on dark nights. Both can be switched off in Settings → Graphics.
- A more realistic sun: white-hot, with glare and bloom, flattened and redder at the horizon, softened by haze. Lens flare when you look towards it (Settings → Graphics).
- The sky's time is steady: Flight Simulator's time when linked, this device's otherwise, eased in when it changes. The light, the weather and sunset colours change gradually and only at the right time of day.
- The sea no longer shows regular lines: waves of many sizes and directions, softening into a sheen in the distance. No more dark patches in the mirror reflections, and no more spikes out of the sea near steep islands.
- Aircraft bump, roll a little and flex their wings in turbulence, from the real hazard reports, storm clouds and wind (and Flight Simulator's turbulence for your own flight).

**3D view: aircraft and airports**
- New views: Cabin (inside, looking forward over the seats, with the passengers from the passenger list in their own seats and pets on their owners' laps), and Window L / Window R (a window seat with the wing tip in the middle of the window). Cabins look like their families: Airbus, Boeing 737, 777/747, 787, regional jets and turboprops. Passengers in the cabin can be switched off in Settings → Graphics.
- The flare: the nose comes up in the last few metres, the main wheels touch first, the nose is lowered after. Thicker tyre smoke, the same on any device.
- Reverse thrust: jet airliners open their reversers two seconds after touchdown, blowing spray forward, and close them as they slow.
- The nose-wheel light is only on when the nose gear is down.
- The fuselage, engines and tail are glossy and reflect the sky.
- Smoother turns while taxiing, on the map and in 3D.
- Apron floodlights stand on proper masts, well apart, beside the stands and never on a taxiway or a stand. Parking areas are paved all over, also where the map has no apron outline.
- A clock beside the battery: Flight Simulator's local time where your aircraft is when linked, otherwise this device's time.
- Crash sites: the aircraft broken in three, fires, a smoke column drifting with the wind, wreckage and fire engines with their lights flashing; in the sea, a burning oil slick and rescue boats. They stay three days; tap the crash notice or the fire mark on the map to go there.
- The loading screen starts from 0% every time and the view stays smooth while it loads.

**Emergencies**
- Failures: when linked, your flight only gets what Flight Simulator reports (the PC app now also reads engine fires). Hired pilots' flights get the app's own, very rarely, more often on aircraft in poor condition: small faults written up for the mechanics, rarer serious ones, an engine fire rarest of all.
- An engine on fire trails flames and smoke. After an emergency landing the aircraft stops on the runway, the fire engines spray it, and after a fire it is towed to the stand.

**Catering and storage**
- Buy hot meals, other food, sweets and snacks, soft drinks and alcohol into your own storage (Finance → Storage), next to your jet fuel and spare parts. Each item shows what's in store, how long it lasts at your use, whether to order, and how its price moved (real US price indices when real-world prices are on).
- The ground crew load each flight from the storage: you can load a flight yourself while it boards (tap it on the map → Catering); otherwise they do it, and very rarely come up short, which the passengers notice. With GSX, its catering truck loads from your storage. In a co-op the storage is the co-op airline's (only the owner buys).

**The map**
- Tap a weather mark or any airport (zoomed in) for its card, with Zoom in and View in 3D.
- Tap another airline's aircraft for a short card (airline, flight, type, registration, from and to, phase, height, speed, arrival) and follow it in 3D.
- The map draws more efficiently, so it stays smooth with many layers on.

**Staff and settings**
- Settings: "3D view" is now Graphics, "Damage and realism" is now Realism, Economy moved into Realism, and sections with several parts have buttons to switch between them.
- Staff figures move a little. In a violent conduct report the person is visibly furious (ten different ways), and someone with serious trouble on record pleads to keep the job when you dismiss them (five different ways).
- Conduct reports: a slider from Never to Often (Settings → Staff → Conduct), a little less often than before by default.
- Messages from staff and ATC no longer read themselves aloud: tap play. To have them read automatically, Settings → Voices.
- Game Booster (Settings → Game Booster, off by default, remembered): the 3D view at up to 60 pictures a second, the map behind it paused; on the phone the screen stays on, the processor is held steady where supported, and Android's game mode recognises the app; on the PC the fast graphics card and higher priority.
- Full screen on the phone can be switched off (Settings → General); a swipe from the edge shows the system bars only for a moment.
- Badges burst into confetti when revealed. Engine and thunder sounds have their own switches and volume (Settings → Sound and feel).
