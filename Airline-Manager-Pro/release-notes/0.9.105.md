**Airline Manager Pro 0.9.105: choose the SID, STAR and approach**

- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-Setup-0.9.105.exe**: the installer for Windows (recommended). It installs over your version and keeps your airline.
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.9.105.zip**: the PC app to unzip anywhere.
- **AirlineManagerPro-0.9.105.apk**: the phone app. It installs over your version and keeps your airline.

**Flight planner → Runways: departures, arrivals and approaches**
- For each route, under each runway, you can choose:
  - the departure (SID) and its transition;
  - the arrival (STAR) and its transition;
  - the approach and its approach transition.
- Leave a choice on "The best one for the route" and flights pick the one that fits where they are going or coming from, as before. Change the runway and the choices below it start again from the best one.
- The waypoints of the departure, arrival and approach are drawn on the map with their names, altitude limits (≥ at or above, ≤ at or below) and speed limits. Labels never cover one another.
- New **Departure** and **Arrival** buttons on the map zoom in on each end of the route.
- Every flight on the route follows your choices: yours and your pilots'.

**Procedures where none are published**
- Outside the US, the app builds procedures from the real radio navaids around the airport when Flight Simulator hasn't supplied any:
  - departures climb straight ahead and turn towards a navaid in their direction;
  - arrivals start at a navaid and lead to the final approach course;
  - approaches have their fixes where real ones put them: about 10 nm out, the final approach fix 5.5 nm out at about 1,800 ft above the runway, and the runway.
- An airport with an ILS gets an ILS approach; the others get an RNAV approach. These are always marked "built from the navaids around it", never as published ones.
