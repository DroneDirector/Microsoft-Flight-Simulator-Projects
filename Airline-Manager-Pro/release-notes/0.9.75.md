**Airline Manager Pro 0.9.75: notifications on your phone**

- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-Setup-0.9.75.exe**: the installer for Windows (recommended). It installs over 0.9.74 and keeps your airline.
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.9.75.zip**: the PC app to unzip anywhere.
- **AirlineManagerPro-0.9.75.apk**: the phone app. It installs over 0.9.74 and keeps your airline.

**Your phone**
- The apps on the home screen sit in the middle of the screen, clear of the rounded edges, with longer names on two lines.
- While you use the phone, new mail and new messages slide down from the top as notifications, with a chime (quiet hours are kept). Tap one to open it: mail opens on that message. Swipe it up to put it away, or it goes by itself after a few seconds.
