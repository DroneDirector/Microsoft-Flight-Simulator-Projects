- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.9.71.zip**: the PC app to unzip anywhere.

**Walking**
- A real step: the heel strikes first, the knee gives a little as the weight comes on, the foot rolls and pushes off from the toes, and the knee bends up as the leg swings through with the toes lifting clear.
- The hips turn and tilt and the weight shifts from side to side; the shoulders turn against the hips; the arms swing opposite the legs, the elbow bending more on the forward swing; the head stays steady.
- Everyone walks a little differently (stride, arm swing, posture), and faster walkers take longer steps.
- In your office and Operations, in the cabin (crew and passengers) and your own feet in first person. Settings > Graphics > Animation > Walking still sets how much of it you see.

**Sitting down and standing up**
- People lean forward and take their weight on their hands as they sit down in front of your desk and as they get up again.

**Also**
- Everyone now has ankles and a spine: shoes, heads, hair, glasses, caps, badges and buttons all move with the body.
