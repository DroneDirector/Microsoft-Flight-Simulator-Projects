**Airline Manager Pro 0.9.97: a clock at the top**

- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-Setup-0.9.97.exe**: the installer for Windows (recommended). It installs over 0.9.96 and keeps your airline.
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.9.97.zip**: the PC app to unzip anywhere.
- **AirlineManagerPro-0.9.97.apk**: the phone app. It installs over 0.9.96 and keeps your airline.

**A digital clock**
- A glowing digital clock now sits at the top, next to the battery (next to the Wi-Fi when the device has no battery reading).
- It shows your device's time to the second, with a blinking colon, and the game's date under it.
- It follows the 24-hour clock setting.

**Fixed**
- The "now" line on the Schedule (in the app, on the laptop and on the Operations computer) didn't move and kept its old time. It now moves along and shows the time, every second.
