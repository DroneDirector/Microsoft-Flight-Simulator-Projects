**Airline Manager Pro 0.8.3: sim check and flight recordings**

- **AirlineManagerPro-0.8.3.apk**, the phone app. It installs over 0.8.2 and keeps your airline.
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.8.3.zip**, the PC app with the Windows app inside.

**Sim check**
- With a flight loaded, double-click **Check Flight Simulator.cmd** in the PC app folder. It reads everything the app uses from Flight Simulator and the aircraft you are in: position, engines, weights, payload stations, fuel, GSX, weather, the airports around you and the nearest airport's stands and procedures.
- Anything missing or out of place is marked with "!", for example an aircraft that loads its passengers its own way instead of through the sim.
- The report is saved in Documents\Airline Manager Pro\Diagnostics.

**Flight recordings**
- Every flight is recorded exactly as Flight Simulator sent it, in Documents\Airline Manager Pro\Recordings (the newest 30, about 1 MB an hour).
- Drag a recording onto **Play back a flight.cmd** to fly it again on the phone, without the simulator.
- Fly without recording: AirlineManagerPro-PC.exe --no-record

**Fixes**
- Add-on aircraft in Flight Simulator 2024's Community2024 folder now show up in the aircraft list.
