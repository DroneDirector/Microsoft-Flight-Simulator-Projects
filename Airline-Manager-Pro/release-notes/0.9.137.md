**Airline Manager Pro 0.9.137: your lounge in the building, always**

- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-Setup-0.9.137.exe**: the installer for Windows (recommended). It installs over your version and keeps your airline.
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.9.137.zip**: the PC app to unzip anywhere.
- **AirlineManagerPro-0.9.137.apk**: the phone app. It installs over your version and keeps your airline.

**Walking between your rooms**
- The lounge is always at the end of the concourse now, joined to your office and the Operations office: your hub's lounge, or another of yours if your hub has none. You walk to it and through its doors; no more "where to next?" when you leave it.
- Every lounge you visit from Lounges opens in the building, so you can walk from it back to your office and Operations.
- Before you own a lounge it is there, built and lit, but its doors are shut behind a "Coming soon, under construction" hoarding. It opens once you buy your first one.
