**Airline Manager Pro 0.9.111: your museum, photo mode, and the controller everywhere**

- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-Setup-0.9.111.exe**: the installer for Windows (recommended). It installs over your version and keeps your airline.
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.9.111.zip**: the PC app to unzip anywhere.
- **AirlineManagerPro-0.9.111.apk**: the phone app. It installs over your version and keeps your airline.

**Your museum (Ranking → Gifts → Walk through your museum)**
- A hall with your airline's name on the wall. Everything you've been given or earned stands on display, each piece on its own plinth under a beam of light, with a brass name plate:
  - the airports' gifts and plaques
  - your type-rating certificates, milestone plaques and medals
  - your best badges.
- Walk round with W A S D or the arrows, and drag to look. On a phone or tablet, use the on-screen arrows; on a controller, the left stick walks and the right stick looks.
- Tap a piece, or press Enter or Cross in front of it, to take it in your hands and turn it round.

**Photo mode (3D view)**
- Press the star button, or H on the keyboard. The controls go away, and a strip offers:
  - a look: Natural, Warm, Cinematic, Black and white, Vivid or Vintage
  - black bars for a wide-screen frame
  - a caption.
- Move the camera as usual (the free camera goes anywhere), then take the picture. It's kept with your screenshots. Tap **To the papers** to have it printed as the photo of the day, once a day.

**Game controllers**
- The controller already works across the whole app (the pointer, clicks, back, scrolling and pages). It now also works in the new parts: it walks you round the museum, turns gifts and badges in the 3D popup, and closes the water salute film with Circle (or B).
