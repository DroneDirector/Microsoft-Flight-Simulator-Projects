**Airline Manager Pro 0.9.57: real cabin lighting, detailed seats, passengers in full detail, Game Booster charts**

- **AirlineManagerPro-0.9.57.apk**, the phone app. It installs over 0.9.56 and keeps your airline.
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.9.57.zip**, the PC app with the Windows app inside.

**Cabin lighting**
- The blue mood lighting is real light: it lights the side walls below the bins, fading further down.
- Blue ceiling lights on top of the bins throw pools of light across the ceiling, one after another along the cabin, and the lit ceiling casts blue onto the overhead bins.
- By day the blue is on but softer. At night the white cabin light is dimmed, the white ceiling lines turn down to a faint blue, and the blue fills the walls and ceiling.
- Padding, curves and folds show more depth: surfaces facing up catch a little more light than those facing down.

**Seats**
- New detailed seats: padded back and seat cushions with side bolsters, seams pressed into the padding with stitching beside them, two-tone headrests with wings (your airline's cloth on the back), a grab handle on top, and moulded armrests curving down to the seat front with padded tops.
- Neighbouring seats share one armrest.

**Passengers**
- The passengers around you in the cabin are the same detailed figures as your staff, with the faces, hair and clothes from the passenger list. They still sit, stand up, walk and fade at the door, and follow you as you walk.
- The passenger list photos use the same figures. Seated people rest their hands in their lap.

**Cabin quality**
- Settings → Graphics → Cabin: Low, Medium, Very high (the standard) and Ultra.
- Very high draws the cabin at up to twice your screen's sharpness so edges stay smooth. Ultra goes up to three times, with the finest seats and curves.

**Game Booster**
- Live charts, updated every 2 seconds. On PC: processor, memory, graphics card, graphics memory, disk, the 3D view's frame rate and the app's memory. On the phone: memory, processor speed, temperature (and how close the phone is to slowing down from heat), frame rate and app memory. Hover over a chart to see the reading at that moment.
- On PC, tick the programs to close while you play. While the booster is on and the app is open, they are closed and kept closed: one that starts again is closed again within a few seconds. Charts show how many were closed, how many were stopped from starting again, and the memory freed. Programs with a window are asked to close as if you closed them, so one asking you to save stays open. Windows, Flight Simulator, Steam, the Xbox app, security software and drivers are never closed.
- On phones with Android 13 or older, other apps running in the background are closed every 30 seconds while the app is open. Android 14 and later don't let an app close other apps; your phone's own game booster can, and this app is listed there as a game.
