**Airline Manager Pro 0.9.32: taxiways at every airport, and a Pets section**

- **AirlineManagerPro-0.9.32.apk**, the phone app. It installs over 0.9.31 and keeps your airline.
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.9.32.zip**, the PC app with the Windows app inside.

- Airports without mapped taxiways now get a taxi system laid out from their real runways with the international (ICAO) standard dimensions: a parallel taxiway at the right distance, exits at the ends and along the runway, and an apron with stands on the terminal side. Shown in the 3D view and on the airport map. Your simulator's layout (when linked) and OpenStreetMap's still come first wherever they exist.
- Newspaper: stories about pets on board now have their own Pets section (earlier ones are moved there).
