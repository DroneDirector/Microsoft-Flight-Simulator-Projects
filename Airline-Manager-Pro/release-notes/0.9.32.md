**Airline Manager Pro 0.9.32: real taxiways from your simulator, laid-out taxiways everywhere else, and a Pets section**

- **AirlineManagerPro-0.9.32.apk**, the phone app. It installs over 0.9.31 and keeps your airline.
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.9.32.zip**, the PC app with the Windows app inside.

- Real taxiways from Flight Simulator itself: while linked, the PC app reads the layouts of the airports that matter to you (bases, routes, flights, bookings, jobs and the 150 biggest airports around your home base) from the simulator in the background, one at a time. The PC app keeps them on your PC (also usable while the simulator isn't running) and the phone keeps them for good, so the 3D view and the airport map show the exact taxiways even when you're not linked. Only on your own devices: nothing is uploaded.
- The phone now keeps up to about 1,500 airport layouts instead of 30.
- Airports with no taxiways anywhere (not in the simulator data you have yet, not on OpenStreetMap) get a taxi system laid out from their real runways with the international (ICAO) standard dimensions: parallel taxiway, exits, and an apron with stands. Replaced automatically as soon as real data arrives.
- Fixed: a hired pilot's flight could race in at four or five times its speed when an arrival procedure from the simulator had a point in the wrong place. Such procedures are now checked and replaced by the app's own arrival.
- Newspaper: stories about pets on board now have their own Pets section (earlier ones are moved there).
