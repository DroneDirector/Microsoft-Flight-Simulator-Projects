**Airline Manager Pro 0.9.33: FAA dimensions for laid-out taxiways in the US**

- **AirlineManagerPro-0.9.33.apk**, the phone app. It installs over 0.9.32 and keeps your airline.
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.9.33.zip**, the PC app with the Windows app inside.

- At airports in the United States and its territories, laid-out taxiways (only used where neither your simulator nor OpenStreetMap has real taxiways) now follow the FAA's Airport Design standards (AC 150/5300-13B): the distance from the runway to the parallel taxiway by aircraft size and approach type, with the extra distance at high airports, and FAA taxiway widths. Everywhere else the ICAO standards stay.
