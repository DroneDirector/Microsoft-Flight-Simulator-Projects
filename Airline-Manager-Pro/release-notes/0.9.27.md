**Airline Manager Pro 0.9.27: crew figures stay drawn**

- **AirlineManagerPro-0.9.27.apk**, the phone app. It installs over 0.9.26 and keeps your airline.
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.9.27.zip**, the PC app with the Windows app inside.

- The co-pilot and other crew figures no longer disappear on screens that update themselves (such as the Overview during a flight).
- After the 3D view, figures are drawn again if the phone took their graphics memory; an empty picture is never kept.
- The ATC chips on the flight card wrap instead of running out of the card.
