**Airline Manager Pro 0.5.8: repairs done when paid**

- **AirlineManagerPro-0.5.8.apk**, the phone app. It installs over 0.5.7 and keeps your airline.
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.5.8.zip**, the PC app with the Windows app inside.

**Maintenance**
- Paying for a repair now fixes the aircraft straight away: it leaves the hangar and is ready for its next flight.
- One payment covers every open report on the aircraft, so you aren't asked to pay again for the same aircraft. The button shows the total when there is more than one report.
- Aircraft that were stuck in the hangar after an earlier payment are released when you update.
- The hangar now shows grounded aircraft waiting for repair.
