**Airline Manager Pro 0.9.128: airports stay in the 3D view, Cinema, and a question before leaving**

- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-Setup-0.9.128.exe**: the installer for Windows (recommended). It installs over your version and keeps your airline.
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.9.128.zip**: the PC app to unzip anywhere.
- **AirlineManagerPro-0.9.128.apk**: the phone app. It installs over your version and keeps your airline.

**3D view**
- **Airports no longer disappear.** If the free camera had been more than 10 miles from an airport, coming back to it could leave it undrawn: runways, buildings and lights all gone. Now any airport within a few miles of the camera, or of the aircraft you're following, is drawn. That includes every airport in the world, not only the main ones, and fields along the way such as a diversion airport.
- **Cinema:** the round star button in the lower right is now a wider Cinema button with a film-camera icon. It does the same thing as before: frame your shot with everything else hidden (H).
- **Leaving asks first:** the X in the top left corner (and Esc in the free camera, the back button and the controller) now asks "Are you sure you want to exit the 3D viewer?". Choose Yes and the "Leaving the 3D viewer" screen comes up.

**Aircraft market**
- On every card, the price, View and Buy (or Lease) now sit side by side. Notes such as "You have 2" and "Not in your sim" go on a line of their own, so no button is cut off.
