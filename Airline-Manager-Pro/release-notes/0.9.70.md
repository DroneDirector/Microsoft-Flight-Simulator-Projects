**Talking**
- Mouths now make the shapes of the sounds being spoken: the lips press together on m, b and p, the lower lip tucks under the top teeth on f and v, the lips round on oo and o, spread on ee, and push forward on sh and ch. Each shape blends into the next.
- With the natural voices, the mouth follows the voice's own sounds, sentence by sentence as you hear them, and opens wider when the voice is louder. With the device's voice or when reading, it follows the words.
- Everywhere people talk: your office, the courtroom and the cabin (Settings > Graphics > Animation > Speaking: High and Ultra).
