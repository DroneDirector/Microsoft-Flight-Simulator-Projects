**Airline Manager Pro 0.9.103: flight pop-ups, who is missing, and shining metal**

- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-Setup-0.9.103.exe**: the installer for Windows (recommended). It installs over 0.9.102 and keeps your airline.
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.9.103.zip**: the PC app to unzip anywhere.
- **AirlineManagerPro-0.9.103.apk**: the phone app. It installs over 0.9.102 and keeps your airline.

**Flight pop-ups**
- A small card pops up for each step of your airline's flights as it happens: delays, taxiing out, take-off, departure, approach, landing, taxiing in and arriving at the gate. Each card names the runway and the procedure flown. Tap a card to follow that flight on the map.
- The cards work on PC, phone and tablet. On phones and tablets they sit at the top, out of the way of the other messages.
- On Android they can also come as phone notifications, which arrive even when the app is closed.
- **Settings → Flight pop-ups**: one switch turns them all off, and each step has its own switch. The cards in the app and the phone notifications have separate switches.

**Schedule: who is missing**
- When a flight is delayed or cancelled for want of people, the Schedule shows exactly what is missing:
  - the pilot (off sick and when they're back, on a course, or on strike)
  - cabin crew (how many the aircraft needs, how many are available, and how many are off sick, on strike or on leave)
  - a mechanic to release the aircraft
  - the ground crew at the airport (how many one turnaround needs, how many are at work, and how many are busy with other aircraft).
- A button takes you straight to Crew.

**Gifts, plaques and medals**
- Metal parts (brass plates, gold, silver and bronze, chrome) now have a shine that moves across them as you turn the object. Wood, glass and paint stay as they are.
