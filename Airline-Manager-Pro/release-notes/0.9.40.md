**Airline Manager Pro 0.9.40: aircraft lights as crews use them, cabin lights at night**

- **AirlineManagerPro-0.9.40.apk**, the phone app. It installs over 0.9.39 and keeps your airline.
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.9.40.zip**, the PC app with the Windows app inside.

- 3D view at night: the passenger windows glow warm, and the cockpit windows softly.
- Aircraft lights switch as airline crews switch them: navigation lights whenever the aircraft has power; the red beacons from pushback until the engines are off at the gate; the white strobes from lining up until the runway is vacated; landing lights for take-off and below 10,000 ft until the runway is vacated; the nose gear's taxi and take-off lights and the runway turn-off lights; the logo light on the ground and below 10,000 ft.
- Landing and taxi lights light up the runway and the ground ahead, and in mist or fog their beams show in the air.
- The error log counts an error that repeats within a minute on one line instead of listing it again and again.
