**Airline Manager Pro 0.9.28: taxiway lines in the 3D view**

- **AirlineManagerPro-0.9.28.apk**, the phone app. It installs over 0.9.27 and keeps your airline.
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.9.28.zip**, the PC app with the Windows app inside.

- The 3D view draws the taxiway and apron lines like the 2D map and the real airport: thin yellow centre lines along the taxiways, the lead-in lines to each stand and the stop bars. No more net of yellow lines across the whole airport.
- Stand connections were read the wrong way in the 3D view (as taxiway points instead of stands), which drew lines from every gate to the far side of the airport. Fixed at every airport.
