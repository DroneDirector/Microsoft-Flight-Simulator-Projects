# 0.9.156

- Frame generation in the rooms (your office, the operations room, the lounge, the training room, the hangar, the
  courtroom): real frames are drawn again. Since an earlier change only the first frame was real, and every
  frame after it was a made one, so the room stretched into streaks.
  Now at most 3 made frames follow each real one, wherever you are.
- Aircraft on the ground no longer jump:
  - One ground model is used everywhere: the world map at any zoom, the airport view, the 3D view and the flight
    trace.
  - Near its airport an aircraft lands on the runway, leaves by an exit, taxis along the taxiways, holds short
    where it must and parks on its own stand.
  - Leaving, it pushes back, taxis out, holds short and lines up.
  - When its route is worked out again, or the airport's layout gets more detailed, it rolls to its new path at
    taxi speed instead of snapping there.
  - Being set down at its place when it first comes into view is no longer reported as a jump.
  - Every airport and every aircraft.
- In the lounge: when you've had too much to drink, a small note at the top says you're intoxicated, with a countdown
  to when you're sober again (it counts what is still coming into your blood too).
