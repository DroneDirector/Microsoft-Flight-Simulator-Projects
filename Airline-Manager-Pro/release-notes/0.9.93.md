**Airline Manager Pro 0.9.93: your family, your pet's bowls and a fresh start**

- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-Setup-0.9.93.exe**: the installer for Windows (recommended). It installs over 0.9.92 and keeps your airline.
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.9.93.zip**: the PC app to unzip anywhere.
- **AirlineManagerPro-0.9.93.apk**: the phone app. It installs over 0.9.92 and keeps your airline.

**Your family photo**
- The photo on your desk shows you as you look now: your face, skin tone, sex and height.
- It also shows your wife, or your husband if you are a woman. You choose their look in Your pilot → "Your wife"/"Your husband": face, hair, skin tone and height.
- New height sliders for you and your partner. The man stands on the left, the woman on the right, and the two children in front.

**Your dog or cat**
- The tail is now one smooth curve, and its fur lies along it.
- It sleeps lying down in its bed, head on its paws, breathing.
- When it wakes, it gets up for real (no fade), front legs first.
- It walks about the office for about a minute, stopping to sniff and sometimes to eat or drink, and goes round furniture and you.
- Then it goes back, turns round in its bed and lies down again.
- Tap the water bowl to fill it up: water pours in.
- Tap the food bowl: you kneel down, pick it up in both hands and turn it to read the name. "Fill it up" pours kibble in.

**Messages in the rooms**
- In court: no pop-ups and no message sounds.
- In your office and Operations: a card at the top to read. It doesn't take you anywhere; leave the room to follow it up.
- On the phone in your hand, the laptop or a monitor: a notification like on a real device. Tap one about a flight and it asks "Do you want to see them on final?". Yes closes the room and opens the 3D view on that flight.

**A new career**
- After a new game, a button at the top reads "Finances are on hold · Click to start your career".
- Until you press it, nothing is booked, and buying, leasing and hiring wait.
- Pressing it opens the books. The first cost is the company's registration, and it comes round once a year.
- The profit charts of a new game show your own books, not the demo's.

**Delete all saves** (start screen → Save game)
- This deletes your game and all 20 saves. Tick the box to delete the automatic backups too; that asks once more first.
- Hold the button for ten seconds to delete. Let go and nothing is deleted.
- Afterwards the start screen offers New game and Load game only.
