**Airline Manager Pro 0.9.143: save your settings yourself**

- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-Setup-0.9.143.exe**: the installer for Windows (recommended). It installs over your version and keeps your airline.
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.9.143.zip**: the PC app to unzip anywhere.
- **AirlineManagerPro-0.9.143.apk**: the phone app. It installs over your version and keeps your airline.

**Settings**
- At the top of Settings → Graphics: save your graphics settings yourself. At the top of Settings → General: the same for all your other settings.
- Save now keeps a copy you can go back to after trying things (Go back to the saved ones). Save to a file puts them on your device; Load from a file brings them back, on this device or another one.
- The game still keeps every change by itself, as before. Nothing private (registration, sign-ins) goes into the file.
