**Airline Manager Pro 0.9.140: your computers grown up, Devices, sounds you can replace**

- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-Setup-0.9.140.exe**: the installer for Windows (recommended). It installs over your version and keeps your airline.
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.9.140.zip**: the PC app to unzip anywhere.
- **AirlineManagerPro-0.9.140.apk**: the phone app. It installs over your version and keeps your airline.

**Your computers and your phone (office, Operations)**
- The star opens a start menu over the corner: pinned apps and folders of apps (the bank in its own folder, your airline's apps, office, photos and videos, phone).
- Search on the taskbar (on the phone at the top of the home screen): apps, folders, photos, videos and documents by name.
- Photos (was Screenshots), Videos and My Documents: make folders, drag a file onto a folder (with the mouse, or press and drag on a touch screen), right-click or press and hold for Open, Rename, Move to and Delete; sort by name, date, size or date made, and filter by name.
- My Documents: make today's schedule, a weather report, a dispatch sheet or a flight report from your airline as it is; add PDFs and other files from your device (a copy: deleting it on the device doesn't touch the game's).
- Print a document or a photo here (it comes out of a printer beside you, in the office and in Operations) or on a real printer.
- Recorder: switch it on and a record button and a photo button show in every 3D place (the 3D view and its cabin, your office, Operations, the corridor and concourse, the lounges, the hangars). Videos go to Videos, photos to Photos.
- Settings on each computer and on the phone, each its own: wallpaper, colour, text size, clock; and Update, which updates the game itself.
- Mail: Inbox, Starred, Spam, Deleted and your own folders; drag a message onto a folder; right-click or press and hold for Move to, Delete, Star, Mark unread and Spam; a short guide shows the first time.

**Settings → Devices** (was Controller)
- Your controllers, keyboard, mouse and sound devices: connected now, and remembered ones that are away.
- Live: the buttons, keys and mouse buttons you press light up.
- Change what any controller button or key does, single or as a combination (hold one, press another). The controller hints in the 3D places follow your buttons.
- Mouse look speed and invert.
- Pairing: on Android, find and pair a wireless controller or headset right there; on Windows, its Bluetooth settings open.

**In every 3D place**
- A Settings button in the corner (it tucks away to a small tab): Settings opens over the scene and closing it puts you straight back where you were.
- With a controller connected, its buttons for that place show at the bottom (PlayStation symbols, combinations too).

**Sound**
- In the 3D view, circling the aircraft you follow (Behind, Around): the roar behind it, the whine in front, from its side, duller far off. New Outside noise slider beside Cabin sound.
- Every sound has a name: footsteps by floor (carpet, wood, tiles, concrete, metal), doors by what they're made of (wood, metal, glass, sliding), the vending and coffee machines. A file with that name in the sounds folder plays instead (see sounds/README.md). Wooden, metal, glass and sliding doors now sound different.

**Overview**
- The side menu is a little wider, so the names fit. The clock is plain: hours and minutes, and the date.
