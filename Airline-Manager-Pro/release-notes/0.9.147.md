# 0.9.147

## Your family photo
- Holding the photo from your desk: your body keeps still (no swaying arms) and the photo sits flush between your
  hands, as one piece with you. Tilting it (drag, mouse, touch or the controller) moves your arms and the photo
  together; put down, it is on its own on the desk again.

## The 3D view
- The aircraft switcher at the top right is gone; Cinema sits there now.
- Satellite and Other airlines are at the bottom right, where Cinema was, above the route, in the same dark blue as
  the other buttons (switched on: a light ring round them).

## Frame generation
- The cabin windows (their glass, blinds and the view through them) now move with the aircraft in the frames in
  between, so the window no longer jumps or changes colour.
- Circling the camera round the aircraft: when it swings round too far for a frame in between to show correctly, a
  real frame is drawn instead, so it no longer jumps.
