**Airline Manager Pro 0.9.19: fix for the 3D view, cleaner airports, quieter updates**

- **AirlineManagerPro-0.9.19.apk**, the phone app. It installs over 0.9.18 and keeps your airline.
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.9.19.zip**, the PC app with the Windows app inside.

- Fixed the 3D view stopping with "P.model.userData.set is not a function".
- Airports in 3D drawn from each airport's own layout as one clean ground picture laid over the terrain: concrete aprons with stand lines and numbers, asphalt taxiways with shoulders, yellow edge and centre lines and clean junctions, and runway hold short lines. Runways follow the ground and keep their markings.
- Updates from inside the app now install without asking (from the next update on: Android checks the version doing the installing), and the app opens again after the update, or a notification opens it.
- The update window no longer flashes while it downloads.
