**Airline Manager Pro 0.9.73: faces shaped to their photographs**

- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-Setup-0.9.73.exe**: the installer for Windows (recommended). It installs over 0.9.72 and keeps your airline.
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.9.73.zip**: the PC app to unzip anywhere.
- **AirlineManagerPro-0.9.73.apk**: the phone app. It installs over 0.9.72 and keeps your airline.

**Faces**
- Each face's nose and mouth are shaped to the proportions of the photographed face it wears, so the nostrils and lips sit exactly on the nose and lips.
- Softer, more natural skin highlights (no sparkle on the forehead).
- Mouths still move with the voice: still while a line is got ready, moving from the moment it is heard to its end, and from the start again each time you press Play.
