**Airline Manager Pro 0.9.98: the Schedule refreshes itself**

- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-Setup-0.9.98.exe**: the installer for Windows (recommended). It installs over 0.9.97 and keeps your airline.
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.9.98.zip**: the PC app to unzip anywhere.
- **AirlineManagerPro-0.9.98.apk**: the phone app. It installs over 0.9.97 and keeps your airline.

**Schedule**
- A new Refresh button redraws the Schedule straight away, with the latest flights, delays and cancellations.
- The Schedule also refreshes by itself every 30 seconds. A small counter on the button shows when the next refresh is due.
- This works in the app, on the office laptop and on the Operations computer.
