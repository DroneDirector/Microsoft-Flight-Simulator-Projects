**Airline Manager Pro 0.8.1: real voices**

- **AirlineManagerPro-0.8.1.apk**, the phone app. It installs over 0.8.0 and keeps your airline.
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.8.1.zip**, the PC app with the Windows app inside.

**Voices**
- The play buttons work: the mechanic, the gate agent and the head of operations read their messages aloud, word for word.
- Natural voices that sound like real people, running on your phone or PC, without the internet. Download them once in Settings → Voices (about 100 MB). Until then, the phone's or PC's own voice reads the messages.
- The mechanic's words are prepared while you fly, so he speaks straight away after landing. Pressing play again starts at once.
- Try each voice in Settings → Voices.
