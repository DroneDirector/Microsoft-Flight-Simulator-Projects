**Airline Manager Pro 0.9.138: cabin windows you can see through, shades you can pull**

- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-Setup-0.9.138.exe**: the installer for Windows (recommended). It installs over your version and keeps your airline.
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.9.138.zip**: the PC app to unzip anywhere.
- **AirlineManagerPro-0.9.138.apk**: the phone app. It installs over your version and keeps your airline.

**Walking between your rooms**
- Operations and the lounge: walking in and moving about inside them works properly now. The view faces the way you walk, as in your office.

**Cabin windows**
- Every window along the cabin now has a real opening in the wall, so you see out of all of them, with nothing in the way and no flicker.
- Each window sits firmly in the cabin's curved wall, turned to follow it, just like the window at your seat.
- From the front of the cabin to just behind the wing, click a window to pull its shade down, and click it again to bring it up. It moves at a gentle pace, and you can keep walking while it does. Not all of them are open to start with.
- Further back, the shades are down.
- At your window seat, click your own window to pull its shade down or up. The windows on either side look out too now.
- A shade that is up stays tucked out of sight behind the top of the window.
