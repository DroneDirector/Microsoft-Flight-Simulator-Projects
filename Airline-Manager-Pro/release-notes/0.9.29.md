**Airline Manager Pro 0.9.29: aircraft stop with the nose wheel on the mark**

- **AirlineManagerPro-0.9.29.apk**, the phone app. It installs over 0.9.28 and keeps your airline.
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.9.29.zip**, the PC app with the Windows app inside.

- On the ground every aircraft is now placed by its nose wheel, as pilots steer it: the nose wheel follows the taxiway line and stops on the end of the stand's lead-in line, so aircraft no longer stand into terminal buildings. In the 3D view and on the 2D airport map.
- In the air the aircraft is placed by its middle again, blended over the last 30 m before touchdown and after lift-off, so nothing jumps.
