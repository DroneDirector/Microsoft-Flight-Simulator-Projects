**Airline Manager Pro 0.9.47: PC updates, walk the cabin, full cabins**

- **AirlineManagerPro-0.9.47.apk**, the phone app. It installs over 0.9.46 and keeps your airline.
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.9.47.zip**, the PC app with the Windows app inside.

**The PC app updates itself**
- On the PC, Update now downloads the new PC version (not the phone's), closes the PC app, puts the new version in place and opens it again. Your airline and settings are kept.
- This version needs installing by hand once (unzip it over the old folder); from then on, updates install from the app.

**Walk the cabin**
- A new 3D view next to Free camera: walk about inside the aircraft in first person while it flies, taxis or stands at the gate. You move with the aircraft, so you can't fall out of it.
- PC: W A S D or the arrow keys to walk, the mouse to look (click the view; Esc lets go), Shift to walk faster. Phone: the stick at the bottom left to walk, drag to look.
- Walk the aisles and the galleys; seats, walls, the crew and the trolley are in the way as they would be. Tap the crew and passengers to hear them here too.

**Full cabins**
- The cabin has as many rows as the aircraft really has, and the passengers from the passenger list fill them all through the cabin: a Boeing 777 with 312 passengers shows 312 people, not a few full rows at the front.
