**Airline Manager Pro 0.9.58: a smooth cabin on every device, cabin settings part by part**

- **AirlineManagerPro-0.9.58.apk**, the phone app. It installs over 0.9.57 and keeps your airline.
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.9.58.zip**, the PC app with the Windows app inside.

**Cabin speed and memory**
- Fixes the cabin view running slowly, and Android closing the app in the cabin on phones and tablets because the graphics ran out of memory.
- The seats near you are drawn in full detail (padding, seams and stitching); further away a lighter seat that looks the same from a distance. Seats fade from one to the other as you move, nothing pops in or out.
- Hair on the lighter figures is made of fewer strands, and the cabin crew follow the cabin quality: the hair alone made one crew member as heavy as a whole cabin of seats.
- Fewer detailed passengers on phones and tablets. They are built while you stand still, so walking stays smooth, and fade in and out instead of swapping.
- Phones and tablets draw the cabin at the screen's own sharpness; only the PC draws it finer.
- The cabin keeps its frame rate smooth: while it is low, the cabin is drawn a little less sharply until it is smooth again.
- Devices with 4 GB of memory or less start on Medium cabin quality.
- On the PC, Automatic uses the high settings: every seat detailed from Very high, more and finer detailed passengers, and the cabin drawn up to twice your screen's sharpness. Every choice up to the highest is open.
- Ultra is unchanged: every seat detailed, up to 48 detailed passengers with full hair, and up to three times your screen's sharpness.

**Graphics → Cabin, part by part**
- Detailed seats, sharpness, keeping the frame rate smooth, mood lighting, passengers in the cabin, the number of detailed passengers and how detailed each one is. Each starts on Automatic, which follows the cabin quality and shows what it is now.
