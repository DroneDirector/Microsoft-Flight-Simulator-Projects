**Airline Manager Pro 0.9.80: your phone's keyboard**

- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-Setup-0.9.80.exe**: the installer for Windows (recommended). It installs over 0.9.79 and keeps your airline.
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.9.80.zip**: the PC app to unzip anywhere.
- **AirlineManagerPro-0.9.80.apk**: the phone app. It installs over 0.9.79 and keeps your airline.

**Typing on the phone**
- Messages and calls on the phone in your office and in Operations now have the phone's own keyboard on the screen: tap the letters with the mouse, a finger or the controller. Shift for one capital (tap it twice for caps lock), 123 for numbers and symbols, an emoji, space, delete and send. A new sentence starts with a capital.
- The keyboard button beside the message box hides it and shows it again (remembered); on a PC you can still type on your own keyboard too.

**Mouse and PlayStation 5 controller**
- Everything on the office and Operations screens and on the phone can be clicked with the mouse, and with the controller's cursor (left stick, Cross to tap; the d-pad jumps from key to key on the phone's keyboard).
- With the phone in your hand, Cross brings its screen up. Circle steps back: the phone's screen goes down, then the phone, then whatever is open, then you leave the room.

**Fixed**
- Saying something in your own words on a call no longer also sends it as a text message.
