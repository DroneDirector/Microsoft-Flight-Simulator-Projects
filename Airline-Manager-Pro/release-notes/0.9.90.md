**Airline Manager Pro 0.9.90: the start screen**

- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-Setup-0.9.90.exe**: the installer for Windows (recommended). It installs over 0.9.89 and keeps your airline.
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.9.90.zip**: the PC app to unzip anywhere.
- **AirlineManagerPro-0.9.90.apk**: the phone app. It installs over 0.9.89 and keeps your airline.

**A start screen with its own soundtrack**
- After the start-up screen, a film plays to the game's own music (4 min 17 s), cut to the beat in an 80s neon look. It starts again when the music ends.
- The film follows a day in order: at the stand with the ground power unit, the pushback with its flashing beacons, the flight deck as it is pushed back, cruising above the clouds at sunset, and the operations desk.
- Between the wide shots, close-up reveals light up the aircraft out of the dark: the tail, the engines, the nose gear and tow bar, the nose and the wing tip.
- Captions, light leaks, flares and film grain move in time with the music.

**The menu, on the left**
- **Continue** takes you into your office. On a first start, **Explore the demo** is shown instead.
- **New game** goes straight to creating your pilot, then your airline. If a game is already running, it asks first, and **No** takes you back.
- **Save game** saves to up to 20 slots. Each slot shows the airline, the pilot, the date, the money and the aircraft.
- **Load game** goes back to any save, or to an automatic backup. Money, staff, markets and the other airlines go back to that moment, and every aircraft is where it was, in the air or on the ground. You land in your office.
- **Options** has every setting from the game, in the start screen's colours.
- **Quit** uses the same hold-to-close button as the game.
- The version number is in the bottom left corner.

**Music on or off**
- Use the button in the bottom right of the start screen.
- Or use Options (or Settings in the game) → Sound and feel → Start screen music.
