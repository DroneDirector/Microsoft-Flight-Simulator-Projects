**Delays**
- Now and then, about 2 to 3 flights in 100, a hired or co-op pilot's flight is delayed, for a real reason:
  - at the gate: de-icing (in winter), a late inbound crew, a departure slot, a technical check, a baggage offload, fog at the destination, thunderstorms on the route, strong crosswind;
  - at the holding point: a departure queue, a runway inspection, bird activity, a runway change;
  - before landing: a holding pattern for arrival traffic, weather or a short runway closure;
  - on final: a go-around and a second approach.
- Strong wind at the airports makes delays more likely; de-icing only happens in winter at higher latitudes.
- You see it on the map (holding patterns are flown as circles), at the airport, in the arrival times and in a notification.
