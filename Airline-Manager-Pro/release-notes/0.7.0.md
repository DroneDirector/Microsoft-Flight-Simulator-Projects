**Airline Manager Pro 0.7.0: zoom from the world down to the airport**

- **AirlineManagerPro-0.7.0.apk**, the phone app. It installs over 0.6.0 and keeps your airline.
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.7.0.zip**, the PC app with the Windows app inside. Update it too.

**One map, from the world to the stand**
- Zoom in on the world map and the dots give way to real coastlines, water and borders in the same look, then main roads, and finally each airport's real layout in place: runways, taxiways, aprons, terminals and stands, with your aircraft taxiing, taking off and landing.
- The airport button in a flight's menu and "Airport view" on an airport's card now fly the map down to the airport and keep following the aircraft, also when it takes off and flies on.
- Works on the satellite map too. Offline, the dotted map and the airports you have opened before still work.

**Bases and stands**
- Every aircraft has a base, and an airport has only as many stands as it really has. Buying or leasing more aircraft than the base has room for is not possible: choose another base.
- Aircraft already over the limit are listed in Fleet with "Choose base". Until they have a stand they are refused take-off and can't board passengers, and you get a notification for each.

**Airport layouts**
- Stands are read correctly (Vágar now has its 7 stands, 1, 2, 3, 3A, 4, 5 and 6) and drawn as lead-in lines with stop bars and numbers.
- More map servers, with a retry, and clear messages when an airport isn't mapped in detail or the servers are busy, with "Try again".
- Layouts from your simulator are kept on the phone.

**Also new**
- Battery indicator next to the PC link: charge level, charging, and wireless charging.
- The flight menu bubble widens to fit all its buttons.
