**Airline Manager Pro 0.9.119: the hangar's computer, fixed**

- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-Setup-0.9.119.exe**: the installer for Windows (recommended). It installs over your version and keeps your airline.
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.9.119.zip**: the PC app to unzip anywhere.
- **AirlineManagerPro-0.9.119.apk**: the phone app. It installs over your version and keeps your airline.

- **Switching off the hangar's monitor:** the power button now takes you back into the hangar. Before, you couldn't get away from the screen.
- **Leaning in:** as the hangar monitor's screen comes up you lean in to it, and back out as it goes, as in the Operations office.
- **Places on the computer:** My office, Operations, Hangars and the courtroom open from the computer in any room. You leave the room you're in (through its loading screen) and go there. Picking the room you're already in just takes you back into it.
- **Walk in from the computer:** "Walk in" on the computer's Hangars list takes you from the room you're in straight into that hangar.
