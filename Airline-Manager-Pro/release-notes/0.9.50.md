**Airline Manager Pro 0.9.50: the courtroom, crash reports with the damage, windows that fit the PC screen**

- **AirlineManagerPro-0.9.50.apk**, the phone app. It installs over 0.9.49 and keeps your airline.
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.9.50.zip**, the PC app with the Windows app inside.

**In court, in person**
- When an accident with deaths is blamed on how the airline is run (an aircraft flown with known defects or in poor condition, mechanics underpaid, too few, tired or not qualified, staff warnings ignored), the airline is taken to court, and you answer for it yourself.
- The hearing runs over a couple of days in a 3D courtroom: a panelled hall with tall windows, a coffered ceiling and chandeliers, the judges on their bench, the clerk, the witness box, the prosecution, you and your lawyer at the defence table, counsel for the families, the relatives in the front row, the public and the press, and police at the doors. Everyone breathes, sways gently and looks at whoever is speaking.
- Day one: the charges and the evidence. The investigation report and your own records (open defects, the aircraft's condition, mechanics' numbers, qualifications and pay) are shown as exhibits, and the judge and the prosecutor question you.
- Day two: the witnesses: your chief mechanic, a surviving passenger, a relative of a victim.
- Day three: the families' settlement offer, your plea, the closing speeches and your last word. Then the verdict, read out in court. An appeal gets its own hearing before five judges.
- Every line can be played aloud or read. Your answers are checked against the records: honesty counts in your favour, an answer the records contradict counts against you, and every answer moves your company rating. Your lawyer suggests answers and, if good enough, can object to the prosecutor's questions.
- You get a reminder before each day. Miss a day and it goes ahead without you, which counts against you.
- Esc (or Leave court) before the court rises: a board member must stand in for you for the rest of the case, for a large sum. The big decisions (the settlement, the plea, an appeal) still come to you.
- Afterwards: a court-ordered safety audit, closer inspections and a higher insurance premium for a while; you can fund a memorial for the victims. Each day is in the papers.
- Settings → Court → Court hearings switches it all off. It is on to begin with.

**Crash reports**
- A crash report now shows the damage (minor, substantial structural damage, or destroyed and written off), the damaged parts, how many died and were injured, and the repair cost and time or the value lost.
- "See the damage" shows the aircraft where it came to rest in 3D, with the parts that broke off lying near it.
- Registering a crash costs what the damage costs; a destroyed aircraft is written off. Crashes with deaths, and destroyed aircraft, make the news.

**PC**
- Every window, panel and menu fits the screen. Windows sized to the screen were made too large on big or high-resolution monitors and TVs; now they follow the scaled app. Menus opened from a button stay inside the window. Windows, and the settings, are a little narrower.

**3D view**
- Water only shows on flat ground at water level, never on land or slopes.
- The floodlights on the apron light a pool three times as wide.
- Airport buildings have facades: glass curtain walls on terminals, rows of windows on offices, lit at night.
- Aircraft flown by the app keep clear of the terrain around airports such as Vágar.
- While linked to the simulator, the real approach and departure procedures are fetched for your network and for the Nordic airports, the Faroes, Iceland and Greenland among them.
