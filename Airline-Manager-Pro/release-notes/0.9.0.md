**Airline Manager Pro 0.9.0: 3D view, real aircraft and a living turnaround**

- **AirlineManagerPro-0.9.0.apk**, the phone app. It installs over 0.8.3 and keeps your airline.
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.9.0.zip**, the PC app with the Windows app inside.

**3D view**
- A **3D** button among the map controls shows the aircraft you follow in 3D, flying over the real ground: mountains and valleys at their real heights, painted like the world map, with cyan coastlines.
- Near an airport you see the airport too: runways with their markings, taxiways, aprons, terminals and every aircraft on the ground.
- The camera follows the aircraft all the time. Drag to look around it, pinch or scroll to zoom. Behind, Around and Tower views.
- Tap any aircraft for its flight details. Flaps and landing gear move as the aircraft configures for take-off and landing.
- Light on the battery: it only draws while it's open, at most 30 times a second.

**Aircraft that look like themselves**
- Every aircraft type is now built from its real dimensions: length, span, wing sweep, engines, tail, nose and windscreen, wingtips (Sharklets, blended and raked wingtips, fences), landing gear, floats and struts.
- The pictures in the fleet, hangar, market and sales are rendered from these models, in your airline's livery.
- **Livery designer** (Fleet → Livery, or Settings → your airline): six designs, your colours for body, underside, tail and engines, your airline's name on the aircraft and the star on the fin.

**Flights**
- Turns along the route are smooth arcs, as a real aircraft flies them, still following the route.
- Each aircraft lands on the runway its approach was planned for, even when the wind changes the runway in use.
- Your own flight before the simulator is linked now flies at its real speed along its real procedures (it used to cross the map in 15 minutes).
- Following an aircraft keeps your zoom and stays on, even after dragging the map or visiting another screen, until you tap Follow again.
- "Arrives in" counts down from take-off to touchdown, goes below zero when the flight runs late, and shows 0:00 on the ground.
- Flight details show heading, vertical speed and ground speed, and can be dragged anywhere on the map by their top line.
- Live on-board numbers: passengers getting off and on, cleaning, catering, refuelling and bags during the turnaround, and the fuel burning off in flight.
- Each aircraft keeps its stand from taxiing in until its next push-back, stays visible there, and no two aircraft share a stand.

**Maintenance**
- A repair you pay for starts as soon as the aircraft lands, and the hired pilot waits until it's out of the hangar.
- A report can only be paid once: the button turns into "Paid" with the minutes left, and the report leaves your notifications.
- The repair time the mechanic quotes, in writing and out loud, is the time the hangar really takes.

**Voices**
- Every staff message can be played: pilots, head of operations, finance, the aviation authority, the company lawyer and the mechanic.
- When sounds are muted, the line under the play button says so, with a button to turn them back on.
- If the natural voice is slow to start, the device's own voice speaks instead of nothing happening.

**Flight trace and logs**
- Settings → Updates and errors → Flight trace: how every aircraft flies, every 30 seconds while the app is open (phase, altitude, planned speed and real speed over the ground, heading, position, runway).
- Glitches are marked: jumps, too fast, touching down off the runway or on another runway, and arriving without a touchdown.
- Save the log file: the flight trace and the error log in one text file.

**Credits**
- 3D terrain heights from Terrain Tiles on AWS (SRTM, GMTED2010, ETOPO1 and national elevation models); the full list is in Settings → About.
