**Cabin windows**
- New windows in the jets' cabins, made after real ones: a deep, soft, rounded surround in satin white, curling in to a deep reveal; a thin grey seal round the pane; a pull-down shade with its lip and grip.
- An open shade shows just its lip at the top of the window; a shut one comes down to just above the bottom, its grip showing.
- In the window-seat views your shade is up and the neighbours' are part or all the way down. Along the whole cabin, most shades are up, some halfway and a few down, and more are down at night.
- They are part of the cabin and follow Cabin quality, from Low to Ultra. Very high and Ultra are the finest, for strong PCs. On phones and tablets they stay smooth and round, never jagged, while the cabin's windows are drawn together for speed.
