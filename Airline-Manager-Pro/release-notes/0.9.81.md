**Your phone**

**The Operations monitor**
- Naturally sharp (twice the pixels on the PC), and laid out for an ultra-wide screen: the next departures on the left (time, route, aircraft, and whether it's on time, delayed, in the air or cancelled), and on the right a live timeline from two hours back to ten hours ahead, so the flights keep their proportions instead of a whole day stretched across the screen.
