**Airline Manager Pro 0.9.116: hangars that follow your maintenance**

- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-Setup-0.9.116.exe**: the installer for Windows (recommended). It installs over your version and keeps your airline.
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.9.116.zip**: the PC app to unzip anywhere.
- **AirlineManagerPro-0.9.116.apk**: the phone app. It installs over your version and keeps your airline.

**Hangars**
- **Where to find them:** Overview → Hangars (at the bottom, with My office and Operations), or from inside your office ("The hangars"). They open on their own page.
- **What's in them:** an aircraft stands in a hangar only while its repairs are being done.
  - Each base always has one hangar of every size to walk into, even when it is empty.
  - When more aircraft of a size are in for work, there are more hangars of that size, one aircraft to a hangar, and you can walk into each.
  - An aircraft only goes into a hangar its size: small, medium, large or very large.
- **Your mechanics:** the mechanics in a hangar are the ones working on that aircraft. They are chosen when the work starts (from those based there, as many as the aircraft needs) and stay on it until it is done. The list shows who is on each aircraft and when it will be ready.
- **One at a time:** only the hangar you walk into is loaded, and it is cleared from memory when you leave. What you put down in it is still there when you come back, for 48 hours.
- **Splash screens:** walking in has its own loading screen with a progress bar. It stays at least 5 seconds, and longer if loading isn't finished. Leaving has one too.
- **Drinking and eating:** the can or bar is now in view as you drink or take a bite.

**Your own models**
- **Every piece of equipment and furniture** in the hangars has its own file in `hangar-props/`: tool trolley, work stand, traffic cone, workbench, fire extinguisher, waste bin, office desk, office chair, keyboard, mouse, bookshelf, kitchen counter, sink, coffee machine, coffee mug, drinking glass, break room table and chairs.
- **The tug** by the doors is the same one as on the apron (`vehicles/pushback-tug.glb`).
- **Putting things down:** tables, counters and trolleys still work for this when you replace them.
- `www/models/README.md` lists every name.
