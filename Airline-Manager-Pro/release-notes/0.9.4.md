**Airline Manager Pro 0.9.4: runways, seats and real message times**

- **AirlineManagerPro-0.9.4.apk**, the phone app. It installs over 0.9.3 and keeps your airline.
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.9.4.zip**, the PC app with the Windows app inside.

**Runways decide where each aircraft can go**
- Every type knows the runway it needs for take-off and landing, the narrowest runway it may use and whether it can use grass or gravel. The real runways of every airport are checked.
- The distance needed follows the real weight of each flight and the airport's height. A 747 can't use Vágar at all; a 737 leaving Vágar with a full load needs more runway than there is, so it takes fewer passengers.
- Routes that can't be flown aren't created, and the reason is shown with the numbers. Hired pilots, charters and jobs follow the same rules.

**Passengers each aircraft takes**
- New, used and lease cards and the aircraft details show the typical seats (in classes), all-economy and the certified maximum, next to the seats in your own layout.

**Messages**
- Messages that came in while the app was closed show the real time of what happened: the landing, the finished repair, the expired job, the check ride, the paid-off loan. The list is in that order.

**Staff**
- Tap Conduct on an employee with warnings to see each warning: when, how serious, what happened, and play the report.
