**Airline Manager Pro 0.9.10: co-op mode, the newspaper and live screens**

- **AirlineManagerPro-0.9.10.apk**, the phone app. It installs over 0.9.9 and keeps your airline.
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.9.10.zip**, the PC app with the Windows app inside.

**Co-op mode**
- Co-op is now its own world: a separate airline with its own aircraft, crew, money, maintenance and logs, flown by real players. Switch to it under Co-op; switch back and your own airline is exactly as you left it.
- Players connecting: who joined, is connecting, connected, failed to connect (and why) or left, with their country. App problems behind a failed connection go to the error log.
- Give a job to a real player in co-op (they accept or decline, and you hear why). Out of co-op, jobs go to AI pilots, also ones in the air, who fly it after landing.
- Flight logs and finance show your flights, everyone's or one player's. Crew lists the real players; rankings show them in co-op only.

**News**
- New Newspaper section in the menu with every article. Stories for new airlines and divisions, deliveries, strikes, milestones, diversions, fuel dumps, celebrities, bans, fines, reviews, crew records, whistleblowers and co-op players.
- Settings → News: articles open by themselves, only in the inbox (animated when opened), or without animation.

**Staff**
- Formal warnings now play out: most people learn, some don't, and a few push back. Give a final warning or dismiss them. Warning the same person again and again makes passengers talk, can reach the papers, and a mechanic who never learns makes mistakes on the aircraft.
- Commended crew reply with a thank-you, in text and voice.

**Screens**
- Screens that update by themselves no longer flash: only what changed changes, and changed text flips like a departure board.
- The menu on the left scrolls when it doesn't fit, with arrows showing there is more.
- Diverted flights show "Diverted to …" in Active flights; tap to see the aircraft on the map. Status badges take you to the aircraft on the map.
- After buying, leasing or buying used, "See it in the fleet" scrolls to the new aircraft and its card flashes.
- Buying, leasing and selling aircraft ask "Are you sure?" first.

**Passengers and flights**
- Every passenger has a card; passengers wear their own clothes. Bans for months, years or life. Pet passengers can be visited, also in the hold.
- Loadsheet stays just under the maximum weights, green or red, with the weight now updating in flight. Heavy aircraft after a diversion dump or burn fuel before landing.
- Passengers walk to and from aircraft parked away from a gate, with their pets.
- 3D view: a flat sea, water shown as land where the map disagrees, a satellite quality slider, and pop-ups above the 3D view.
- Fixed a crash when tapping a co-op flight on the map.
