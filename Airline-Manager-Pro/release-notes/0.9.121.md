**Airline Manager Pro 0.9.121: borrowed crew, and Hire that works**

- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-Setup-0.9.121.exe**: the installer for Windows (recommended). It installs over your version and keeps your airline.
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.9.121.zip**: the PC app to unzip anywhere.
- **AirlineManagerPro-0.9.121.apk**: the phone app. It installs over your version and keeps your airline.

**Crew**
- **Hire works:** the Hire buttons under "Crew to fly every aircraft" now take you straight down to the hiring list, showing only that kind of crew. Before, the list opened off screen, so it looked as if nothing happened.
- **More people looking for work:** more flight attendants, mechanics and ramp staff are on the hiring list.
- **Borrow crew from other airlines:** when you're short of flight attendants, mechanics or a ramp team, a Borrow button sits next to Hire.
  - Three airlines offer you people for 30 days, each at its own price: 2.5 to 5 times what your own crew are paid, paid weekly with the salaries.
  - They work like your own people and count towards the crew your fleet needs.
  - "Borrowed crew" in Crew shows who you have, from whom, what they cost and how long is left. Renew for another 30 days, or send them back.
  - You're told 3 days before a contract ends. If it isn't renewed, they go back to their airline that day.
