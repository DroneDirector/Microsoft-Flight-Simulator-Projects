**Airline Manager Pro 0.9.1: voices that start at once**

- **AirlineManagerPro-0.9.1.apk**, the phone app. It installs over 0.9.0 and keeps your airline.
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.9.1.zip**, the PC app with the Windows app inside.

**Voices**
- Pressing play starts the voice straight away: every line on screen, the mechanic's open reports and your unread messages are got ready in the background as soon as they appear.
- Lines already spoken are kept on the device, so they play at once again, also after the app was closed.
- A line you press goes ahead of anything being got ready, and the first words are made first, so even a new line starts quickly.
- One voice for the whole message: a message is never finished in a different voice.
- When the speaker is done, the playback stops and the button returns to play.
