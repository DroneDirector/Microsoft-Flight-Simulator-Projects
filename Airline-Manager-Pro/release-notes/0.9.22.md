**Airline Manager Pro 0.9.22: real-world prices, badge reveals and a world map**

- **AirlineManagerPro-0.9.22.apk**, the phone app. It installs over 0.9.21 and keeps your airline.
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.9.22.zip**, the PC app with the Windows app inside.

- Jet fuel and spare parts prices now follow the real world: jet fuel from the US Energy Information Administration, aircraft parts from the US Bureau of Labor Statistics, updated daily. Turn it off in Settings → Economy for the simulated market.
- Upcoming price changes: the Finance markets card shows the EIA's jet fuel outlook for the coming months, and you get a notice when a noticeable rise or fall is expected. For spare parts it shows where the last year's trend leads.
- New badges open with a card reveal animation, and you can replay it by tapping a badge.
- My career has a world map of every place you've flown.
- 3D view: aerial photos cover the whole area around you (also when the satellite imagery fails to load).
