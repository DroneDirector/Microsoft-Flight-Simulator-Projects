**Airline Manager Pro 0.9.84: fewer conduct reports**

- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-Setup-0.9.84.exe**: the installer for Windows (recommended). It installs over 0.9.83 and keeps your airline.
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.9.84.zip**: the PC app to unzip anywhere.
- **AirlineManagerPro-0.9.84.apk**: the phone app. It installs over 0.9.83 and keeps your airline.

**Conduct reports**
- Far fewer: most days there are none. On a day with reports there is usually one, sometimes two, rarely three, and a few days pass in between. They come at random times through the day. Settings > Staff > Conduct still makes them rarer or more frequent.
- Most are light (late for a shift, a missed call), some are moderate, and only now and then is one severe. The rare police and court cases only come from severe ones.
- Reports already waiting for you stay until you deal with them.

**Fixed**
- In Operations the phone lies on the desk next to the mouse mat (it sank into the desk under the mat).
- The time to arrival in the small flight card no longer breaks over several lines.
