**Airline Manager Pro 0.9.65: a proper Windows installer, free mode everywhere, the right airport at Vágar**

- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-Setup-0.9.65.exe**: the new installer for Windows (recommended).
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.9.65.zip**: the PC app to unzip anywhere, as before.
- **AirlineManagerPro-0.9.65.apk**: the phone app. It installs over 0.9.64 and keeps your airline.

**Installing on Windows**
- Setup has its own window in the app's look: choose the drive and folder (a folder named Airline Manager Pro is made inside it), see the space needed and free, then a progress bar as it installs.
- A shortcut on your desktop every time, wherever Windows keeps your desktop (OneDrive too); any older Airline Manager Pro shortcut is replaced. A Start menu entry too, if you like.
- Airline Manager Pro appears in Settings > Apps, and removing it there takes away every file, the shortcuts and the folder itself. Your airline is kept, unless you choose to delete it as well.
- The app's compass-star icon on the programs and the shortcuts.

**Walking about and the free camera**
- On the PC the view takes the mouse: no pointer, and you can turn round as far as you like. Esc gives the mouse back.
- What you can use glows softly when you look at it: your chair and the door in your office, crew and passengers in the cabin; in Operations, under the mouse.
- Hide or show the controls for a clean view: the button, H, or R3 on a controller.

**3D view**
- The cameras keep working after a flight has ended: the aircraft stays parked and you can still look around it from every camera.
- Look all the way round from the cockpit, the cabin and the tower.
- Approach lights at every airport (Vágar's runway, 1 m short of the old minimum, had none), PAPI from 1,500 m.
- Landings come down smoothly: aircraft are no longer held above the hills beside the runway and then dropped onto it.
- Vágar and other airports no longer show another airport's layout: answers from Flight Simulator can't be mixed up any more, layouts are checked against their airport, and a layout that arrives late is drawn at once. New error codes in Settings > Updates and errors: APT-01 (another airport's layout refused) and APT-02 (the airport changed while in view), ready to copy.
- The flight card can be minimised to a slim bar (on the map, the Overview and in 3D) and expanded again.

**Cabin**
- The overhead bins reach the ceiling all along.
- Passengers sit on their seats, legs clear of the seat.

**Controller**
- The PlayStation touchpad turns the view in 3D, the offices and the courtroom; two fingers zoom.
- The D-pad changes sliders and lists under the pointer; the controller works in the Operations office.

**Premium (demo)**
- Settings > Satellite: HD satellite imagery as a Premium subscription, shown as a demo. It can't be bought yet.
