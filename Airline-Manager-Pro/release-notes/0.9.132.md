**Airline Manager Pro 0.9.132: 2D clouds, and quicker rooms on phones**

- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-Setup-0.9.132.exe**: the installer for Windows (recommended). It installs over your version and keeps your airline.
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.9.132.zip**: the PC app to unzip anywhere.
- **AirlineManagerPro-0.9.132.apk**: the phone app. It installs over your version and keeps your airline.

**Clouds: 3D or 2D (Settings, Graphics)**
- New choice between 3D clouds (built in three dimensions, as before) and 2D clouds: flat cloud layers that are lighter to draw on slower phones and PCs.
- 2D clouds come from the same real-world weather reports: the cover, the height of each layer, rain, snow and storms are all there. They might not show the real weather as closely as 3D clouds, since heaps and towering storm clouds become flat layers; a note in the settings says so.
- Switching between them reloads the 3D view, with its loading screen, once you leave the settings (not while you are still in them), and you are back where you were.

**Quicker on phones and tablets**
- The lounge's armchairs and the seats in the test rooms' concourse are drawn all together instead of one by one: the same looks, far less work for the phone.
- Furniture that appears more than once is built once and shared.
- On the lightest graphics settings your office and the Operations office use two wider ceiling lamps instead of five, and the test rooms' corridor and concourse fewer lamps; the rooms stay well lit.

**Fixed**
- A 3D view that was reloaded no longer closes itself with "The 3D view lost its graphics".
