**Airline Manager Pro 0.9.67: your laptop, accident investigations, your own hands and feet, and the PC app with its own browser engine**

- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-Setup-0.9.67.exe**: the installer for Windows (recommended). It installs over 0.9.65 and keeps your airline.
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.9.67.zip**: the PC app to unzip anywhere.
- **AirlineManagerPro-0.9.67.apk**: the phone app. It installs over 0.9.65 and keeps your airline.

**The PC app brings its own browser engine**
- The app now runs in a window of its own with its own copy of Chromium, so it looks and runs the same on every PC, and an Edge update can't change it. Your airline comes along the first time it starts.
- It keeps running at full speed while Flight Simulator has the focus.
- The phone link code is now in Settings > Simulator link ("Link your phone too"). Close the window while a phone is linked and the link keeps going, with the app's icon by the clock.

**You, in your own rooms**
- In your office and in Operations you now see yourself: look down and there are your body, legs and feet; walk and your feet step and your arms swing, at your own height.
- Footsteps sound like the floor under you: the parquet and the rug in your office, the courtroom's boards, marble and carpet, the carpet in Operations, and the aisle carpet and galley floor in the cabin. They change as you step from one to the other.
- In your chair, click the family photo: you lean in and reach for it with your arm, lift it towards you in both hands, and can tilt it gently from side to side and up and down (drag, or the arrow keys on the PC). Click again, press Esc or Put it back, and your hand sets it back where it stood.

**Device voices**
- With the device's own voice (Settings > Voices), everyone now gets one of several male or female voices: the device's voices, each in a few pitches and paces. Each person keeps theirs every time you meet them, and the next one sounds different. Listen to them all in Settings.

**Your laptop, in your office**
- Click the laptop on your desk (or My laptop below): you lean in and its screen opens, as at the Operations monitor.
- The bank asks you to sign in first (Nordlys Bank), and signs you out when you close the laptop.
- Its own desktop and a bar along the bottom with the game's time and date, unread mail and today's notes.
- Mail: the court, the accident investigators' preliminary reports (can be switched off), airlines asking to lease one of your aircraft (accept and the rent comes in every month; the requests are in Fleet too), and now and then your family, with a choice of what to answer.
- Calendar: notes on the days to remember; on the day, you are reminded in the game.
- Bank: your company account, everything in and out, your loans, and accounts of your own to set money aside.
- Screenshots: take them in the 3D view with the camera button or P.
- Ranking, My career, Markets, the Aircraft market and a web browser with real web pages, YouTube included.

**Accident investigations**
- After a crash, the investigation board's preliminary report comes within minutes or after 1 to 3 days (Settings > Staff > Court). You are reminded until you read it; then sign it, or disagree and take it to court.
- The cause is traced through your airline as it is: the aircraft's state and unrepaired defects, the mechanics' numbers, training, pay, mood and quarrels, the pilot's record, and working conditions.
- Witnesses where someone could see it; the court waits for you, hears the witnesses and your evidence before the preliminary hearing, and the person at fault is heard at the trial. The worse the accident, the more and harder the questions. Your answers move your company rating.

**Your office and Operations**
- Conversations in your office: the visitor's greeting is spoken; your own questions and decisions are spoken in your own voice (20 male and 20 female voices to choose from when you create or edit your pilot). Mouths move with the voice, and start again when you press Play.
- Minimise the conversation, or bring it back from your office's name at the top left; it goes when they leave.
- When someone blames being short of people, long hours or equipment, you can agree and promise to improve their working conditions. How you handle it moves your company rating.
- Operations: walk around the room, sit in the chair again, and leave through the door to the Overview.
- Links from Needs you open where you are working (at the monitor or on the laptop).
- The 3D view opened from a screen in your offices no longer loses its graphics; you come back to the same screen.
- The live map on the Operations monitor no longer flickers.

**Also**
- Floatplanes with wheels in their floats lower them to land on a runway and keep them up for water.
- Finance > Markets: food and drink has its own tab next to jet fuel and spare parts.
- Spare parts in stock make repairs cheaper and quicker; missing parts are ordered with a rush charge and the aircraft waits for them.
- Map layer, weather and airport search windows always open inside the screen on the PC.
