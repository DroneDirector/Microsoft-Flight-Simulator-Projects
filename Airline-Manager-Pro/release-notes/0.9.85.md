**Airline Manager Pro 0.9.85: home screen widgets**

- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-Setup-0.9.85.exe**: the installer for Windows (recommended). It installs over 0.9.84 and keeps your airline.
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.9.85.zip**: the PC app to unzip anywhere.
- **AirlineManagerPro-0.9.85.apk**: the phone app. It installs over 0.9.84 and keeps your airline.

**Four widgets to choose from** (hold the home screen, Widgets, Airline Manager Pro)
- **Airline Manager Pro**: the one you have, now with far more on it. Make it bigger and it shows more: first your airline, then charts, then your tablet; wider and it adds more tiles; taller and the charts grow to fill it.
- **Small**: the balance, today's profit, the next flight and what is in the air.
- **Medium**: your airline with charts: profit, the jet fuel price and the fleet in the air.
- **Large**: everything, your airline and your tablet.

**Your airline on the widget**
- The balance and today's profit, the next flight, aircraft in the air, what needs you (conduct reports and messages), the fleet (flying, ready, in maintenance), and charts of profit, jet fuel and the fleet in the air.

**Your tablet on the widget**
- Memory: in use of the total, with a bar and a chart. Processor: its speed and the temperature, with a chart. Graphics: the graphics chip's load where the tablet lets apps read it, otherwise the game's own frame rate, with a chart. Storage: in use of the total and what is free.
- Free memory closes other apps' background work and tells you how much memory it freed (Android 13 and older; Android 14 and later don't let apps close other apps, and the widget says so).
- Clean space clears the game's own caches; then tap the note for Android's own Free up space, which clears the rest of the tablet (other apps' files are only Android's to clear).
- Refresh takes new readings at once.

**Tracking on and off**
- Every widget has a Tracking button: tap it and that widget stops taking readings; tap it again and it starts again. The game itself is never stopped.
- While tracking is on, the widget takes its readings every 15 minutes, only while the tablet is awake anyway (it never wakes it for that), so it uses next to no battery. While the game is open, it keeps the widget up to date.
