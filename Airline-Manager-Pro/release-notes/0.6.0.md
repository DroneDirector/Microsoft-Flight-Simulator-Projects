**Airline Manager Pro 0.6.0: leasing, split screen and picking up where you left off**

- **AirlineManagerPro-0.6.0.apk**, the phone app. It installs over 0.5.9 and keeps your airline.
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.6.0.zip**, the PC app with the Windows app inside. Update it too.

**Lease aircraft**
- Fleet now has "Lease aircraft" next to "Buy aircraft", and the market switches between Buy and Lease.
- Lease for 1, 3 or 6 years: a monthly rent (cheaper for longer leases) instead of the full price, with a deposit of two months' rent that comes back at the end.
- Rent is paid every 30 days, also while the app is closed, and listed in Finance under Leases.
- From the aircraft's page: extend by a year, buy it out, or return it early for three months' rent. At the end of the lease it goes back to the leasing company after it lands.

**Minimise, close and come back**
- Minimising the app no longer makes it crash or restart: everything is saved the moment it goes to the background, and if Android shuts it down to free memory it comes back instead of closing.
- When you open the app again within 12 hours it is right where you left it, without the start-up screen.

**Split screen**
- The app runs in split screen and pop-up windows on phones and tablets, with a layout for narrow windows.

**Airport view**
- The real runways show straight away, and the full layout replaces them as soon as it arrives. The download is also much quicker than before.
- When linked, the PC app answers at once if the simulator can't give a layout, so you no longer wait for it.
