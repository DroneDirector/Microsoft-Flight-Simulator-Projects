**Airline Manager Pro 0.9.52: the PC app takes over from an older copy, the family photo, the office and courtroom in My career**

- **AirlineManagerPro-0.9.52.apk**, the phone app. It installs over 0.9.51 and keeps your airline.
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.9.52.zip**, the PC app with the Windows app inside.

**PC**
- When an older copy of the PC app is still running, the new one closes it and takes over, instead of stopping with "port 47610 already in use". If the same version is already running, it simply opens its window. If another program is using the port, it says so.
- Install this version by hand once: close the PC app completely (in the Task Manager if needed), unzip 0.9.52 and start it. From then on, Settings → Updates and errors → Check for updates installs new versions directly on the PC.

**Your office**
- The family photo on your desk shows the children at their size and looking like their parents. The family photo view looks straight at it.
- The conversation sits at the side, so you see the person you're talking to. The laptop sits further away, the notepad is on the desk, and the desk lamp has a proper green glass shade.

**My career**
- My career has your office and the courtroom too, as on the Overview.
