**Airline Manager Pro 0.9.104: updates always find the newest version**

- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-Setup-0.9.104.exe**: the installer for Windows (recommended). It installs over your version and keeps your airline.
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.9.104.zip**: the PC app to unzip anywhere.
- **AirlineManagerPro-0.9.104.apk**: the phone app. It installs over your version and keeps your airline.

**Updates**
- Versions 0.9.100 and later weren't offered by the app's update check, which stopped at 0.9.99. The update check now finds the newest version every time.
- If your app shows 0.9.99 or older, install this version by hand once from the download. After that, updates arrive in the app again.

**Website**
- The download buttons and the version number on the website now point to the newest release (they were stuck on 0.9.99).
- The website's screenshots show the app again. Since the start screen arrived, every screenshot had shown the start screen instead.
- The feature list now includes everything new since the start screen: the water salute, gifts and plaques, 3D badges, runways and published procedures, flight pop-ups, autosave, Force departure and more.
