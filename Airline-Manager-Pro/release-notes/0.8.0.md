**Airline Manager Pro 0.8.0: running the business**

- **AirlineManagerPro-0.8.0.apk**, the phone app. It installs over 0.7.7 and keeps your airline.
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.8.0.zip**, the PC app with the Windows app inside. Update it too: the new flight plan and aircraft features need both.

**Aircraft: sell, buy used, cabins**
- Sell aircraft you own, to a broker at today's market value (age and condition), less a 6% fee.
- Used aircraft: a new list every Monday of second-hand aircraft, with age, hours, cycles and condition, and the ferry flight to your base.
- Cabin layouts: first, business and economy seats per aircraft, or a freighter conversion.
- Only you, the airline's owner, buy, sell and lease aircraft. Hired pilots never do, and in a co-op only the owner can.
- The market shows the aircraft actually installed in your simulator, found by the PC app.

**Routes: your fares, real demand**
- Tap a route: set your own economy, business and first class fares.
- Demand follows the airports, the distance and the season (summer, Christmas, ski airports in winter, Alaska bush in summer), your fares, your popularity and your marketing.
- Competing airlines on many routes, with their own fares; passengers choose between you.
- Fuel prices differ by airport and day; tanker fuel from cheaper airports.
- Landing, parking and gate fees by airport size, and monthly hangar rent at your bases.
- Belly cargo on every passenger flight, and full cargo loads on freighters.

**Finance**
- Loans: borrow against your fleet, pay back monthly, pay off early.
- Insurance: basic or full cover pays back accident repairs.
- Marketing campaigns for the whole airline or one route.

**Jobs**
- A job board with charters and cargo jobs. Fly them yourself or give them to a hired pilot.
- Tours: eight sets of legs to fly yourself for a reward.
- A weekly challenge, new every Monday.

**Pilots**
- Type ratings: hired pilots fly only types on their licence. Send them on a course with a check ride.
- Your first flight on a new type is your check ride.
- Landing leaderboard under Ranking: you, your hired pilots and your co-op pilots.

**Dispatch** (a new page in the side menu)
- Your next flight, with a link to plan it on SimBrief with the leg, airline, aircraft and registration filled in.
- Flight plans for the simulator (.PLN): sent to the PC and saved in Documents\Airline Manager Pro\Flight plans, or saved from the phone. Load them in the simulator's world map.
- Live weather: real METAR and TAF reports. The real wind sets the runway in use everywhere in the app (Settings → Online services).
- Discord: your flights, new and sold aircraft and finished jobs posted to your server through a webhook (Settings → Online services).
- A public airline page to save and share.

**Co-op**
- Flight reports (PIREPs): the owner accepts or rejects each co-op flight before it counts.
