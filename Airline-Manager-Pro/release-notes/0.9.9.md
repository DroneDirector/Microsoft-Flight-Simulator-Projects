**Airline Manager Pro 0.9.9: smoother and clearer**

- **AirlineManagerPro-0.9.9.apk**, the phone app. It installs over 0.9.8 and keeps your airline.
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.9.9.zip**, the PC app with the Windows app inside.

- Aircraft hold short at the holding point before the runway, off the runway, and only line up when cleared.
- 3D view: your own flight glides smoothly instead of jumping once a second. The loading screen shows for at least five seconds.
- The start-up screen shows on every start: at least five seconds, longer while the voices download, then back where you were.
- Active flights counts only aircraft truly in the air; flights on the ground show their phase (taxi out, boarding, delayed at the gate, taxi in). The map's list shows each flight's phase.
- Passenger list: tap the chips for conduct records, dogs and cats, pets left behind and celebrities to jump to them.
- Avatars no longer flicker when a card refreshes.
