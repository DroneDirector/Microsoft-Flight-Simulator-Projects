**Airline Manager Pro 0.9.125: real lounge guests, a proper Quit, and jets, turboprops and helicopters in the market**

- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-Setup-0.9.125.exe**: the installer for Windows (recommended). It installs over your version and keeps your airline.
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.9.125.zip**: the PC app to unzip anywhere.
- **AirlineManagerPro-0.9.125.apk**: the phone app. It installs over your version and keeps your airline.

**Lounges**
- The guests are your real passengers: the business and first class travellers on your next departures from that airport, over the next four hours. Each one is the same person as on the flight's passenger list: same name, same face, same clothes. When the flight leaves, they're on board.
- Look at a guest to see who they are: their name, class, destination and departure time.
- Guests come and go. They sit for a while, then get up and fetch a coffee or a tea, take a snack, look in the fridge, read the magazines, check the departures screen, or stand at the window. The Business and Flagship lounges add the bar, the fireplace and the piano. Then they sit down again wherever there's a free seat.
- When their flight is boarding, they walk out of the door.
- A seat with a guest in it shows as taken. When nobody has a lounge ticket, the lounge says so.

**Quit**
- Quit now really closes the app. Your airline keeps running on the real clock, and the notifications you've switched on still come.
- On the PC the app carries on unseen, with no window and nothing in the taskbar. Clicking a notification brings the window back.
- Opening the app again starts it afresh, from the start-up and start screens, instead of dropping you back where you were.

**Continue**
- After Continue on the start screen, a loading screen shows your airline's name for at least 5 seconds. It says what's loading: your airline, the airports, the runways, the models, the people, and what happened while you were away. If loading takes longer, the bar follows it.

**Aircraft market**
- A new menu sits next to Buy new, Used and Lease: All aircraft, Jets, Turboprops and Helicopters, each with how many there are. It works when buying new, buying used and leasing, and in the new Sell view, which lists your own aircraft and what each would fetch.
- The makers shown follow your choice, with helicopter makers in the same row. Bell helicopters no longer read "Bell Bell".

**Phone app**
- The app icon is now the same compass rose as on the PC.
