**Airline Manager Pro 0.5.3: ground services from GSX**

- **AirlineManagerPro-0.5.3.apk**, the phone app. It installs over 0.5.2 and keeps your airline.
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.5.3.zip**, the PC app with the Windows app inside. Update it too: it now reads the GSX ground services.

**GSX ground services in your finances**
- Settings → Simulator link → I use GSX Pro. When it's on and GSX is running, every ground service you actually use in GSX is charged in Finance the moment GSX finishes it: boarding, deboarding, catering, fuelling, pushback and de-icing, at real-world ground handling rates for your aircraft size.
- GSX itself doesn't put prices on its services, so the app prices what GSX did.
- Without GSX, or with the setting off, each flight gets the app's standard handling and catering charge instead, as before.
