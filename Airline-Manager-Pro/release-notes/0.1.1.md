**Airline Manager Pro 0.1.1**

- **Exit:** tap the power icon at the top right, then hold the button to close the app.
- **Full screen:** the phone's status and navigation bars stay hidden. A stray swipe at the edge only shows them briefly, and the back gesture never closes the app by accident.
- **Version** shown in the bottom-left corner.
- **Payment sound** for repairs and fines, and a **mute all sounds** switch in Settings → Sound and feel.
- **Map styles** in Settings → Map: Classic (default, works offline), street maps (dark or light) and satellite.
- **Follow a pilot:** tap a message like "on final at EKVG" and the map zooms to that aircraft and follows it.
