**Airline Manager Pro 0.9.102: gifts revealed on their own**

- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-Setup-0.9.102.exe**: the installer for Windows (recommended). It installs over 0.9.101 and keeps your airline.
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.9.102.zip**: the PC app to unzip anywhere.
- **AirlineManagerPro-0.9.102.apk**: the phone app. It installs over 0.9.101 and keeps your airline.

**Gifts and plaques**
- When you receive a gift or a commemorative plaque, it's now revealed on its own, without a badge card behind it. The beams of light, the sparks and the confetti are the same as for a badge, and the object glows in their colour.

**Turning a gift or badge round**
- The popup shows one set of controls at a time: the keyboard's, or your controller's. It switches by itself to whichever you used last. Touch the stick or a button and the controller drawing appears; press a key, click or touch the screen and the keyboard keys come back.
- The close button shows the right key too: Esc, or your controller's close button (○ on PlayStation, B on Xbox).
