**Airline Manager Pro 0.9.126: graphics settings for lounges, hangars and rooms**

- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-Setup-0.9.126.exe**: the installer for Windows (recommended). It installs over your version and keeps your airline.
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.9.126.zip**: the PC app to unzip anywhere.
- **AirlineManagerPro-0.9.126.apk**: the phone app. It installs over your version and keeps your airline.

**Settings → Graphics**
- Three new tabs next to Cabin: **Lounges**, **Hangars**, and **Offices and rooms** (your office, the operations room and the courtroom). Each one is set part by part, like the cabin:
  - **Quality:** furniture, curves, light and textures. Low, Medium, Very high or Ultra.
  - **Sharpness:** from 75% to 300% of your screen's.
  - **Shadows:** on or off.
  - **Guests (lounges) and mechanics (hangars):** switch them off for a quicker, empty room.
  - **Detail of each person:** from Light to Highest.
- Everything starts on Automatic, which follows the Rooms setting and shows what that means now. Changes apply the next time you go in.
