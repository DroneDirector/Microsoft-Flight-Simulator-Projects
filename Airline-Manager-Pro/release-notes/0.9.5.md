**Airline Manager Pro 0.9.5: your crew, your services, your start**

- **AirlineManagerPro-0.9.5.apk**, the phone app. It installs over 0.9.4 and keeps your airline.
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.9.5.zip**, the PC app with the Windows app inside.

**First start**
- After the start-up screen on a new install: explore the demo airlines, or start from scratch with your own airline name, code, callsign and home base. You can also start over later in Settings → Backup and restore (settings are kept).

**Crew**
- "Pilots" is now "Crew": pilots, cabin crew, mechanics, ground staff (drivers, baggage handlers, ramp agents and more) and hiring, with job filters.
- Everyone levels from 1 to 500, and new hires bring their career experience. A few arrive with a poor record and start below zero. Warnings cost experience.
- Staff have a mood built from pay, hours, roster, aircraft, training, conditions and recognition. Unhappy staff come to you with a request; say yes or no. Mood affects their work, and very unhappy staff leave.
- Commend good staff: a thank-you, an award, a medal, a bonus, extra days off or an all-expenses trip with their family.
- Transfer anyone to another airport, or to another of your airlines, with a reason.
- Apron crew setting: your own mechanics and ground staff at the home base only, or at every airport. Switching back to the home base asks what happens to the people elsewhere: move them home, put them on leave (60% pay) or let them go. The home base takes as many as its size and the aircraft based there call for.

**Passenger services**
- Settings → Passenger services: food, drinks, special meals, Wi-Fi, entertainment, blankets and kits, duty-free, bags, seat selection, priority boarding, a lounge and pets. Each one changes what a flight earns and costs, how many passengers choose you, and how they see you.

**Passengers**
- Passenger list on every flight card: names, nationality, faces, seats, and anyone with a record. Ban people from your airline.
- Passport and ticket scans at boarding flag banned passengers; deny or let them board.
- Loadsheet for every flight. Loaders take cargo off first, then passengers, when a runway limits the weight.

**Ranking**
- Your own ranking and the landing leaderboard stay where they were. New sections rank your pilots, cabin crew, mechanics and ground crew by level, per airline or all together.

**Other**
- People you turn in 3D stay turned; double-tap to face them forward again.
