**Airline Manager Pro 0.9.122: helicopters, the neo and MAX families, and lounges you can visit**

- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-Setup-0.9.122.exe**: the installer for Windows (recommended). It installs over your version and keeps your airline.
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.9.122.zip**: the PC app to unzip anywhere.
- **AirlineManagerPro-0.9.122.apk**: the phone app. It installs over your version and keeps your airline.

**Helicopters**
- 16 civil helicopters to buy, lease or buy used: Airbus H125, H130, H135, H145, H160 and H175; Bell 407, 429, 505 and 525; Leonardo AW109, AW139, AW169 and AW189; Sikorsky S-76D and S-92. Each has its real seats, weights, speeds, range, engines and weather limits, and its own 3D model.
- **Helipads worldwide:** more than 23,000 heliports, hospital pads and offshore helidecks. Helicopters land on them and at any airport; aeroplanes can't use them.
- **Missions:** air-ambulance transfers to hospitals, search and rescue, offshore crew changes, VIP flights, sightseeing tours and sling loads, from wherever your helicopter is based.
- **Crew:** helicopters fly with two pilots. Rescue and air-ambulance helicopters with a hoist also need a **winch operator**. Hire one, or send a flight attendant on the 12-day winch course.
- **Licences:** helicopter and aeroplane licences are separate. A pilot moving between them takes a conversion course, which costs more and takes longer than a type rating.
- **In 3D:** a cabin built to each type's size, with an instrument panel and seat rows (a stretcher in air ambulances), a turning rotor, and lights on the cabin sides.
- **Other operators:** an air ambulance, an offshore operator and a charter company fly their helicopters near your base. You see them on the map and in 3D, and you can follow them.
- **Newspaper:** your first helicopter, and your first from each maker, make the news. A helicopter crash has its own picture and its own wreckage at the scene.
- Every helicopter has a replaceable model file, for example `helicopters/helicopter-airbus-h145.glb` (the full list is in `models/README.md`).

**New airliners**
- A319neo, A321ceo, A321neo, A321LR, A321XLR, A330-800neo and A330-900neo; 737 MAX 7, 8, 9 and 10; 787-8, 787-9 and 777-9. All have their full data and 3D shape.
- **Engine choice:** CFM or IAE on the A321ceo, and LEAP or GTF on the neos. Each engine changes fuel burn, maintenance and the runway needed for take-off. The fleet card shows which engine each aircraft has.
- Every type can be bought. A type that isn't in your simulator says "Not in your sim: your pilots fly it".

**Lounges**
- Visit your lounges in 3D: Lounge, Business and Flagship. The carpet, furniture and lighting get better with each level, and there's a coffee and tea bar with an urn, cups and napkins, a glass-door fridge, and guests.
- Every lounge prop can be replaced with its own model file (`lounge-props/`).

**Fleet and 3D**
- The "crew needed" text on a fleet card takes you to the hiring list for that kind of crew.
- An aircraft in the air has "Show on the map" and "3D view" buttons.
- Following another airline's aircraft in 3D works again, and its details show in the 3D view.
