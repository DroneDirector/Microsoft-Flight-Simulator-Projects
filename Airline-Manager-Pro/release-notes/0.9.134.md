**Airline Manager Pro 0.9.134: walk between your rooms**

- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-Setup-0.9.134.exe**: the installer for Windows (recommended). It installs over your version and keeps your airline.
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.9.134.zip**: the PC app to unzip anywhere.
- **AirlineManagerPro-0.9.134.apk**: the phone app. It installs over your version and keeps your airline.

**One building for your rooms**
- Your office and the Operations office now open off a staff corridor at your hub, and a door at its end leads onto the concourse. If you have a lounge at your hub, its sliding doors are at the far end.
- Get up from your chair, open the door and walk out: along the corridor, into the Operations office, down the concourse and into your lounge, and back. No loading screens between them.
- Everything in each room works as before: your chair, the laptop, the phone, the family photo, your pet, visitors and interviews, the monitor and its screens, the lounge's guests and coffee.
- The Office, Operations and Lounge buttons still take you straight to your chair (or into your lounge).
- Doors open and shut with a click, from either side. Airport-style signs beside them say which room it is.
- Lounges at other airports are still their own rooms.
- Settings → Graphics → "Walk between your rooms" turns it off, and then each room opens on its own as before.
