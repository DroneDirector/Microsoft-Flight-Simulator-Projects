**Airline Manager Pro 0.9.35: clouds, passenger mood and twelve new features for single player and co-op**

- **AirlineManagerPro-0.9.35.apk**, the phone app. It installs over 0.9.34 and keeps your airline.
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.9.35.zip**, the PC app with the Windows app inside.

**Clouds from real-world weather**
- The 3D view shows soft volumetric clouds placed from the airport weather reports around you: few, scattered, broken and overcast layers at their reported heights, towering cumulus and thunderstorm clouds, thin high cloud.
- On the map, the weather layer shows the same clouds as see-through layers (Weather → Clouds).
- Settings → 3D view: a slider for how far you see clouds (or off) and one for how detailed they are.

**Passengers' mood changes during the flight**
- Delays, turbulence, the meal service, pets on board and the landing all move it. Very rarely a crew member is rude to a passenger for no reason, and the people sitting around react too.
- Each passenger shows their mood in the list; tap one to see what happened to them on this flight.

**Both modes**
- Connecting passengers at your bases: arrivals that land 40 to 150 minutes before a departure feed it; late arrivals miss connections.
- ETOPS: twin-engine routes far from land need an approval for the aircraft type.
- Night curfews and slots at busy airports.
- Sustainable fuel and emission charges (in the EU, the UK and Switzerland).

**Single player only**
- Take over a rival airline: its aircraft join your fleet as a new division.
- Shares and investors: go public, pay dividends, buy shares back; investors may offer money when you're in the red.
- Rivals react to your fares with fare wars, more flights or by leaving a route.
- Scenarios with medals: Island hopper, Rescue a failing airline, Atlantic bridge and Bush flying.

**Co-op only**
- Profit sharing between members, set by the owner.
- Company radio with position reports and short messages (can be read aloud).
- League against other co-op airlines.
- Live positions of the real members in the 3D view (when the server provides them).
