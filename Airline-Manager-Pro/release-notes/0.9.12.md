**Airline Manager Pro 0.9.12: lease out aircraft and close routes**

- **AirlineManagerPro-0.9.12.apk**, the phone app. It installs over 0.9.11 and keeps your airline.
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.9.12.zip**, the PC app with the Windows app inside.

- Lease your own aircraft out to other airlines: Fleet → the aircraft → Lease out. Pick one of three offers and one to five years, then confirm. Rent comes in every month; the aircraft comes back with more hours and some wear. Recall it early for three months' rent.
- Close a route: Routes → the route → Close route (asks first). Legs not yet flown are cancelled, a flight in the air lands as planned, and the pilot waits for a new assignment. The local paper reports it.
