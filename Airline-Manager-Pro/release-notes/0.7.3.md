**Airline Manager Pro 0.7.3: real take-off, landing and taxi speeds**

- **AirlineManagerPro-0.7.3.apk**, the phone app. It installs over 0.7.2 and keeps your airline.
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.7.3.zip**, the PC app with the Windows app inside.

**Take-off and landing speeds per aircraft**
- Every aircraft type now takes off and lands at its own real-world speeds: rotation and climb-out speed, approach speed, threshold and touchdown speed.
- Within each type's range the speed follows the flight's weight (empty aircraft, crew, passengers with their bags, cargo and fuel, up to the type's maximum take-off and landing weights), and the wind along the runway changes the speed over the ground.
- Take-off rolls accelerate to rotation speed and lift off; landings touch down, brake to taxi speed and turn off, braking harder on short runways.

**Taxi speeds**
- 5-10 kt in tight turns and the last 100 m before the runway or the stand, 15-25 kt on straight taxiways, up to about 30 kt on long straight ones. Pushback at about 4 kt.
- Departing aircraft wait at the gate and push back in time for the runway, instead of waiting at the holding point.
- The speed is shown on each aircraft's label and in the airport list.

**Map**
- Panels and buttons on the map always stay in front of runways, taxiways and aircraft.
