**Airline Manager Pro 0.9.123: a crash scene you can replace**

- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-Setup-0.9.123.exe**: the installer for Windows (recommended). It installs over your version and keeps your airline.
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.9.123.zip**: the PC app to unzip anywhere.
- **AirlineManagerPro-0.9.123.apk**: the phone app. It installs over your version and keeps your airline.

**Crash scene**
- Every part of a crash site can be replaced with your own file in `models/crash/`. Each file has a clear name, and the full list is in `models/README.md`.
  - Vehicles: `crash-fire-engine.glb`, `crash-command-vehicle.glb`, `crash-ambulance.glb`, `crash-rescue-boat.glb`. The app keeps their flashing lights. If there's no `crash-fire-engine.glb`, the airport fire truck (`vehicles/fire-truck.glb`) is used.
  - Wreckage: `crash-debris.glb` (one piece, copied for all the debris), `crash-engine.glb`, `crash-wing-piece.glb`, `crash-wheel.glb` and `crash-rotor-blade.glb`.
  - Effects (PNG pictures): `crash-fire.png`, `crash-smoke.png`, `crash-soot.png`, `crash-oil.png` and `crash-glow.png`. The app still moves, fades and tints them.
- Without a file, the app uses its own model or effect.
- Helicopter crash sites now show fire and smoke properly.
