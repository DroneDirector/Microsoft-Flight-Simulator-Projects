**Airline Manager Pro 0.8.2: voices with feeling**

- **AirlineManagerPro-0.8.2.apk**, the phone app. It installs over 0.8.1 and keeps your airline.
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.8.2.zip**, the PC app with the Windows app inside.

**Voices**
- The voices download on the start-up screen, the first time: the same bar and aircraft show the download, and the app opens when they are all in.
- Women speak with women's voices, men with men's.
- Moods: angry people sound angry, apologies sound sorry, and a drunk passenger sounds drunk (slow, slurred, wavering).
- The drunk passenger at the gate now speaks for themselves, right after the gate agent.
- Hear every voice and mood in Settings → Voices.

**Sound settings**
- Settings → Sound and feel → Only the voices: every other sound off, the people in the dialogues still speak.
- Settings → Sound and feel → Voices in dialogues: switch the voices off (and their play buttons) or on again.
