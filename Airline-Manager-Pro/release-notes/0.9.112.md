**Airline Manager Pro 0.9.112: extra quality on the PC**

- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-Setup-0.9.112.exe**: the installer for Windows (recommended). It installs over your version and keeps your airline.
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.9.112.zip**: the PC app to unzip anywhere.

**On the PC: the full quality**
- Some of the new parts are drawn lighter so phones and tablets run them smoothly. On the PC they now run at full quality:
  - **Museum:** every piece has a real spotlight, and eight of them cast their own soft shadows. The hall gets four times finer shadows from the ceiling lamps, a sharper picture on high-resolution screens, and smoother light beams.
  - **Photo mode:** pictures up to 3840 pixels wide, saved at a higher quality; the photo of the day is sharper too.
  - **Awards:** the type-rating certificates, plaques and medals, and their reveal, are rendered in more detail.
- Phones and tablets keep the lighter versions. From now on, everything new gets the extra quality on the PC as well.
