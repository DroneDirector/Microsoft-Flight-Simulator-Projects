**Airline Manager Pro 0.9.113: graphics settings, finer mountains, frequent flyers and alliances**

- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-Setup-0.9.113.exe**: the installer for Windows (recommended). It installs over your version and keeps your airline.
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.9.113.zip**: the PC app to unzip anywhere.
- **AirlineManagerPro-0.9.113.apk**: the phone app. It installs over your version and keeps your airline.

**On the PC: every graphics category**
- **Settings → Graphics → PC graphics:**
  - **A preset:** Low, Medium, High or Ultra sets everything at once, including view distance, terrain, clouds, cabin, water, satellite and anti-aliasing.
  - **Picture:** render scale (a slider from 50% to 200%), anti-aliasing, texture filtering and a frame rate limit.
  - **Light and shadow:** shadows from Off to Ultra (up to 8192 pixels), lighting (drawn, real lights, or real lights with shadows) and metal shine.
  - **Detail:** rooms (offices, operations room, courtroom) and pictures (awards, thumbnails and photos up to 7680 pixels wide).
- Each category starts on Automatic, which is what the PC drew before.

**On phones and tablets: light and shadow**
- **Settings → Graphics → Light and shadow:**
  - **Shadows:** Off, Low, Medium or High.
  - **Lighting:** Drawn, or real lights for the eight nearest pieces in the museum.
  - **Rooms:** their quality, on its own.
- Each one starts where the phone was before, and the heavier choices show a warning.

**Mountains and coastlines**
- **Terrain detail** (Settings → Graphics → Distance and clouds): Low, Medium, High and Very high, plus Ultra on the PC, which also adds heights about 19 m apart nearest you.
- **Smooth mountain ridges:** on by default. Ridges, peaks and coastlines no longer break into spikes and saw teeth. The sea stays perfectly flat.

**Smoother on Android**
- The app asks for the screen's fastest refresh rate (90 or 120 Hz where the phone has it).
- It gets more memory for big airports, the cabin and satellite imagery.
- Its 3D drawing keeps a high priority while it is on screen.

**Passengers who come back**
- **Frequent-flyer programme** (Finance):
  - Launch it with your own name.
  - Members join from the passengers you fly and reach Silver, Gold and Platinum. They choose you over your rivals more often.
  - Perks come in three levels: Basic, Plus or Premium.
  - A co-branded card pays you every month; reward seats and perks cost you every month.
  - Members show their tier on the passenger list.
- **Lounges** at your hubs and bases (Routes): a lounge, a business lounge or a flagship lounge. Premium travellers choose you more often on flights from and to them.
- **Alliances** (Routes): Aurora Alliance, Meridian Group and Polaris, all made up.
  - You can join one once your airline is liked enough and big enough.
  - Members connect their passengers onto your flights, their lounges count for your travellers, and your frequent flyers earn with all of them.
  - If you fall below the alliance's standard you get a warning, then you are asked to leave.

**Money**
- **Fuel hedging** (Finance): buy 25%, 50% or 75% of your fuel ahead, for 1, 3 or 6 months, at today's fuel index. The card shows what it saves or costs you.
- **Flexible fares** (open a route): fares rise as flights fill and drop when seats stay empty. They follow the seasons and special days, up to 30% dearer and down to 25% cheaper.
- **Spare slot pairs:** closing a route keeps its slots for two months. A new route there uses them, or you can sell them to another airline. Unused slots go back to the airport (use it or lose it).

**Goals**
- **Achievements** (Ranking → Achievements): 21 of them, with progress bars, covering flying, the world, the airline, passengers and jobs.
- **Your first week:** a checklist in the Overview for a new airline that ticks itself as you go. It can be hidden, and Settings → General brings it back.
- **Special cargo jobs:**
  - Live animals: needs a jet with a heated hold.
  - Vaccines kept cold: needs a flight under 7 hours.
  - Oversized freight: needs a freighter or a widebody.
  - Urgent engine parts for a grounded airliner (AOG): due within hours.
  - Each one pays two to three times as much.
