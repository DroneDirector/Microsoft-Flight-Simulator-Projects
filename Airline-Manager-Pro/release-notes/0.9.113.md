**Airline Manager Pro 0.9.113: graphics settings and finer mountains**

- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-Setup-0.9.113.exe**: the installer for Windows (recommended). It installs over your version and keeps your airline.
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.9.113.zip**: the PC app to unzip anywhere.
- **AirlineManagerPro-0.9.113.apk**: the phone app. It installs over your version and keeps your airline.

**On the PC: every graphics category**
- **Settings → Graphics → PC graphics:**
  - **A preset:** Low, Medium, High or Ultra sets everything at once, including view distance, terrain, clouds, cabin, water, satellite and anti-aliasing.
  - **Picture:** render scale (a slider from 50% to 200%), anti-aliasing, texture filtering and a frame rate limit.
  - **Light and shadow:** shadows from Off to Ultra (up to 8192 pixels), lighting (drawn, real lights, or real lights with shadows) and metal shine.
  - **Detail:** rooms (offices, operations room, courtroom) and pictures (awards, thumbnails and photos up to 7680 pixels wide).
- Each category starts on Automatic, which is what the PC drew before.

**On phones and tablets: light and shadow**
- **Settings → Graphics → Light and shadow:**
  - **Shadows:** Off, Low, Medium or High.
  - **Lighting:** Drawn, or real lights for the eight nearest pieces in the museum.
  - **Rooms:** their quality, on its own.
- Each one starts where the phone was before, and the heavier choices show a warning.

**Mountains and coastlines**
- **Terrain detail** (Settings → Graphics → Distance and clouds): Low, Medium, High and Very high, plus Ultra on the PC, which also adds heights about 19 m apart nearest you.
- **Smooth mountain ridges:** on by default. Ridges, peaks and coastlines no longer break into spikes and saw teeth. The sea stays perfectly flat.

**Smoother on Android**
- The app asks for the screen's fastest refresh rate (90 or 120 Hz where the phone has it).
- It gets more memory for big airports, the cabin and satellite imagery.
- Its 3D drawing keeps a high priority while it is on screen.
