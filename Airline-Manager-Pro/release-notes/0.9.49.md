**Airline Manager Pro 0.9.49: a clearer restore, voices on the PC, a birthday bow**

- **AirlineManagerPro-0.9.49.apk**, the phone app. It installs over 0.9.48 and keeps your airline.
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.9.49.zip**, the PC app with the Windows app inside.

**Restoring a backup**
- "Restore this backup?" now shows the backup and what is on this device now side by side: when each was saved, by which version, the airlines, balance, aircraft and flights, marked "Will be restored" and "Will be replaced".
- If the backup is older than what it would replace, a clear warning says so and the button reads "Restore the older backup". With nothing saved here yet, it says so: nothing will be lost.

**Voices on the PC**
- The natural voices download on the PC again. The PC app now fetches the voice pack's file list itself (the app window couldn't reach it), and the app knows the list in case the server can't be reached.

**PC updates**
- Before an update the app window is closed the normal way first, so it saves everything, and only then made to close.

**Birthday**
- On your birthday your captain stands at ease, then gives a slow thank-you bow, as at the end of a show, and goes back to standing at ease. Once for each birthday.
