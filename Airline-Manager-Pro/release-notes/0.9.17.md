**Airline Manager Pro 0.9.17: free camera on the PC**

- **AirlineManagerPro-0.9.17.apk**, the phone app. It installs over 0.9.16 and keeps your airline.
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.9.17.zip**, the PC app with the Windows app inside.

- On the PC the free camera works like a shooter game: click to capture the mouse and look around, W A S D to move, Space and C (or E and Q) to climb and sink, Shift for faster, the mouse wheel sets the speed. No touch controls on the PC.
- Esc leaves the free camera and the 3D view, back to the 2D map; an Esc key badge in the top-left corner shows it.
