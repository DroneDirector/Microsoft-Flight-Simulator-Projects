**Airline Manager Pro 0.3.0: works with Microsoft Flight Simulator**

Two downloads:
- **AirlineManagerPro-0.3.0.apk**, the phone app. It installs over 0.2.0 and keeps your airline.
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.3.0.zip**, the PC app for the Windows PC with Flight Simulator.

**Getting started**
1. Unzip the PC app on your sim PC and run `AirlineManagerPro-PC.exe`. Windows may warn because test builds aren't signed yet: choose *More info → Run anyway*, and allow it on private networks.
2. Start Microsoft Flight Simulator 2024 (2020 works too).
3. On the phone, tap **Demo, link your PC** at the top, tap your PC and type the 6-digit code from the PC window. Phone and PC must be on the same Wi-Fi.
4. Fly. Your flight shows live on the map and in the flight card. When you park and set the parking brake, the flight is logged with the landing rate, hours, revenue and fuel. Any damage goes to the mechanic.

To try the link without the sim, run `AirlineManagerPro-PC.exe --demo 30` for a simulated EKVG–EKCH flight.

Hired pilots' flights are still simulated. ATC clearance tracking and installed-aircraft detection come later.
