**Airline Manager Pro 0.9.53: inviting crew to your office works every time**

- **AirlineManagerPro-0.9.53.apk**, the phone app. It installs over 0.9.52 and keeps your airline.
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.9.53.zip**, the PC app with the Windows app inside.

**Your office**
- Inviting someone to your office over a conduct report works every time, also after the first one. Before, a second invite never got to the questions.
- They knock, come in and sit down facing you. If anything holds them up, they are in the chair within a few seconds anyway and the conversation starts.
- If their figure can't be shown, you can still question them and decide. Any problem is noted in Settings → Updates and errors.
