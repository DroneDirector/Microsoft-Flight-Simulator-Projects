**Airline Manager Pro 0.9.6: real weather, strikes, sick days and happy passengers**

- **AirlineManagerPro-0.9.6.apk**, the phone app. It installs over 0.9.5 and keeps your airline.
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.9.6.zip**, the PC app with the Windows app inside.

**Real-world weather, always**
- Your hired pilots fly in the real weather: airport reports and forecasts, and warnings for thunderstorms, severe turbulence and icing, volcanic ash and tropical cyclones, from the US Aviation Weather Center.
- Each aircraft type has its real limits: crosswind, tailwind, the lowest visibility and cloud base it may land in (CAT III, CAT II, CAT I or VFR), and whether it may fly in icing. Airports add their own limits by size.
- Beyond the limits, a flight waits at the gate, holds before landing, flies around a storm area, or diverts to an alternate with good weather and carries on afterwards. A wait of more than five hours becomes a cancellation, flown the next day. Hurricanes cancel flights.
- The weather button on the map shows or hides it: airport weather by flight category with thunderstorms, rain, snow, fog and strong wind, and the hazard areas. With Flight Simulator connected, a panel shows the sim's own weather at your aircraft.

**Schedule**
- Delayed legs show how late they are and why, with the planned time outlined; the legs after them move too. Cancelled legs are marked and come back the next day. New Tomorrow view.

**Staff**
- Pay follows real salaries by job, experience, region and kind of airline. Set a pay scale for each group in Settings → Staff. Everyone already employed has been put on a fair salary.
- Badly underpaid or deeply unhappy groups warn you twice through their union before they strike. On strike: meet their demands, offer half way, wait, or dismiss them and hire cheaper, weaker replacements.
- Staff call in sick now and then. Find a stand-in or a contract pilot, or agency cover for cabin crew and mechanics, or the flights wait.

**Passengers**
- Pets: dogs and cats fly with their owners when your policy allows it, in the cabin or the heated hold. The captain leaves a pet behind when the destination is too hot or too cold for the hold. Pet tickets add to income.
- Every passenger rates each flight 1 to 5 stars: the landing, delays, service and crew, turbulence (some hate it, some love it), their pet, celebrities, diversions. See it in Ranking → Passengers.
- Now and then a celebrity is on board.
- Rarely a passenger falls ill: the flight diverts, the passenger goes to hospital, and the flight carries on.
