**Airline Manager Pro 0.9.110: the turnaround live, and weather decisions**

- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-Setup-0.9.110.exe**: the installer for Windows (recommended). It installs over your version and keeps your airline.
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.9.110.zip**: the PC app to unzip anywhere.
- **AirlineManagerPro-0.9.110.apk**: the phone app. It installs over your version and keeps your airline.

**Turnaround (Schedule)**
- An aircraft on the ground between two legs shows its turnaround as a live timeline:
  - passengers off, bags and cargo out, cleaning, catering and fuel
  - bags and cargo in, boarding, doors closed and push-back
  - de-icing, when it's cold and wet.
- A line shows where it stands now, with the gate time, the doors-closed time and the off-blocks time.
- When the next leg is late, you can buy time back: an extra cleaning team, a second fuel truck, priority catering or more gate agents. Each saves a few minutes, once, but never makes the flight leave before its timetable.

**Weather decisions**
- About an hour before your airline's legs leave, the weather can ask for a decision:
  - **Cold and wet at the airport:** book priority de-icing, or wait about 35 minutes in the queue.
  - **Thunderstorms at the destination:** wait 45 minutes for them to pass, carry 30 minutes of extra fuel and go, or go as planned and accept the chance of a diversion and its costs.
