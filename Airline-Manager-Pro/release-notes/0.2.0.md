**Airline Manager Pro 0.2.0, crew update**

Installs over 0.1.5 and keeps your saved airline.

- **Animated avatars** for every pilot, co-pilot, flight attendant and mechanic, gently swaying.
- **Create your pilot's look:** Pilots → tap your pilot. Name, date of birth, nationality, skin tone, face, hair, glasses and uniform. Always fully dressed.
- **Cabin crew and mechanics** to hire under Pilots, each with a weekly salary.
- **Co-pilot** on jets and other two-pilot aircraft: choose male or female.
- **Conduct reports:** discipline or dismiss. Set how often it happens in Settings → Staff.
- **Real-world crew rules** decide which aircraft can fly.
- **Route builder** blocks legs that are too far for the aircraft.
- **Fuel and weights** of your flight: tap "Fuel on board" in your flight card.
- **Finance:** tap any transaction for details, and see every salary.
- **Birthday greeting** on your pilot's birthday (the demo pilot's birthday is today).
- **Connection panel:** tap "Linked over Wi-Fi" at the top.

Everything still runs on demo data until the PC app links to Flight Simulator.
