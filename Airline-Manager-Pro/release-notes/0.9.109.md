**Check rides for you (Crew → your licence)**
- Book a check ride for a type you aren't rated on (examiner's fee $3,500). Your next flight in that type in Flight Simulator is the test, graded live from the simulator's data:
  - **Stable approach from 1,000 ft:** descending no faster than 1,000 ft a minute, gear down, flaps out, and speed within 15 kt of the type's approach speed.
  - **Speed:** no faster than 250 kt below 10,000 ft.
  - **Landing:** under 400 ft a minute and 1.6 g, with no damage.
  - **Engine failure:** if you fail an engine in the simulator, keep it flying, without getting too slow or too steep.
- After landing you see each task, passed or not, with the numbers. Pass, and the type goes on your licence with a framed certificate.

**Career milestones (Ranking → Gifts → Career Milestones)**
- These are celebrated with the reveal animation, the papers and an award you can turn round in 3D:
  - 100, 500, 1,000, 2,500, 5,000 and 10,000 hours (a plaque)
  - 500, 1,000 and 2,500 landings (a medal)
  - each promotion and your first widebody flight (a certificate)
  - each type rating (a certificate).

**Airline events**
- About every two or three days, something happens that needs your decision. Each choice has a cost and a consequence you'll see:
  - **An aircraft stuck at an outstation, waiting for a part.** Fly the part in on a charter (expensive, flying again in 6 hours), send it by courier (cheap, 30 hours), or take the part from another aircraft at your hub (free, but that aircraft is grounded for a day).
  - **A VIP charter at short notice** for a team, an orchestra or a delegation. Good money, and the press is watching.
  - **A mistake fare gone viral.** Honour the tickets, cancel them, or cancel with a voucher. Each choice affects your money and your reputation.
  - **A fuel supplier's fixed price** at your hub for 30 days. You save if prices rise and pay more if they fall.
- Each event arrives as a notification you can open later. When you're on a calm screen, the card opens by itself; it never interrupts the Schedule, Dispatch, the Flight planner, Crew, Finance, Routes or Fleet.

**Rivals**
- Open a new route on a city pair another airline flies, and they answer for six weeks: more flights, or a 20% fare cut. The papers write about it.
- Rivals also try to hire your best people. Match the offer (12% more pay) or let them go.

**Partners and codeshares (Routes)**
- Each month, three airlines from your routes offer a codeshare. Each partner has its own hub, a monthly fee and a share of connecting passengers.
- Their passengers connect onto your flights to and from their hub, and you're paid for each one every day.
- A partner in trouble can make the news. Then you choose whether to stand by them or end the codeshare.
