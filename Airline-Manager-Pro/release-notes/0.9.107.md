**Airline Manager Pro 0.9.107: morning briefing, and special days**

- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-Setup-0.9.107.exe**: the installer for Windows (recommended). It installs over your version and keeps your airline.
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.9.107.zip**: the PC app to unzip anywhere.
- **AirlineManagerPro-0.9.107.apk**: the phone app. It installs over your version and keeps your airline.

**Morning briefing**
- Once a day, a few seconds after you reach the Overview (after any badges or gifts), one page shows:
  - **Yesterday**: the result in money and the flights flown.
  - **Today**: the flights still to fly, bad weather at your airports, crew who are off sick or on strike, grounded aircraft and aircraft in poor condition, and messages that need you.
  - **Coming up**: the special days on your routes in the next three weeks.
- Open it again any time with the **Morning briefing** button in the Overview. Switch it off in Settings → General.

**Special days**
- Holidays, festivals and events now fill aircraft to and from the places they happen, on top of the seasons:
  - Christmas and New Year, Easter, Thanksgiving, the 4th of July, Lunar New Year and Eid al-Fitr
  - Ólavsøka in the Faroe Islands (about 60% busier), Norway's 17 May, Iceland's 17 June and Midsummer in the Nordic countries
  - Oktoberfest in Munich, Carnival in Brazil, and the winter ski week at the mountain airports
  - the Danish summer holidays, the UK's late summer bank holiday, and a big football final in a different European city each year.
- Each one has its real dates, worked out every year, plus the days people travel before and after. They're known ahead, so you can raise fares or add flights in time.
