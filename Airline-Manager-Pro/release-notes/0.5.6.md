**Airline Manager Pro 0.5.6: registration, every airport, co-op and levels**

- **AirlineManagerPro-0.5.6.apk**, the phone app. It installs over 0.5.5 and keeps your airline.
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.5.6.zip**, the PC app with the Windows app inside. Update it too: it now gives the Windows app its registration and the simulator's full airport list.

**Registration**
- New in Settings: Registration. Enter the registration key you received with your purchase.
- A key belongs to the first phone or PC it is registered on. That device can register it again as often as needed, also after reinstalling the app.
- On a new device, or after the phone or Windows has been set up from scratch, the key is moved with a reactivation code. Each code works once.

**Every airport**
- About 49,000 airports from around the world, and when the app is linked to your simulator, every airport the simulator has, add-on airports included.
- The maps only show the airports on your routes and where flights are going. Find any other airport by its name, city, ICAO or IATA code with the search button on the maps.
- Tap an airport on the map to see its name, runways and which of your routes use it.

**Flights on the map**
- Tap a flight to open a small menu above it: Follow, Favourite and Details. Favourite pilots are listed first.

**Co-op (first part)**
- A Co-op screen to start an airline with other pilots, with an invite code, shared income and rules for who may buy aircraft. Online play with other people comes in a later update; until then you can try it with sample pilots.

**Pilots, crew and badges**
- Hired pilots and crew now gain levels from their flights and work, with a higher rating at each level. Badges are only for you.
- Ranking shows your own record and the badges you have earned, newest first. Badges appear once you have earned them.
- Hired pilots only fly the routes you give them, and never take off and land at the same airport unless you make it a test flight. Only you create and schedule routes and test flights.
