**Airline Manager Pro 0.9.36: aircraft no longer race to the airport at the end of a flight**

- **AirlineManagerPro-0.9.36.apk**, the phone app. It installs over 0.9.35 and keeps your airline.
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.9.36.zip**, the PC app with the Windows app inside.

- Fixed: a hired pilot's flight could cross the map at four to five times its real speed (ATN318 on its way to Skavsta). When the app started, the flight's timing could be worked out before the world airport list had loaded, with a guessed distance of 200 nm, and that guess was kept for the whole flight. Now it is worked out again once the airports are known, and from the runways in the meantime.
- If the simulator's published procedures make the route much longer than the trip the times are planned for, the app's own departure and arrival are used instead.
