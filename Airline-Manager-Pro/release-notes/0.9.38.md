**Airline Manager Pro 0.9.38: day and night, the sun, moon and stars, living water and night lights in the 3D view**

- **AirlineManagerPro-0.9.38.apk**, the phone app. It installs over 0.9.37 and keeps your airline.
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.9.38.zip**, the PC app with the Windows app inside.

**Sky, day and night**
- The sun and the moon are where they really are for the place you look at and the time, at their true size. The moon shows its real phase.
- Sunrise, sunset, twilight and night as they are there: fly east or west and you reach day or night sooner, just as in the real world.
- The real stars at night (from the Hipparcos catalogue), turning with the sky.
- When linked to your simulator, the sky follows the simulator's time, so it matches your flight. Otherwise it's the real time.
- Overcast weather greys the sky and hides the sun, moon and stars.

**Water**
- The sea and lakes move with the wind from the weather reports, reflect the sky, and sparkle in the sun (a gold or red streak at sunset) and the moon. Surf where they meet the land.
- Settings → 3D view → Water: Simple, Living, or Mirror reflections (also reflects the land, aircraft and clouds; it draws the view twice, so it's for fast PCs).

**Lights at night**
- Airports: runway edge and centre line lights, green thresholds, red ends, approach lights, green taxiway centre lines, blue taxiway edges and floodlit aprons.
- Aircraft: red and green navigation lights, white strobes, red beacons, and landing lights when taking off and landing.
- Towns and roads glow at night (NASA's Black Marble night pictures of the Earth).

**Also**
- The 2D map shades the night side of the Earth.
- Every option has its own switch in Settings → 3D view.
- Airports whose aprons are mapped in OpenStreetMap but not their stands now get stands laid out on the aprons.
- The passenger list can be sorted by seat, name, nationality, age, gender, mood, conduct and pets, and searched by name or nationality.
