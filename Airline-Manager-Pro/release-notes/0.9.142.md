**Airline Manager Pro 0.9.142: hair for every device, calmer text**

- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-Setup-0.9.142.exe**: the installer for Windows (recommended). It installs over your version and keeps your airline.
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.9.142.zip**: the PC app to unzip anywhere.
- **AirlineManagerPro-0.9.142.apk**: the phone app. It installs over your version and keeps your airline.

**Hair (Settings → Graphics)**
- Strands: every hair drawn one by one, the finest, for strong PCs.
- Hair cards: fewer, wider locks of hair over the painted hair, nearly as good and far lighter. The new standard on phones and tablets.
- Flat: painted on the head only, the lightest.
- Automatic: strands on the PC, hair cards on phones and tablets. It shows on the people you see next.

**Text that changes**
- The yellow flip of changing letters and numbers is now only on the opened flight card, Overview, Ranking, and in the 3D and 2D views. Everywhere else text simply changes.
