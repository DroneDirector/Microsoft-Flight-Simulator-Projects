**Airline Manager Pro 0.9.115: walk into your hangar**

- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-Setup-0.9.115.exe**: the installer for Windows (recommended). It installs over your version and keeps your airline.
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.9.115.zip**: the PC app to unzip anywhere.
- **AirlineManagerPro-0.9.115.apk**: the phone app. It installs over your version and keeps your airline.

**The hangar, in 3D**
- **Walk in:** Maintenance → Walk into your hangars. Every hangar is listed by base and size, with its aircraft and what each one needs fixing. Pick an aircraft and walk in.
- **Four sizes:** small, medium, large and very large (up to the A380). The whole aircraft fits inside with the doors shut.
- **The building:** steel trusses, high-bay lamps and skylight strips across the roof, plus a band of windows high on the side walls and in the doors.
  - The windows show the sky at that base right now: day, sunrise, sunset or night.
  - By day the sun shines in through them. On the PC it is real sunlight with shadows.
- **Doors:** huge sliding doors across the whole front, each leaf on its own track.
- **Your mechanics:** the mechanics based there work round your aircraft. Talk to them: what needs fixing, when it will be ready, how it is looking, how they are doing.
- **The maintenance office:** the same monitor, keyboard and mouse as the Operations office, with the same screens behind it, plus a board listing the work on the aircraft.
- **The break room:** a vending machine with drinks and snack bars (no real brands).
  - Choose one and the coil turns, it drops into the tray, and you open the flap to take it.
  - Carry it as you walk (look down and it's in your hand), look at it, drink it (4 or 5 sips) or eat it (3 or 4 bites).
  - Put it down on any table, counter, trolley or the floor, and pick it up again.
  - Whatever you put down stays in that size of hangar for 48 hours, saved with your airline and its backups. Just for fun: it changes nothing in the game.
- **Quick walks:** buttons take you straight to the aircraft, the office or the break room. Shift (or the stick pushed all the way) runs.

**Your own models**
- **Hangar buildings:** one file per size, `hangars/hangar-small.glb` to `hangars/hangar-very-large.glb`. A `-pc` file is used first on the PC, so a heavier, higher-quality building can go to the PC only.
- **The vending machine:** `items/vending-machine.glb`, with named parts the app works with: `slot-A1`…`slot-C3`, `coil-A1`…`coil-C3`, `flap`, `tray` and `display`.
- **Drinks and bars:** each has a file of its own in `items/`.
- `www/models/README.md` lists every name.

**Fixed**
- **Updates:** the update check always asks GitHub for a fresh list, so a new version is found as soon as it is published. If a version is out but its download isn't up yet, it says so.
