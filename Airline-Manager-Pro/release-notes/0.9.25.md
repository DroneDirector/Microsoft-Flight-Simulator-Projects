**Airline Manager Pro 0.9.25: virtual airline features, the airline league and awards**

- **AirlineManagerPro-0.9.25.apk**, the phone app. It installs over 0.9.24 and keeps your airline.
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.9.25.zip**, the PC app with the Windows app inside.

**Your flights**
- Flight replay: open a flight in the log and play it back on a map, with a chart of altitude and speed and take-off, top of climb, top of descent and touchdown marked.
- Flight score out of 100: points come off for more than 250 kt below 10,000 ft, taxiing faster than 30 kt, flaps or gear above their limit speeds, an unstable approach, a hard or off-centre landing, touching down past the touchdown zone and landing below the fuel reserve. Light and stall checks follow once the PC app reads them from the simulator.
- Hired pilots fly by procedure: their score depends on their skill, and you hear about it when one misses an important step.
- Fuel plan at dispatch and a fuel check after each flight.
- Screenshots: add them to a flight; they show on the flight and on your profile.
- Recency: 3 landings on a type in 90 days. Otherwise your next leg on it is a recency flight without passengers, or book a simulator session. Hired pilots go to the simulator automatically.
- NOTAMs in the briefings: closed runways, taxiway works, ILS out of service, cranes and birds. Closed runways and ILS aren't used.

**Your airline**
- Airline league (Ranking → Airline league): your airline against the other airlines, on time, flight scores, landings, popularity and amount of flying.
- Airline of the Month and Airline of the Year, with the reasons, badges and a newspaper story.
- Monthly report at the start of each month, against the month before.
- Pilot of the Month: your best hired pilot (with an optional bonus) or, in co-op, the co-op's vote. Staff can give awards with a reason.
- The other airlines now fly their own daily schedules from their own home bases, with delays carried through the day. Tap one in the league to see its schedule.

**Co-op: the new Crew room**
- Shared cockpit flights: captain and first officer, both logged, pay split 60/40, first officer hours count towards the next rank.
- Check rides are signed off by a trainer (instructor) with comments.
- Pilot of the Month vote, photo contest and base transfer requests.
- Flight reports can be held for a flight score below a limit you set.
