**Airline Manager Pro 0.9.76: Messages, Phone and the Store on your phone**

- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-Setup-0.9.76.exe**: the installer for Windows (recommended). It installs over 0.9.75 and keeps your airline.
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.9.76.zip**: the PC app to unzip anywhere.
- **AirlineManagerPro-0.9.76.apk**: the phone app. It installs over 0.9.75 and keeps your airline.

**Your phone**
- A dock at the bottom of the home screen, in the middle: Messages, Phone and the Store.
- **Messages**: write to your family and to your airline's people. They start typing and answer after a moment; when you are somewhere else on the phone, their answer comes as a notification, and Messages shows how many are unread.
- **Phone**: favourites, recent calls, your contacts (family first, then your staff, with a message and a call button each) and a keypad. Calling rings, then tells you calls aren't available yet: they come in a later update.
- **Store**: what's new, the apps coming soon and the apps on your phone, to open.
