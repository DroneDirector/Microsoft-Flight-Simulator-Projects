# 0.9.152

## Security officers
- Hire your own security officers (Crew → Security): a dark jacket and tie, paid like aviation security officers.
  Each airport shows how well it is covered by how many you have there and how good they are.
- Gate checks: a very drunk passenger is spotted at the gate, and you can Send Security: an officer collects them
  from the lounge and they don't board. A good officer does it quietly; a weak one may cause a scene the other
  guests notice. In the lounge you see the officer come in, talk to the guest and lead them out.
- Aircraft parked overnight are guarded; without officers (or with weak ones) someone now and then gets through the
  fence and takes equipment.
- A drunk passenger you let fly is less likely to make trouble on board when Security has briefed the crew.
- Reports now and then from each airport with officers: what they checked and found, and sometimes an officer's
  own conduct (a conduct report for your office).
- An officer stands guard by the staff door in the concourse.
- Training for them in the Training room: the initial course, x-ray image reading, conflict management and
  restraint, and the yearly security awareness course.
- Settings → Staff → Security officers pauses all of it; no one hired and nothing recorded is lost.
