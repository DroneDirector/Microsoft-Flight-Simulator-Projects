**Airline Manager Pro 0.9.16: airports in 3D**

- **AirlineManagerPro-0.9.16.apk**, the phone app. It installs over 0.9.15 and keeps your airline.
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.9.16.zip**, the PC app with the Windows app inside.

- Tap an airport on the map, then View in 3D: the 3D view opens at that airport with the free camera, whether or not any aircraft are there. The arrows at the top still take you to an aircraft to follow.
