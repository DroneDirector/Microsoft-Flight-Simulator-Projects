# 0.9.146

## The Training room (the Academy)
- A new room off the staff corridor, opposite the Operations room: Training room 2.22. Its double doors open inwards
  and it is always open to walk into, with or without a class.
- 12 student desks and chairs, the teacher's desk, a whiteboard, bookshelves full of aviation books, a poster, a
  clock that keeps the time, plants and windows. On every desk your airline's Academy notebook (your mark, your name
  and Academy); pens, pencils, calculators and paper differ from desk to desk.
- Everyone on a course sits there: your own people, with their own names and looks, up to 12 at a time. They sway a
  little and follow the teacher.
- The teacher, Daniel Brandt, talks all through the lesson about what the class is learning (pilots, cabin crew,
  mechanics or ground staff). He looks at the class, down at his book, walks to the window, sits behind his desk
  for three to four minutes, gets up and reads from his book. Click him to see what he says; you hear him while you
  are in the room (Voice on or off in his box).
- Every object in the room has its own model file name (training-props/), to put your own models in.

## Crew → Training
- A new Training tab: courses for pilots, cabin crew, mechanics and ground staff, each in its own section, priced as
  training centres charge for them. Choose who goes; they don't work until the course ends, it goes on their record
  and makes them a little better at their job.
- Go to the Training room from there, or from the corridor's Training button.

## The staff corridor
- The guard rail and the strip along the floor stop at every doorway and its frame instead of running across them.
