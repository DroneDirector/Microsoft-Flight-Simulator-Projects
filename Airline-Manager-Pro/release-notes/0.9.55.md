**Airline Manager Pro 0.9.55: airports are back in the 3D view**

- **AirlineManagerPro-0.9.55.apk**, the phone app. It installs over 0.9.54 and keeps your airline.
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.9.55.zip**, the PC app with the Windows app inside.

**3D view**
- Airports show fully again: runways, taxiways, aprons, the ground markings, the lights (approach lights too) and the buildings. Since the new building facades, an airport could disappear when its full layout arrived a moment after the view opened, for example at Vágar.
- A building with an odd outline is left out instead of stopping the rest of the airport.
