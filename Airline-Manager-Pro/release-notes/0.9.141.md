**Airline Manager Pro 0.9.141: frame generation, always on**

- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-Setup-0.9.141.exe**: the installer for Windows (recommended). It installs over your version and keeps your airline.
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.9.141.zip**: the PC app to unzip anywhere.
- **AirlineManagerPro-0.9.141.apk**: the phone app. It installs over your version and keeps your airline.

**Frame generation (Settings → Graphics)**
- Now always on: after each frame your device draws, one or two more are made from it and how far each point lies, shifted to where you are looking now. The 3D view (the cabin too), your office, Operations, the corridor and concourse, the lounges, the hangars and the courtroom become smoother: a device drawing 20 frames a second shows about 45, one drawing 30 shows about 55. When the device keeps up by itself, nothing is added.
- The other ways of keeping it smooth by drawing less sharply (automatic performance, the cabin's sharpness guard) are now off unless you switch them on.

**Performance monitor** (Settings → Graphics, on for now)
- A narrow panel of text in every 3D place: frames a second (real and made), the processor's and the graphics chip's time per frame, the processor's cores (their speed on Android, how busy each one is on the PC). Drag it anywhere; it stays there.
- Its Frame gen button switches frame generation on and off on the spot, so you see the difference at once. Settings follows it, and the other way round.
