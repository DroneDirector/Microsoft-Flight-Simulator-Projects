**Airline Manager Pro 0.9.7: fixes**

- **AirlineManagerPro-0.9.7.apk**, the phone app. It installs over 0.9.6 and keeps your airline.
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.9.7.zip**, the PC app with the Windows app inside.

- Voices: long messages no longer stop part way on slower phones and tablets ("the voice took too long"), and a slow sentence is no longer reported as an error.
- Flight trace: hired pilots' landings are recognised properly, so they are no longer reported as "no touchdown". A landing is only flagged when the app was watching at the moment of touchdown.
