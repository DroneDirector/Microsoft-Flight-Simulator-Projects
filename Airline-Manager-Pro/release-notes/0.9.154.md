## Security incidents
- When you send Security for a drunk or unruly guest in your lounge, two of your officers come in and talk to them.
  The lead officer speaks first and they take turns, with the game's own voices. The words also show on screen.
- Most guests give in. The defiant ones argue back and sometimes cut in. The warnings get firmer, and at the 4th
  the taser comes out. At the 7th the officer shouts.
- Still refusing: the officer tases them. They fall and lie shaking. The officers run over, cuff their hands behind
  their back, lift them up and walk them out.
- Your rules (Crew → Security): tasers are allowed unless you switch them off. With tasers off, the officers call
  the police after the 3rd warning. Two city police officers come in and take over.
- Officers who keep to your rules rate a little higher. Using the taser too early, or against your rules, brings a
  conduct report.
- Officers' uniforms: jackets, shirts with epaulettes, tactical vests or jumpers; peaked caps, baseball caps or
  berets. Your airline's name and SECURITY GUARD are printed on the chest and back.
- Hiring: at least 12 security officers are looking for work from the start, and new ones come every 2 to 4 days.
  When few are looking, they ask for more pay. You can also borrow officers from other airlines for 30 days, at 2.5
  to 5 times the pay.
- The taser and the handcuffs can be replaced (`security-props/taser.glb`, `handcuffs.glb`).

## Mod Manager
- New on the start screen: **Mods**. Replace any model, picture or sound in the game by its slot ID. The game's own
  files are never changed.
- Install a .zip on the phone, or put packages in Documents\Airline Manager Pro\Mods on the PC. Every package is
  checked: only models, pictures and sounds (no code), within each slot's size and detail limits.
- Installed and Browse tabs, with sorting, filters and search by name, author or slot ID.
- Each mod's details page: description, screenshots, version, changelog, size, author, date and what it needs. Every
  slot it replaces has a 3D preview beside the game's own.
- Conflicts (choose which mod wins), drag the load order, profiles, updates, favourites and ratings. Also undo, all
  mods off, a health check and storage use.
- In co-op: see which mods you and your partners share.
- The mods in Browse are demo content for now.
- The modding guide is in the project and on the website.
