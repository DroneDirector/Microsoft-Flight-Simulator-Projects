**Airline Manager Pro 0.9.78: into the cabin, and hands that hold your phone**

- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-Setup-0.9.78.exe**: the installer for Windows (recommended). It installs over earlier versions and keeps your airline.
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.9.78.zip**: the PC app to unzip anywhere.
- **AirlineManagerPro-0.9.78.apk**: the phone app. It installs over earlier versions and keeps your airline.

**The cabin in the 3D view**
- Going into the cabin (Cabin, Walk or a window view) now shows a Loading cabin screen while the cabin and its passengers are got ready, like the one when the 3D view opens.
- On phones, tablets and PCs with a touch screen, the Cabin view has the same walking stick as your office, Operations and the court: push it and you get up and walk the aisle, and drag to look around.

**Your hand on the phone**
- Hands now turn at the wrist, and the fingers and the thumb bend at their joints.
- Picking up the phone, your hand comes down over it from above and takes it. As you lift it, your hand turns until the phone lies in your palm, with your fingers round its left edge and your thumb resting on the glass. Your fingers stay outside the phone instead of going through it.

**Game Booster**
- The memory graph shows how much memory is in use out of the total (for example 5.3 of 7.5 GB), with the share in use and what is free.
