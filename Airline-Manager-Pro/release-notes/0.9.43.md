**Airline Manager Pro 0.9.43: real airline names and colours on other airlines' aircraft (optional)**

- **AirlineManagerPro-0.9.43.apk**, the phone app. It installs over 0.9.42 and keeps your airline.
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.9.43.zip**, the PC app with the Windows app inside.

- New setting, Settings → Damage and realism → Real airline names on other aircraft (off by default). With it on, other airlines' aircraft on the map and in the 3D view fly as real airlines from their real home airports, with their flight codes, registrations in their country's style, the aircraft types they fly, and their colours in broad strokes (no names or logos painted on).
- The airline league, takeovers, fare wars and the newspaper always use made-up airlines.
