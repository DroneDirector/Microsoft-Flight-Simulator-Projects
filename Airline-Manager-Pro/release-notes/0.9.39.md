**Airline Manager Pro 0.9.39: a forward view from the aircraft, the battery in the 3D view, flaps and spoilers as on the real aircraft**

- **AirlineManagerPro-0.9.39.apk**, the phone app. It installs over 0.9.38 and keeps your airline.
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.9.39.zip**, the PC app with the Windows app inside.

- 3D view: a new **Forward** camera looks out of the front of the aircraft you follow, turning, climbing and banking with it. Drag to look around a little.
- 3D view: the battery level now shows right after the camera buttons.
- Aircraft bank the right way: turning right lowers the right wing, turning left the left wing.
- Flaps come down on both wings together (only the left one moved before).
- Spoilers rise on the runway after touchdown and fold away as the aircraft slows to taxi speed; they stay flat in flight.
- Fixed: "Cannot read properties of undefined" from the map when an aircraft at an airport had a taxi route of a single point. One airport's problem no longer interrupts the map.
