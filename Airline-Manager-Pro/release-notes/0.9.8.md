**Airline Manager Pro 0.9.8: courts, news, engines and ground crew**

- **AirlineManagerPro-0.9.8.apk**, the phone app. It installs over 0.9.7 and keeps your airline.
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.9.8.zip**, the PC app with the Windows app inside.

**Courts**
- Your choices in every case: a lawyer, the airline's stance, the employee's status meanwhile, a guilty plea, settling with victims, acting on the investigators' recommendations, and an appeal. The court decides; your choices weigh in.
- Court systems by country: a jury, a judge with lay judges, or a panel of judges.
- Accidents come with an investigation report and recommendations before the charges. The airline itself can be in the dock, and your record counts.
- Civil claims from victims beside the criminal case.
- The full sentence at the end: prison, suspended, time already served in custody, likely release, fine, licence, community service. Released people may ask for their job back.
- Whistleblowers: staff report safety problems in confidence. Fix it, ignore it, or fire them, at your peril.
- New case types follow real prosecutions in aviation, with made-up people.

**In the news**
- Newspapers report charges and verdicts, with the person and the airline named, and write a story when you open a new route. The paper spins in like in the old films.

**Ground crew**
- Your hub and every base need your own ground crew. Without them nobody can unload, load, fuel or push back, and aircraft can't leave; short-handed turnarounds take longer. Elsewhere the local handling company does it. Numbers follow real ramp staffing. Existing airlines get three days to hire.

**Engines**
- Choose the engines when you buy or lease new: CFM or IAE on the A320, LEAP or the geared turbofan on the A320neo, GEnx or Trent 1000 on the 787, GE or Pratt & Whitney on the A310, and a turbine conversion for the Beaver. Engines change fuel, maintenance, reliability and price. Used aircraft come with theirs.

**Other**
- A new airline or company needs a home base, chosen from the airport list.
- 3D view: aircraft no longer jiggle; height, climb, heading and bank are smooth, and the camera stays with the aircraft.
- Flight card: "121 of 189 seats · 64% full" instead of "121 / 189".
