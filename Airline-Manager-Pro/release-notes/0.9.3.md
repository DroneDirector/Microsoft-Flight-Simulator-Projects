**Airline Manager Pro 0.9.3: sharper 3D scenery and satellite view**

- **AirlineManagerPro-0.9.3.apk**, the phone app. It installs over 0.9.2 and keeps your airline.
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.9.3.zip**, the PC app with the Windows app inside.

**3D scenery**
- Much sharper mountains and valleys: the ground around the aircraft now uses heights about 30 m apart, with coarser rings further out.
- Real coastlines, fjords, lakes and rivers, drawn exactly from the OpenStreetMap water outlines instead of being guessed from the heights. The sea lies flat at sea level.
- **Satellite** button in the 3D view (and in Settings → 3D view): the real ground from space, Sentinel-2 cloudless imagery by EOX, draped over the mountains.
- The earth's curvature is included: far mountains drop below the horizon as they really do.

**View distance**
- Settings → 3D view: a slider from 20 km to 800 km.
- High settings show far more of the world, with a note that very high settings might affect performance; low settings note that they might help slower phones and PCs.

**Opening the 3D view**
- A loading screen like the start-up screen, livelier, stays until the mountains, the sea and the airport have loaded.
- From the small map on the overview, the 3D view grows smoothly out of that window to the whole screen.

All settings you choose are kept, also through updates.
