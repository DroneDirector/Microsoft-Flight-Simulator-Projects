**Airline Manager Pro 0.4.0: 3D crew**

- **AirlineManagerPro-0.4.0.apk**, the phone app. It installs over 0.3.1 and keeps your airline (or use Settings → Updates and errors → Check for updates).
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.4.0.zip**, the PC app, unchanged apart from the version number.

**New**
- Every crew member is now a 3D figure: your captain, co-pilots, hired pilots, flight attendants and mechanics. All look options carry over: face shape, eyes, nose, lips, skin tone, hairstyle and colour, facial hair, glasses, garments, shoes, hats and uniform colour. Everyone is always fully dressed.
- The bigger figures breathe, sway, blink and look around. Drag one sideways to turn it around.
- Pilot's cap: captains get gold braid on the visor, co-pilots a plain one. Choose it under Outfit → Hat for your pilot, or on the co-pilot's "Male or female" screen.
- Phones that can't show 3D keep the drawn avatars.
