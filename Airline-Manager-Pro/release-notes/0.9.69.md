**Airline Manager Pro 0.9.69: smoother on phones and tablets, frame generation, and a few office touches**

- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-Setup-0.9.69.exe**: the installer for Windows (recommended). It installs over 0.9.68 and keeps your airline.
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.9.69.zip**: the PC app to unzip anywhere.
- **AirlineManagerPro-0.9.69.apk**: the phone app. It installs over 0.9.68 and keeps your airline.

**Smoother on phones and tablets**
- Automatic performance (Settings > Graphics > Smooth on this device): while the frame rate drops, the 3D view, your offices and the courtroom are drawn a little less sharply, and sharp again when there is room. When the device gets warm or battery saver is on, they draw up to 30 frames a second, which keeps it cooler and saves battery. On by default on phones and tablets; you can switch it on for the PC too.
- Frame generation (same place, off to begin with): while a view draws 30 frames a second, the frames in between are made from the last one, shifted to where you are looking now, so looking around, orbiting and walking are as smooth as your screen.

**Your office**
- The conduct reports window (and the conversation window, and the one in court) can be dragged by its grip or heading. It stays where you leave it; double-tap the grip to put it back.
- The Report to the police buttons flash red and blue.
