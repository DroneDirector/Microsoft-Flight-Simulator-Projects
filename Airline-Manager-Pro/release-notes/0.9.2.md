**Airline Manager Pro 0.9.2: when each message arrived**

- **AirlineManagerPro-0.9.2.apk**, the phone app. It installs over 0.9.1 and keeps your airline.
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.9.2.zip**, the PC app with the Windows app inside.

**Notifications**
- Every notification shows how old it is, as on your phone: "Now", "5 min ago", "2 h ago", "Yesterday 14:05", "Mon 09:12", "28 Oct".
- The times keep up while the list is open.
- An opened message shows exactly when it arrived: "Received Today 14:05 · 5 min ago".
