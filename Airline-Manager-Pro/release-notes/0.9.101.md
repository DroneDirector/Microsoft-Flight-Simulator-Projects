**Airline Manager Pro 0.9.101: autosave, gifts and badges in 3D, and forcing a departure**

- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-Setup-0.9.101.exe**: the installer for Windows (recommended). It installs over 0.9.100 and keeps your airline.
- **AirlineManagerPro-PC-0.9.101.zip**: the PC app to unzip anywhere.
- **AirlineManagerPro-0.9.101.apk**: the phone app. It installs over 0.9.100 and keeps your airline.

**Saving**
- **Settings → Backup and restore → Autosave**: Off (the default), or every 5, 10, 15, 25 or 30 minutes. The game is saved in its own Autosave slot, which you can load from the start screen like any other save.
- When you quit, or go back to the start screen, you're asked first. If you say yes, you can save the game before you go: in the slot you last used, in a new slot, or in one you pick. You can also leave without saving.

**Gifts: Ranking → Gifts → Aviation Gifts**
- The first time your airline lands at an airport, the airline and the airport exchange gifts and commemorative plaques. Your airline gives a model of the aircraft and an engraved plaque. The airport gives something made locally, such as a Faroese jumper, a lava stone carving or a glass vase, plus its own plaque.
- Every gift is a 3D model. Tap one to open it and turn it round freely.
- A gift arrives with the same animation as a badge, but only the first time you receive that particular gift. It also arrives as a notification: tap it to see the gifts again.

**Badges in 3D**
- Every badge you've earned is now a medal in its tier's metal, on a ribbon. The front has the badge's symbol; the back has its name and the date you earned it. Tap a badge in Ranking to turn it round.

**Turning a gift or badge round**
- Mouse or finger: drag to turn it, scroll or pinch to zoom, double-tap to reset.
- Keyboard: the arrow keys or W A S D turn it, + and − zoom, Space spins it, R resets the view, Esc closes.
- Game controller (PlayStation, Xbox, Switch Pro or any standard controller): the popup shows a drawing of your controller with its own buttons, and they light up as you press them. The left stick turns the object, the triggers zoom, the bottom face button spins it, the top one resets the view and the right one closes.

**When badges and gifts are shown**
- If you're working in the Schedule, Dispatch, the Flight planner, Crew, Finance, Routes or Fleet, the animation waits until you're back in the Overview.
- Badges and gifts that arrived while you were away play a few seconds after you reach the Overview. If you tap something before then, the animation plays first and you tap again for what you wanted.

**Force departure at your home hub**
- A delayed flight at your home hub now has a **Force departure** button in the Schedule. It works once the cause of the delay has been fixed: a pilot or cabin crew member assigned, enough ground crew, or the technical fault repaired.
- Delays that can't be overridden say so: weather, a night curfew, a strike or a court order.
