RUNWAY REQUIREMENTS AND PASSENGER CAPACITY
Reference guide | Prepared 30 October 2026
Companion to the speed and weight guides in this folder

SCOPE AND SAFETY NOTE
For every aircraft in Airline Manager Pro: the runway it needs for take-off at maximum take-off weight
and for landing at maximum landing weight, the narrowest runway it is cleared for, whether it can use
grass or gravel, and how many passengers it carries. Broad, approximate figures for the app's
simulation, not operational data. Never use this guide to operate an aircraft.

HOW TO READ THE LIST
- Take-off / landing = field length in metres at MTOW / MLW, sea level, standard day, dry, no wind.
- Width = narrowest runway in metres the type is normally operated from.
- Rough = can use grass or gravel strips.
- Seats: typical layout (classes), all-economy, certified maximum (exit limit).
- How the app scales them: take-off length with the square of the weight, landing length with the
  weight, plus about 7% (take-off) and 5% (landing) per 1,000 ft of airport elevation. When a full load
  doesn't fit, the flight is payload limited: the app works out the most passengers that fit both runways.
- Source: manufacturers' airport-planning documents (Airbus "Aircraft Characteristics - Airport and
  Maintenance Planning", Boeing "Airplane Characteristics for Airport Planning", Bombardier/ATR airport
  planning manuals) and the pilot's operating handbooks for the light aircraft; read from the published
  charts at the stated weights, rounded.
