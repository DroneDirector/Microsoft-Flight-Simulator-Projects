ADDITIONAL AIRCRAFT: TAKEOFF AND LANDING SPEEDS, WEIGHTS AND PASSENGER LOADS
Reference guide | Prepared 29 October 2026
Companion to takeoff-speeds-and-weights.txt and landing-speeds-and-weights.txt

SCOPE AND SAFETY NOTE
This guide covers the aircraft in Airline Manager Pro that the two companion guides do not list:
the Boeing 737-600 and six light / utility aircraft. Same rules as the companion guides: these are
broad, approximate educational figures for the app's simulation, not operational speeds. Never use
this guide to operate an aircraft.

HOW TO READ THE LIST
- Takeoff speed = rotation / lift-off speed in knots (indicated airspeed).
- Approach speed = final approach speed in landing configuration, in knots.
- MTOW / MLW = maximum takeoff / landing weight.
- Source column: P = published by the manufacturer or in the aircraft's handbook (POH/AFM),
  R = published in a review or report by a pilot organisation,
  D = derived (worked out) because nothing usable was published: rotation about 1.2 x and approach
      about 1.3 x the published landing-configuration stall speed (Vso), the usual rule of thumb.
- 1 knot = 1.852 km/h; 1 mph = 0.869 kt; 1 lb = 0.4536 kg.

Notes
- Takeoff and approach speeds rise with weight in roughly the square root of the weight ratio
  (same rule as the companion guides, section 9).
- The light aircraft land on short or grass strips and on water (Beaver, Twin Otter, Caravan and
  Kodiak on floats): the same speeds apply; ground rolls are much shorter than airliners'.
- Passenger payload illustration: 100 kg per passenger including baggage, as in the companion guides.
